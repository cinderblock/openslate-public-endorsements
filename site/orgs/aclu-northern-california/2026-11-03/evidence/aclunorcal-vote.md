## Coordinator re-fetch excerpt (2026-10-19 ~22:20Z, curl GET of the same URL)

The text above did not include the Prop 39 content. These lines are verbatim from the live page's accordion item and "Fast Facts" card:

```text
NO on PROP 39 — Prohibits citizens from voting unless they present government-issued ID
The ACLUs of California say NO on Prop 39.
Prop 39 Fast Facts
California's elections are secure. Prop 39, the voter ID initiative on the November 2026 ballot, would require voters to show government-issued ID every time they vote — creating privacy risks, barriers for eligible voters, ballot rejections, and targeting communities that already face obstacles to voting. All this to solve a problem that doesn't exist.
Vote NO on Prop 39.
```
