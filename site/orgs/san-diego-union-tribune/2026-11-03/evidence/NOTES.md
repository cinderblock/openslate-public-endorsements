## Judgment calls / review flags

- **Statements are the list lines.** The page puts a hyperlink in the
  middle of many lines (e.g. "San Diego City Council [link] District 2:
  Richard Bailey"). The quotes drop the link marker. The evidence file
  keeps it.
- **Oceanside Measure R.** The U-T describes it as "requiring city
  contractors to use project labor agreements". The existing title (from
  the SD bundles) is "Oceanside Charter — Prevailing Wage & Fair
  Competition Measure". The letter matches, so the existing title was
  reused. Worth a check by whoever is harmonizing SD titles (R2).
- **Name normalization.** "Herb W. Morgan" → `Herb Morgan` and "Shirley N.
  Weber" → `Shirley Weber`, to match existing choices.
- **Top-two.** SD City Council D2, D4, D6 and D8, Supervisor D4 and D5,
  Treasurer, SD 40, CD 48 and CD 49 all had June primaries. The picks
  match finalists already recorded in the SD bundles (and the SOS list for
  SD 40 and CD 48/49). National City Mayor (Marcus Bush) is a
  November-only race. Chula Vista Mayor (McCann) is a McCann-vs-Tamayo
  runoff.
