  Party preference)": explicitly not an endorsement. Omitted.
- "Area : 4 Tom Lando": Thomas Lando is the only qualified candidate for
  Chico Unified TA 4, and the ROV list appears to mark that contest "On
  Ballot: No" (an uncontested seat, likely appointed in lieu of election).
  Omitted as not a ballot entry. Re-add it if the contest is on the ballot.
- "Area 1: No endorsement": Chico Unified TA 1 is "On Ballot: No" in the ROV
  list, so no `abstain` entry was created.
- "Assembly 3: Uncontested (No Democrat running)": no pick.
