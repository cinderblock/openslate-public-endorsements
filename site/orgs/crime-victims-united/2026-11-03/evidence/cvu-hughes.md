Crime Victims United Endorses Charles Hughes for California State Assembly
 May 10
Written By Mallory Adams
Crime Victims United is proud to endorse Charles Hughes for the California State Assembly representing District 34. Hughes brings a lifetime of public service and leadership that reflects a deep commitment to protecting communities, supporting law enforcement, and strengthening victims’ rights throughout California.
A U.S. Navy veteran, retired law enforcement lieutenant, and current member of the Antelope Valley Union High School District Board, Hughes has dedicated his career to public safety and community service. His extensive background in law enforcement has given him firsthand experience with the devastating impact crime can have on victims and families, as well as the importance of accountability within the justice system.
Throughout his career, Hughes has demonstrated a strong commitment to safer neighborhoods, effective public safety policies, and support for those working on the front lines to protect California communities. His approach closely aligns with the mission of Crime Victims United to advocate for victims, strengthen public safety, and ensure offenders are held accountable for their actions.
Crime Victims United believes Charles Hughes will bring a strong voice for victims and public safety to Sacramento and is proud to support his campaign for the California State Assembly.
 Mallory Adams
