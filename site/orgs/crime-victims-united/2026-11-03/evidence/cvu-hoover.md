Crime Victims United Endorses Assemblyman Josh Hoover for Re-Election
 May 10
Written By Mallory Adams
Crime Victims United is proud to endorse Assemblyman Josh Hoover for re-election to the California State Assembly representing District 7. During his time in office, Assemblyman Hoover has demonstrated a strong commitment to public safety, victims’ rights, and policies that prioritize safer communities across California.
At a time when many Californians are increasingly concerned about crime, community safety, and accountability within the justice system, Assemblyman Hoover has remained a reliable advocate for protecting families and supporting law enforcement. His leadership reflects a belief that victims deserve to be heard, communities deserve to feel safe, and criminal justice policies must maintain balance and accountability.
In addition to supporting policies focused on strengthening public safety, Assemblyman Hoover has consistently emphasized the importance of safer neighborhoods and practical solutions that put California families first. His approach aligns closely with the mission of Crime Victims United to support victims of crime, promote meaningful accountability, and reduce victimization throughout the state.
His continued dedication to protecting communities and standing up for victims has earned the endorsement and support of Crime Victims United for the 2026 election.
 Mallory Adams
