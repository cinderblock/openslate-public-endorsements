## Judgment calls

- **City College term.** CADC's "City College Trustee — Jeremy Lee, Elijah
  Ball" doesn't say which term. Elijah Ball is a full-term candidate on
  every other slate, and other Nov bundles also put Jeremy Lee under the
  full-term "San Francisco Community College Board". Both are recorded
  there.
- **Name normalization.** "Joaquín Torres" becomes "Joaquin Torres",
  "Philip Kim" becomes "Phil Kim", and "Dion-Jay Brookter" becomes "DJ
  Brookter" (as used in harvey-milk-club). Each statement quotes the
  original listing.
- **Close committee votes.** CADC prints its committee tallies on Prop 43
  (4 NO, 3 YES), Prop 44 (3 NO, 2 YES, 2 ABSTAIN) and Prop F (4 NO, 2 YES,
  4 ABSTAIN). They are recorded as the stated NO positions.
- **Aggregator differences.** sfendorsements.com omits CADC's City College
  pick and its RTM position, but matches everything else.
- The SF measure and RTM titles follow the alice-b-toklas-club bundle (see
  its NOTES).
