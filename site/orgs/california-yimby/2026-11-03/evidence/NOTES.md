# NOTES — California YIMBY, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://cayimby.org/endorsements/ (HTML, scraped with curl). Sections
  "Statewide Office Endorsements", "State Legislature Endorsements" ("We
  have endorsed the following candidates for the November 2026 election.")
  and "Ballot Measures" ("Our thoughts on this year's statewide
  propositions.").

## Extracted: 77 positions

  Insurance Commissioner Ben Allen. No Superintendent or BOE picks.
- 14 State Senate, 50 Assembly (bipartisan, e.g. AD 1 Hadwick, AD 8
  Tangipa, AD 12 Eric Lucan, SD 12 Magsig).
- 6 propositions: "YES on 1", "YES on 2", "NO on 40", "NO on 43"; "NO
  RECOMMENDATION on 37" and "NO RECOMMENDATION on 45" → `abstain`. Other
  props are not mentioned.

## Top-two check

All candidates were checked against the SOS Certified List of Candidates
for Nov 3 2026 (8/27/2026). All are on the general ballot in the listed
district (accent-only differences: "Anamarie Ávila Farías", "Mark
González", "David Peñaloza" vs. unaccented ballot spellings).
