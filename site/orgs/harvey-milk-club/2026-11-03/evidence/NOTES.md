# NOTES: Harvey Milk LGBTQ Democratic Club, November 3, 2026 general

Source: https://www.milkclub.org/endorsements ("Milk Club Endorsements for
November 2026"), fetched 2026-10-15T01:07:05Z.

## Extracted: 53 positions

- 43 endorse positions for candidates and measures, as listed.
- 10 `abstain` positions, one for each explicit "No Recommendation" or "NO
  POSITION": Governor, Lt. Governor, Treasurer, AD-19, BoS D2, BoS D6,
  Community College Board special election (partial term), SF Prop G, and
  state Props 37 and 44. The quoted wording is in `statement`. These are
  explicit declinations on the page, not omissions.

## Ballot verification

- Statewide and district endorsees (Chan, Weber, Cohen, Bonta, Kim,
  Barrera, Lieber, Haney) are certified finalists on the SOS list dated
  8/27/2026,
  https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf.
- SF endorsees are qualified on the SF Dept. of Elections list
  (https://www.sf.gov/candidates-november-3-2026-general-election).
- Three June picks did not advance: Governor (Tony Thurmond), Lt. Governor
  (Michael Tubbs) and Superintendent (Nichelle Henderson). For Governor and
  Lt. Governor the club now says "No Recommendation". For Superintendent it
  endorses Richard Barrera. June's BoS D4 pick (Natalie Gee) is not on the
  Nov D4 ballot, and the club now endorses Jeremy Julian Greco.

  D10 ("Dionjay (DJ) Brookter #1, J.R. Eppler #2") are each recorded as two
  `endorse` positions: weight 1 for #1 and 0.5 for #2, with the ranking
  quoted. Both are endorsements, so `endorse` is used rather than
  `lean_for` for the #2.
- "Community College Board (Special Election)" is the contest the Dept. of
  Elections calls "Community College Board Partial Term". It maps to the
  title "San Francisco Community College Board, Partial Term".
- Name normalization for cross-org collation: "Manohar Raju" becomes "Mano
  Raju" (other SF bundles use that form). "Jeremy Julian Greco" becomes
  "Jeremy Greco". "Dionjay (DJ) Brookter" becomes "DJ Brookter". "Michael
  Nguyen" is kept; the ballot name is Michael T. Nguyen.
- The page's own labels for the state props (e.g. "Prop 43 – Local Special
  Taxes", "Prop 44 – Clinic Spending") are consistent with the SOS titles.
