Lateefah Simon

 12th District, california 
Lateefah Simon represents California's 12th Congressional District, covering much of the East Bay area. Born and raised in San Fransisco, Simon became an outreach worker at the Young Women’s Freedom Center, and at age 19 she was named the center’s executive director. Simon led the "Back on Track" initiative, which was a highly effective reentry initiative for young adults charged with low-level offenses. During this project, Simon worked with then-district attorney Kamala Harris.
In 2016, Simon was elected to the Bay Area Rapid Transit Board of Directors. Being born legally blind, Simon relied on public transit all her life, ingraining in her a deep appreciation for the necessity of public transportation, and in 2024, she was elected to Congress.
Rep. Simon believes in a strong US-Israel relationship built on shared values of democracy. She believes that "The U.S. must adamantly maintain its bipartisan commitment to stability and peace in the region." Simon firmly opposes "incitement, any unilateral annexation of West Bank territory, and acts that entrench the occupation like settlement expansion."
