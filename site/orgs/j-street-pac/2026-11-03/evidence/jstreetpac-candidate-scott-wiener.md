Scott Wiener is a California State Senator running for California’s 11th Congressional District.
First elected in 2016, he currently chairs the Senate Elections Committee and has previously led the Senate Budget, Housing, and Human Services Committees.

Wiener received a bachelor’s degree from Duke University and a law degree from Harvard Law School.
