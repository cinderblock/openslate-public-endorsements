# NOTES — J Street PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

J Street PAC is a national organization. This bundle holds only its
California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://jstreetpac.org/candidates/: a roster of 229 endorsed candidates
  that gives a name and state only. It has 35 California cards.
- One candidate page per California card
  (https://jstreetpac.org/candidate/<slug>/). Each shows an "Endorsed"
  badge and the district ("49th District, california", or for Pan and
  Wahab a sentence naming "California's 6th/14th Congressional District").
  These are the statements.

## Extracted: 31 positions (all US House)

31, 33, 37, 39, 41, 42, 45, 47, 48, 49, 50, 51.

## Skipped (not on the Nov 3 2026 ballot)

- Adam Schiff ("Senate, california"). No California US Senate seat is up
  in 2026.
- Nancy Pelosi (CD 11) and Julia Brownley (CD 26). Both are retiring; the
  November finalists are Wiener vs. Chan (CD 11) and Irwin vs. Gallucci
  (CD 26). J Street's roster still lists them.
- "Von Wilpert Victory Fund", a duplicate fundraising card for Marni von
  Wilpert (CD 48).

## Review flags

- **Stance.** The roster carries no cycle or date. It still lists
  officeholders who are not running, so it is a standing roster rather
  than a dated 2026 slate. Each candidate recorded here is on the November
  ballot for the district J Street names, so `endorse` is used.
- **Sara Jacobs.** Her page reads "51th District" (sic).
- **Names normalized:** "Sydney Kamlager" -> "Sydney Kamlager-Dove";
  "Dr. Aisha Wahab" / "Dr. Richard Pan" / "Dr. Raul Ruiz" without the
  title; "Linda Sánchez" -> "Linda Sanchez" (existing bundles).
- Every choice matches a November finalist in existing bundles.
