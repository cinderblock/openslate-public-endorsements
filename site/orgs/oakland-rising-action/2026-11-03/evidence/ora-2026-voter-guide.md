[] [] Barbara Lee is Moving Oakland Forward!
As Mayor of Oakland, Lee has implemented multiple campaign promises that have moved Oakland forward.

School Board District 2
Jennifer Brouhard
[] [] About Jennifer
As School Board President, Jennifer has led the board to work together to advance the structural changes OUSD needs for long-term success.
A dedicated educator and historian, Jennifer began her career as a special education and history teacher to students in grades 4–8. Jennifer is a lifelong union activist, serving three terms on the OEA Executive Board, one term as OEA Secretary, and more than 20 years as a site representative.
In 2022, she ran and was elected to the OUSD Board as a former teacher and advocate for students and district staff. Her colleagues made her Board President in her first term.
In just one term, under Jennifer’s leadership:
- OUSD has made tremendous strides in stabilizing the district’s finances.
- Staffed every classroom in every Oakland school 99%
- Not one public school closed.
- With four decades of experience as an Oakland educator, parent, grandparent, union leader, and school board member, Jennifer’s deep understanding of OUSD and its leadership is critical to moving our schools toward a healthier, stronger future.

WHO WE ARE
Oakland Rising Action is a community-led political organization focused on supporting leaders who drive a progressive agenda dedicated to racial, economic and environmental justice that puts Oakland’s working-class, immigrant and communities of color living in the flatlands of Oakland at the center of our city’s government.
Our thorough endorsement process included a written questionnaire and panel interviews with select candidates. It engages activists, organizers and leaders of Oakland’s progressive left at each stage, with the goal of endorsing candidates who most align with our values, vision, and strategy for Oakland.
Above are our endorsements for Oakland races that voters will decide on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
To ensure that we are building a thoughtful, progressive voting block that is accountable to our values, we offer some analysis as to how we landed our endorsements.
