# NOTES: League of Women Voters of California, November 3, 2026 general

## What changed in this revision

The earlier version of this bundle (captured 2026-06-01, before propositions
had numbers) held one position, titled "California Fair Elections Act". It
has been replaced with LWVC's full November recommendations on all 14
statewide propositions, and the Fair Elections Act is now titled per the
cross-org convention: "California — Proposition 4 (Repeals Prohibition
Against Public Funding of Election Campaigns)". The older evidence files
(`lwvc-ballot-initiatives.md`, `ca-fair-elections-act-factsheet.md`) are
kept as history; positions now cite only the ballot recommendations page.

## Source

- https://lwvc.org/ballot-recommendations/ ("November 3, 2026 General
  Election"), retrieved 2026-10-15T01:27Z in Chrome (accordion panes
  extracted from the DOM), cross-checked with WebFetch at ~01:05Z.

- Yes: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
- No: 37, 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45.
- Neutral: 40 ("the League takes a neutral position on Prop 40").
- Each position quotes a short sentence from LWVC's write-up in `statement`.

## Judgment calls / flags for review

- **Props 37, 41, 42**: the "LWVC RECOMMENDS:" line has no "No on Prop N"
  campaign link, unlike the other No positions. The position is conveyed
  only by an icon image: the file name is `OPPOSE.svg` for these three (as
  for 38, 39, 43, 44, 45), `Support.svg` for 1–5, `Neutral.svg` for 40. Every
  icon's alt text reads "Support position (meter)", which is wrong and was
  ignored. The write-ups are clearly critical ("Prop 37 does not meet that
  test"; Prop 41 "would put revenue from new state taxes under the Gann
  Limit's rigid constitutional spending cap"; Prop 42 "would lock broad new
  restrictions on state taxing power into the California Constitution").
  Recorded as `endorse` / "No". Because these three positions rest on an
  image, `attribution.mode` is `transcribed`.
- The PDF flyers ("Vote with the League", Quick Reference Guide, Answer Key)
  that would confirm 37/41/42 in text are behind a name/email sign-up form
  at https://lwvc.org/vote-with-league/. Not submitted.
- LWV does not endorse candidates; no candidate races.
