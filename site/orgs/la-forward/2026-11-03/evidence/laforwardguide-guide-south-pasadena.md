## south-pasadena / City Council

### District 4: Nevin Stanton-Trehan [Recommended]

Nevin Stanton-Trehan is challenging incumbent Councilmember Michael Caciotti who’s aligned with police unions and hesitated to support mobility improvements. Nevin Stanton-Trehan is a South Pasadena Educational Foundation Board Trustee who advocates for community-based public safety and a more walkable and bikeable South Pasadena.

His platform also includes “investing in reliable city services and upgrading our aging systems to support long-term community growth, including supporting local and attracting new businesses” and “modernizing our green spaces, Library, and Senior Center, and recreational facilities to serve children, families, and seniors alike.

He has extensive endorsements from the South Pasadena community. For his day job, he works as General Counsel for the YMCA of Metropolitan Los Angeles.
