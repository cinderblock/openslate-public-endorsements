## santa-monica / City Council (At-Large)

### Caroline Torosis [Endorsed]

Three seats on the seven-member Santa Monica City Council are open for election this fall. Incumbents Caroline Torosis and Lana Negrete are both running for re-election, while Jesse Zwick is stepping down after one term on the council.

LA Forward is proud to endorse Caroline Torosis for re-election and to recommend two other candidates, Brett Morrow and Danny Ivanov.

These three candidates are being backed as a progressive slate by a few of the key political groups in Santa Monica, including Santa Monicans for Renters’ Rights (SMRR) and UNITE HERE Local 11. Torosis and Morrow are also backed by Santa Monica Forward (which despite the name is not connected to LA Forward).

The competing slate is led by incumbent Negrete and includes Doug Trussler and Ashley Oelsen, and backed by police and firefighters unions. There are many other candidates, but we think the real battle is between these two slates and that the progressive slate members are the ones worth voting for.

Torosis began her career as a public interest attorney representing workers who had been denied wages they were owed, arguing their cases in front of the Labor Commissioner and the Unemployment Insurance Appeals Board. From there sheI moved into building the systems that could protect workers at scale, helping stand up LA County's Wage Enforcement Program for its new minimum wage ordinance and co- authoring state legislation giving local agencies subpoena power to enforce it, then went on to lead economic development and affordable housing work for the County. In 2016, she was elected to the Santa Monica Rent Control Board, later serving as Chair, working hard to protect tenants. In my day job, she serves as Policy Director and Senior Deputy to Supervisor Holly J. Mitchell on the LA County Board of Supervisors, focusing on economic justice and human services.

As mayor, she brought on a highly effective city manager and launched the city’s Realignment Plan for economic revitalization.

As a councilmember, she’s supported new housing, protected bike infrastructure, and proactive community safety approaches.
