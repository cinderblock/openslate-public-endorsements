## city-of-maywood / City Council (At-Large)

### Mayra Alejandra Aguiluz [Recommended]

Mayra Aguiluz has been a lifelong resident of Maywood, and she is a product of our local public schools in her city. Mayra graduated from Maywood Academy HS in 2011, she then went on to Cal State LA where she graduated with her Bachelor's degree in Psychology in 2016 and her Master's of Science degree in Rehabilitation Counseling in 2021. Mayra’s career goal is to continue to help the disenfranchised and underrepresented people from her community. Mayra Aguiluz was raised by a single immigrant mother from Guatemala who came to the US for the American dream and always emphasized the importance of obtaining an education. Her mother taught her essential values of being a human such as being empathetic, compassionate, optimistic and most importantly help out those who are in need.

Mayra Aguiluz has dedicated her professional career to working with and assisting others. For 5 years, she worked with youth to encourage and assist them with attaining a higher education and applying for financial aid/scholarships. She currently works with coordinating services for homeless individuals. Through her work, she provides homeless people with shelter, food, clothing, and connect them to the resources they need to get back on their feet. Mayra is truly passionate about serving underrepresented and marginalized individuals. As a recently licensed therapist, Mayra wishes to continue using her skills to help those in need.

In November 2022, Mayra was elected as Mayor Pro Tem for the City of Maywood to continue serving her community.

Mayra is endorsed by the CA Working Families Party and we recommend voting for her.
