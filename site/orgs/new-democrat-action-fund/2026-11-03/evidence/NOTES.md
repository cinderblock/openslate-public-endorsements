# NOTES — New Democrat Coalition Action Fund, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

The NewDem Action Fund is a national organization. This bundle holds only
its California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://newdemactionfund.com/candidates ("Endorsed Candidates": "New
  Dem-endorsed candidates know what it means to serve their neighbors...").
  It lists 49 candidates nationwide; three are in California. The home page
  (https://newdemactionfund.com/) shows the same California names.

## Extracted: 3 positions

- CD 6 Richard Pan, CD 26 Jacqui Irwin, CD 48 Marni von Wilpert.

## Review flags

- Choice names: "Dr. Richard Pan" became "Richard Pan", and "Marni Von
  Wilpert" became "Marni von Wilpert" (the ballot spelling used by existing
  bundles).
- Incumbent New Dem members are on a separate /members page. They are
  caucus members, not listed as endorsements, so they were not recorded.
- Top-two: CD 6 Pan vs. Kevin Kiley; CD 26 Irwin vs. Sam Gallucci; CD 48
  von Wilpert vs. Jim Desmond.
