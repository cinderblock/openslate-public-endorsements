U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. David Valadao for California’s Twenty-Second Congressional District
Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. David Valadao for California’s Twenty-Second Congressional District.
Published
October 09, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. David Valadao for California’s Twenty-Second Congressional District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“As a Central Valley native and longtime dairy farmer, Congressman Valadao understands the unique challenges facing small businesses and families in the Central Valley,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “Congressman Valadao is a leader on critical issues, such as water infrastructure, permitting reform, and workforce, while his pragmatic and bipartisan approach to tackling tough issues has enabled him to get things done in a divided Washington. The country needs leaders like Congressman Valadao, and the U.S. Chamber is proud to endorse his reelection.”
"David Valadao understands the industries that keep our region working, and he knows first-hand how challenging it can be to run and grow a business,” said Janelle Capra, President & CEO of the Greater Bakersfield Chamber. “He’s a longtime partner with our Chamber, and the strong, practical leader we need in Congress. From funding for water supplies, to addressing homelessness, and helping small businesses compete in the global market, we can count on David to fight for the resources and solutions we need in the 22nd District.”
“Kern County Farm Bureau is proud to endorse Congressman David Valadao for reelection,” said Todd Snider, President of the Kern County Farm Bureau. “Just this year, he helped secure $540 million for California water infrastructure and advance the 2026 Farm Bill with key provisions that matter to our members. As a dairy producer himself, David understands firsthand the challenges facing our industry and the importance of having a strong voice in Washington. We trust him to continue fighting and delivering real results for the 22nd District.”
