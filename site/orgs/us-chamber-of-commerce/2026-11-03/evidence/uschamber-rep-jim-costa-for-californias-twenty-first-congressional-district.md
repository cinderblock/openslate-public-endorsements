U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. Jim Costa for California’s Twenty-First Congressional District
Published
August 19, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. - Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Jim Costa for California’s Twenty-First Congressional District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman Jim Costa has been a strong supporter of free enterprise and long-time champion of small businesses in the Central Valley,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “At a time of bitter partisan acrimony, Congressman Costa stands out as a bipartisan leader who’s willing to work across the aisle on critical issues like water security, permitting reform, and public safety.  We appreciate the congressman’s dedication to delivering results for Central Valley businesses and families and are proud to support his reelection to California’s 21st congressional district.”
“It is an honor to once again receive the endorsement of the US Chamber of Commerce. American businesses are essential for creating jobs, spurring economic growth, and supporting our communities,” said Congressman Jim Costa. “We must continue working together to ensure we make investments to address our affordability crisis. That includes work to improve access to health care, and water infrastructure to provide additional water supply. All of which is essential for our economy and for the local businesses in my district to thrive.”
“The Fresno Chamber of Commerce endorses Jim Costa because he delivers for the Central Valley. He has secured federal investments in water infrastructure and brought resources home for local projects, consistently prioritizing the needs of farmers, businesses, workers, and communities,” said Scott Miller, President and CEO of the Fresno Chamber of Commerce. “Costa remains a strong advocate for agriculture, reliable water supplies, infrastructure, and economic growth across the Valley, including securing more than $131 million in federal funding for key water projects. He has also demonstrated the ability to work across party lines to achieve results for the Central Valley. The Fresno Chamber is proud to support Jim Costa and his continued service to the region."
“For decades, Representative Costa has been there for California agriculture. Whether it’s supporting our 400 specialty crops through the Farm Bill, securing critical investments for water storage infrastructure, opening new trade opportunities, helping farmers reduce greenhouse gas emissions, or expanding markets for California dairy products, he has consistently worked to make sure California agriculture has a voice in Washington,” said Shannon Douglass, President of the California Farm Bureau. “California’s farmers and ranchers are grateful for his leadership and his support over the years.”
