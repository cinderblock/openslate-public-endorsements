WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. James Gallagher for California’s First District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman James Gallagher has a strong track record of fighting for Northern California businesses and families,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “As a former county supervisor and state legislator, Congressman Gallagher supported commonsense policies that cut red tape and lowered taxes, while working across the aisle to deliver results for Northern California. Congressman Gallagher has continued that important work in Washington, D.C, and the U.S. Chamber is proud to endorse his reelection to California’s 1st congressional district.”
