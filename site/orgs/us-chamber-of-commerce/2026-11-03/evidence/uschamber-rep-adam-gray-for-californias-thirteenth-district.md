WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Adam Gray for California’s Thirteenth District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
"Adam Gray understands the best way to lower costs, empower small businesses, and create more opportunities for families across California's 13th District is through free enterprise. The U.S. Chamber is proud to endorse him for reelection, and we look forward to working with him to advance pro-growth policies in Congress,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President for the Western Region at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce.
