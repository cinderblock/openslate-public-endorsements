WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Tom McClintock for California's Fifth District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman Tom McClintock has been a consistent advocate of American free enterprise,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “Throughout his career in Congress, Representative McClintock has been a champion for free markets and has supported pro-growth policies that help California businesses invest, hire, and succeed.  The U.S. Chamber appreciates Congressman McClintock’s leadership and is proud to endorse his reelection for California’s 5th congressional district.”
