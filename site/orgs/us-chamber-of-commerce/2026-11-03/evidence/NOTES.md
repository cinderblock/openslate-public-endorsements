# NOTES — U.S. Chamber of Commerce, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

The U.S. Chamber of Commerce is a national organization. It endorses
federal candidates in many states. This bundle holds only its California
picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

The Chamber has no single endorsements list. It announces each endorsement
in its own press release under `https://www.uschamber.com/improving-government/`.
To enumerate them, I read the site's own XML sitemaps
(https://www.uschamber.com/sitemap.xml and the child sitemaps it lists) and
kept every URL containing both "endorse" and "californ". 47 matched. 34 of
them are 2021–2024 releases (old district numbers, earlier cycles) and were
ignored. The 13 releases with 2026 `lastmod` dates were fetched. Each one is
dated between August 19 and October 13, 2026 in its "Published" line, and
each opens "Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement
of ...". One evidence file per release.

I also checked the sitemap for 2026 endorsement URLs without "californ" in
the slug that name California members (Kim, Calvert, Issa, etc.). There
were none.

## Extracted: 13 positions (all US House)

CD 1 Gallagher, CD 5 McClintock, CD 6 Kiley, CD 13 Gray, CD 16 Liccardo,
CD 19 Panetta, CD 20 Fong, CD 21 Costa, CD 22 Valadao, CD 23 Obernolte,
CD 46 Correa, CD 48 Desmond, CD 50 Peters.

Seven are Republicans and six are Democrats (Gray, Liccardo, Panetta, Costa,
Correa, Peters).

## Top-two check

Each choice matches a November finalist already recorded for that district
in other bundles (ca-democratic-party, ca-republican-party, etc.). CD 6 is
Kiley vs. Richard Pan. CD 48 is Desmond vs. von Wilpert. CD 1 is Gallagher
vs. McGuire.

## Review flags

- The CD 1 release calls James Gallagher "Rep.". I didn't check whether
  he currently holds a House seat; it doesn't change the November race.
  The quote is kept verbatim.
- The release titles use ordinal words ("Forty-Sixth Congressional
  District"). These were mapped to the numeric district titles.
- No state-level, legislative or ballot-measure positions. The U.S. Chamber
  endorses federal candidates only. CalChamber is a separate organization
  and not covered here.
