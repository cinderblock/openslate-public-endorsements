# NOTES — Club for Growth PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

Club for Growth PAC is a national PAC. This bundle holds only its
California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://www.clubforgrowth.org/elections/pac-endorsed-candidates/ ("2026
  Club for Growth PAC-Endorsed Candidates"). It lists statewide, U.S. Senate
  and U.S. House endorsements nationwide. The only California entry is "Tom
  McClintock (CA-05)".
- https://www.clubforgrowth.org/club-for-growth-pac-endorses-rep-tom-mcclintock-in-ca-05-re-election-campaign/
  (release dated February 19, 2026): "Club for Growth PAC is proud to
  endorse Rep. McClintock for reelection".

## Extracted: 1 position

- US House CD 5: Tom McClintock.

## Top-two check

- CD 5: McClintock vs. Michael Masuda in November (matches existing
  bundles and the NRA-PVF California general-election list).
