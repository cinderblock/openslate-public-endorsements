## Why a new bundle

The sweep flagged this page under santa-cruz-yimby. It's YIMBY Action's own California list, not a chapter's. Of the
112 picks only sf-yimby's 24 and santa-cruz-yimby's Capitola pick were in the catalog; the rest (Alameda, Marin, LA,
San Mateo and Santa Clara locals, the Legislature, Congress) had no home. California YIMBY (cayimby.org) is a
separate organization with its own list, so nothing here goes in that bundle. The page doesn't say which chapter made
each pick. The chapter bundles are unchanged, and santa-cruz-yimby's republished statewide picks can adopt from here.
