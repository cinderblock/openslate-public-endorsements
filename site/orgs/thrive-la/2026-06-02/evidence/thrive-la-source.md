## Notes on joint endorsements

Thrive LA issued "joint" endorsements for several races, meaning they
listed two candidates either of whom they consider acceptable. Modeled
here as two separate `endorse` positions on the same subject, with a
`statement` line cross-referencing the other name. Affected races:

- LA Mayor: Spencer Pratt / Karen Bass
- LA City Attorney: John McKinney / Hydee Feldstein Soto
- LA City Council District 13: Rich Sarian / Dylan Kendall
