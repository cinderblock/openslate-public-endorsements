## Review flags

- **File name and cover say "Primary".** The URL has
  "Comprehensive-Primary_Voter-Guide" and the cover says "November 3 Primary
  Election Day". The candidate pages are headed "2026 GENERAL ELECTION
  ENDORSED CANDIDATES", the cover says "on or before November 3rd", and the
  PDF was created 2026-09-30. Read as the general-election guide (the
  "Primary" wording looks like a template leftover).
- **Bipartisan slate.** Includes Republicans (Niello, Patterson, Hoover,
  Flora) and Democrats; Metro PAC picks Guerra over Cofer for Supervisor D1
  and Matsui in the all-Democrat CD 7 race.
- **Measure titles not from registrars.** Rocklin C (Placer County), Yolo
  County X and Woodland Y use short descriptions from the guide's summaries.
  The Placer and Yolo election sites were not reachable or were not checked.
  Retitle from the official lists. Los Rios H reuses the Sacramento title.
- `us/ca/yolo-county`, `us/ca/woodland`, `us/ca/west-sacramento`,
  `us/ca/citrus-heights` and `us/ca/rocklin` jurisdictions are new/shared with
  the other Sacramento-region bundles.
- Ballot names: "Sergio D. Robles" (as on SacDems). Others as printed in
  title case.
