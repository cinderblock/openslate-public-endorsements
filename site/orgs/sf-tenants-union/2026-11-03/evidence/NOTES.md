## Flags for review

- **Paid placement (asterisks).** The page is a slate-mailer-style ad. It
  states it was "PREPARED BY SAN FRANCISCO TENANTS UNION SMO" and that
  "Appearance is paid for and authorized by each candidate and ballot
  measure which is designated by an *." Asterisked items: Connie Chan,
  Jeremy Greco, Gary McCoy, Prop C, Prop D and Prop I. Their `statement`
  quotes the notice so consumers can see it. They are still recorded as
  `endorse`, because SFTU lists them as its endorsements on its own
  endorsements page. A reviewer may want to decide whether paid
  appearances deserve different treatment. Prop D's asterisk sits on a
  "HELL NO", so the paid appearance there is presumably the No campaign's.
- **State prop labels vs numbers.** SFTU's short labels for 43 and 44 do
  not match the SOS titles. SFTU has "Prop 43 Regulate Health Clinic
  Spending" and "Prop 44 Clinic Funding". The SOS has 43 = Limits Voters'
  Ability to Raise Revenues for Local Government Services and 44 = Requires
  Community Health Clinics Spend 90% Of Revenue on Program Services. SFTU
  says NO on both, so the recorded positions (No on 43, No on 44) are the
  same under either reading. The positions are keyed by number. SFTU's
  label for 41, "Requires Limits on Audits on New State Special Taxes", is
  loose but points at the right measure.
- "HELL YES" (C, I) and "HELL NO" (D) are quoted in `statement`. Plain
  YES/NO items have no statement.
- Name normalization for cross-org collation: "Dion-Jay “DJ” Brookter" is
  recorded as "DJ Brookter"; the ballot name is Dionjay (DJ) Brookter.
  "Jeremy Greco" is kept; the ballot name is Jeremy Julian Greco.
