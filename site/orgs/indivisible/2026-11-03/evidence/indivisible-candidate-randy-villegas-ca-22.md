A son of the Central Valley, Randy has dedicated his life to giving back to the community that raised him and fighting for his neighbors.
Randy is the child of hard working Mexican immigrants. Despite that hard work, his family still struggled, needing the support of Medicaid and food stamps to make ends meet. It was these formative experiences that pushed Randy to become an educator and community organizer, and empower his hard working neighbors to make change. 
Randy’s close community ties have given him a deep, personal understanding of the impact of bad policy, the costs of not fighting back, and what’s on the chopping block in Trump’s Washington, and is running for Congress to fight for working families like his.
