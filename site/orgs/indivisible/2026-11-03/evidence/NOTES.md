# NOTES — Indivisible, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

Indivisible is a national organization. This bundle holds only its national
California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://indivisible.org/resources/candidates/: the "Endorsed Candidates"
  directory (15 results nationwide; filterable by state). Two California
  entries: Dr. Aisha Wahab (US House) and Randy Villegas (US House).
- https://indivisible.org/candidates/randy-villegas-ca-22/ and
  https://indivisible.org/candidates/aisha-wahab-ca-14/: each says
  "Endorsed by Indivisible".
- https://indivisible.org/news/indivisible-endorses-aisha-wahab-in-californias-14th-congressional-district-special-election/
  (Jul 30, 2026): the press release, which also lists "Randy Villegas in the
  CA-22 Congressional race" among earlier endorsements.

## Extracted: 2 positions

- CD 22 Randy Villegas (finalist vs. David Valadao).
- CD 14 Aisha Wahab (November finalist per existing bundles).

## Review flags

- **Wahab and the CA-14 special.** The press release is headed as an
  endorsement "in California’s 14th Congressional District Special
  Election", and its text says "in the Democratic primary". That special
  election (Aug 18 special general) is a separate contest from the Nov 3
  regular election. The candidate page is not limited to the special: it
  shows her under "CA-14" as "Endorsed by Indivisible" and talks about GOTV
  "from the primary through the general election". So she is recorded for
  the November regular CD 14 race. Drop it if the reviewer reads the
  endorsement as special-election-only.
- Local Indivisible groups' own endorsement slates were not searched.
