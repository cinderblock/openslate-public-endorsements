# NOTES — International Association of Fire Fighters (IAFF), Nov 3 2026 general (California picks only)

## Sources — republisher, not the IAFF's own page

- **The IAFF does not publish its California list.** iaff.org/firepac/,
  iaff.org/political-action/endorsements/ (endorsement philosophy and the
  federal candidate questionnaire only) and the Oct. 15, 2026 article
  "What fire fighters need to know before Election Day" (which says to
  "check with your Local or state associations for information about
  IAFF-endorsed candidates") have no candidate list. The iaff.org WordPress
  search API (/wp-json/wp/v2/search) has no 2026 California endorsement
  posts. iaff.org/political-action/endorsement2026/ is a 404.
- The list comes from https://cpfelectionguide.org/, the Digital Election
  Guide of California Professional Firefighters (CPF), the IAFF's state
  affiliate. Its federal section is headed "IAFF Federal Office
  Recommendations" and the guide says "NOTE: Federal recommendations
  provided by IAFF." Every position cites that page.
- Corroboration for CD 49: the Mike Levin campaign post "International
  Association of Fire Fighters endorses Mike Levin for Congress" (March 26,
  2026), quoting IAFF General Secretary-Treasurer Frank Lima:
  https://www.mikelevin.org/2026/03/26/iaff-endorses-mike-levin-for-congress/

## Extracted: 51 positions

- 46 `endorse` and 5 `abstain` ("No Recommendation": CD 5, 14, 22, 40, 48).
- CD 23 says "Pending". Omitted.

## Stance

The section's only word for its picks is "Recommendations", so per the
round-2b rule they are `endorse`. (The CPF bundle recorded the same 51 as
`lean_for` under the earlier rule.)
