Aysha Pamukcu is running for City Council to champion an affordable, sustainable South San Francisco — for this generation and the next. As an experienced policy director and public interest attorney, she currently chairs SSF's Planning Commission and serves on the San Mateo County Commission on the Status of Women. Shaped by her immigrant parents' journey and experience as a mom of little kids, she works to ensure all families and neighbors can put down roots in strong, vibrant communities.
