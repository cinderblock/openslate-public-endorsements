# NOTES — Sacramento County Democratic Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.sacdems.org/endorsements/ (HTML, scraped). Heading "November 3,
  2026 General Election". One list covering federal, state, county, city,
  school board, special district, state propositions and local measures.
- Measure letters and official titles were checked against the Sacramento
  County Voter Registration & Elections "Current Measure Information" page
  (https://elections.saccounty.gov/us/en/election-information/current-measure-information.html):
  D Natomas USD, E Center JUSD, G Galt JUHSD, H Los Rios CCD SFID No. 1,
  I/J/K City of Folsom, L City of Galt, M Sacramento City USD (F, City of
  Sacramento, was withdrawn).
- Candidate ballot names for state and federal contests were checked against
  the SOS Certified List of Candidates for the Nov 3 2026 general (8/27/2026).

## Extracted: 71 positions

- Federal: CD 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 (5).
- Statewide: all 8 constitutional offices plus BOE District 1 (9).
- Legislature: SD 6, SD 8; AD 6, 7, 9, 10, 11 (7).
- County: Board of Supervisors D1 (Flo Cofer).
- City: Elk Grove D4; Folsom D1, D3, D5; Rancho Cordova D5 (5).
- School boards: 17 positions, incl. 3 `neutral` (Los Rios Areas 2 and 6,
  Natomas USD Area 1, each marked "NEUTRAL*" on the page) and 2 picks for the
  Robla ESD at-large race.
- Special districts: 5.
- State propositions: all 14.
- Local measures: 9 (8 Yes, Measure L Galt `neutral`).

## Skipped

- "Center Joint Unified School District, Area 4 – NO ENDORSEMENT**": the
  footnote says the party "declined to endorse any of the candidates". That is
  not a stated neutral position, so it is omitted.
- The "Who are the Democrats on my ballot for offices not listed here?" list.
  The page says "Being listed here does not constitute an endorsement nor a
  voting recommendation."
- Judicial retention: the page says the party "does not typically make formal
  endorsement recommendations" and only lists the justices. Nothing extracted.

## Review flags

- **Top-two.** Federal and legislative picks match the SOS certified general
  list. Supervisor D1: Cofer and Eric Guerra advanced from the June primary
  (Abridged / CapRadio coverage). City, school and special-district races are
  November-only contests.
- **New titles and jurisdictions.** No existing bundle covered Sacramento
  County. Local races follow the existing "<District> Board, Area N" style;
  district races use `us/ca/sacramento-county`, city races use the city slug
  (`us/ca/elk-grove`, `us/ca/folsom`, `us/ca/rancho-cordova`, `us/ca/galt`).
  Dry Creek Joint ESD and Sierra Joint CCD are Placer County-based districts
  (`us/ca/placer-county`), San Joaquin Delta CCD uses
  `us/ca/san-joaquin-county`, El Dorado Irrigation District uses
  `us/ca/el-dorado-county`.
- **Measure short titles.** Measures D and G use the short name printed in
  the ballot question. E, H, I, J, K, L and M have no short name in the
  ballot question, so the parenthetical is a brief description of the
  ballot question. Rename if the county publishes official short titles.
- Local candidate names are as printed by the party (not checked against the
  county candidate list); nicknames in quotes ("Jag", "Moe") are kept.
