# NOTES — Swing Left, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

Swing Left is a national organization. This bundle holds only its
California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://swingleft.org/house ("Flip the U.S. House"). Races are grouped as
  Must-Flip, Must-Hold and Reach. Each card names the candidates and has
  Donate/Volunteer buttons for the Democrat. California cards: Must-Flip
  CA-06, CA-22, CA-48; Must-Hold CA-13, CA-45. No California races are in
  Reach.

## Extracted: 5 positions, all `lean_for`

  but not a formal endorsement, so the stance is `lean_for`. Upgrade to
  `endorse` if the reviewer reads it as an endorsement.
- Choice "Dr. Richard Pan" became "Richard Pan".
- https://swingleft.org/states (state legislatures) was not used:
