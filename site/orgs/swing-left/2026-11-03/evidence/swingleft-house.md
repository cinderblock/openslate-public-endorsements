After 24 years in Congress, Rep. Mike Turner has never faced a serious challenge in this Dayton-based district. Air Force veteran and oncology nurse practitioner Kristina Knickerbocker presents a clear contrast and can appeal to independents and moderate Republicans, having served at Wright-Patterson Air Force base, one of the largest employers and economic engines in the district. Trump won by 7.8% in 2024 and a more modest 5.8% in 2020, but with the favorable environment and a strong Democratic top-of-ticket, Knickerbocker is within reach of an upset.

Republican Rep. Tony Gonzales resigned after an ethics probe, handing Democrats a rare opening in this sprawling border district that stretches from San Antonio westward along the U.S.-Mexico border to El Paso. Democrat Katy Padilla Stout won her primary outright and is already on the ground making the case that this district deserves better representation than her controversial opponent, who has a long history of offensive remarks and videos online, making this one of the most unexpected pickup opportunities of the cycle.

VA-05
D - Challenger: Tom Perriello
R - Incumbent: John McGuire
Former Rep. Tom Perriello pulled off one of the biggest upsets of the cycle in 2008, when he defeated a six-term Republican incumbent by 727 votes. He is hoping to repeat this upset in 2026, running against unpopular first-term Rep. John McGuire. While Trump won by 12.2% in 2024, his margin was more narrow in 2020, at 8.2%. Perriello has outraised McGuire and is putting together a strong operation that can turn out voters in every corner of the district.
