What’s the Difference?

In California, the Secretary of State indentifies both as Measures. On the actual ballot they are separated once a measure is qualified. Propositions and measures both put issues directly before voters. Propositions appear on the statewide California ballot, while local measures apply only to voters within a specific city, county, school district, or other local jurisdiction.
