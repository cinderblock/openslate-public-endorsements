# NOTES — Kern County Democratic Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.kerndemocrats.com/, home page, under the heading "2026 Voter
  Guide". The guide is one image ("OFFICIAL DEMOCRATIC VOTER GUIDE / VOTE ON
  OR BEFORE NOV. 3, 2026"). It was downloaded at full size and transcribed by
  hand, so `mode` is `transcribed`. See `kerndemocrats-voter-guide.md`.
- Other site pages (Resources, About, Events, News) have no endorsement
  lists.

## Extracted: 50 positions

- Statewide: 8 offices + BOE Districts 1–4 (12).
- Federal: CD 20, CD 22 (2).
- Legislature: SD 16, AD 35 (2).
- Local: 20 (Bakersfield council Wards 1, 3, 7; Kern CCD Areas 2, 3;
  Bakersfield City SD Areas 2, 4; Greenfield Union SD Areas B, D, E; Kern

## Review flags

- **Dual pick.** "Kern County Board of Education Area 3 Anastasia Hopper Or
  Regina Velasquez": both recorded as `endorse`.
- **BOE Districts 2–4.** The guide lists all four BOE districts, though
  Kern voters only vote in one. All four are kept as published.
- **Local names and seats were not checked** against the Kern County
  Elections certified list (kernvote.com could not be reached from search
  results; time-boxed). Names are as printed. Area numbers/letters are taken
  from the graphic ("District 2", "B", etc.) and titled "<District> Board,
  Area N". Arvin, Delano and Shafter council seats are at-large in the
  graphic, so they have no district in the title.
- **Top-two.** All state and federal picks are on the SOS certified general
  list. Bakersfield Ward 1 (Arias vs. Mercy Peña) is a November contest per
  local filing coverage. Kern County Board of Education seats could be
  primary-only contests in some years. Check that Areas 3 and 7 are on the
  Nov ballot.
- **New jurisdictions:** `us/ca/kern-county`, `us/ca/bakersfield`,
  `us/ca/arvin`, `us/ca/delano`, `us/ca/shafter`.
- Choice spellings for state and federal races use the SOS certified list
  ("Shirley N. Weber", "Malia M. Cohen", "Sally J. Lieber"). The graphic's
  "Dr. Shirley Weber" is kept in the statement.
