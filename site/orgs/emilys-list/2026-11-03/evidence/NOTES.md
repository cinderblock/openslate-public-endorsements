# NOTES — EMILYs List, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

EMILYs List is a national organization. It endorses Democratic pro-choice
women in many states. This bundle holds only its California picks for the
November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://emilyslist.org/donate/all-candidates/ ("Federal and Gubernatorial
  Candidates"). The candidate grid is rendered from JSON embedded in the
  page. The site's own search API with the California filter
  (https://emilyslist.org/wp-json/emea/v1/search?state=california&type=all&per_page=100)
  returns one candidate: Marni von Wilpert, U.S. House, 2026, "Majority
  Maker". Of the 64 federal/gubernatorial candidates on the page, she is the
  only Californian.
- https://emilyslist.org/candidate/marni-von-wilpert/: names CA-48 and says
  "EMILYs List is proud to stand with her".
- https://emilyslist.org/state-and-local-candidates/: "Here are the
  candidates who are running strong campaigns at the state and local level
  in 2025 and 2026 and have earned the EMILYs List endorsement." The
  California section lists three names.
- https://emilyslist.org/candidates/ is a historical "Women We've Helped
  Elect" roll (updated April 2026). It lists past winners, not 2026
  endorsements, so it was not used.

## Extracted: 4 positions

- US House: CD 48, Marni von Wilpert.
- Statewide: Secretary of State, Shirley Weber.
- Local: Oakland Mayor, Barbara Lee; Los Angeles Mayor, Karen Bass.

## Top-two check

- CD 48: von Wilpert vs. Jim Desmond in November (matches existing bundles).
- Secretary of State: Weber vs. Don Wagner (existing bundles).
- Los Angeles Mayor: Bass vs. Nithya Raman runoff (existing bundles).
- Oakland Mayor: November election; existing `Oakland Mayor` subject reused.

## Review flags

- Incumbent California congresswomen (e.g. Judy Chu, Nanette Barragán,
  Doris Matsui) appear only on the historical "helped elect" page, not on
  the 2026 candidate list, so they are not recorded as 2026 endorsements.
- The state/local list is a bare list ("Name – Office"), so statements
  quote the list line.
- Choice spelling: the list says "Shirley N. Weber"; the existing bundles
  use the ballot name "Shirley Weber".
