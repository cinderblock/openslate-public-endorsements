# NOTES — Placer County Democratic Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://placerdems.org/endorsements (HTML, Squarespace, scraped).
  "2026 General Election Endorsements": candidate cards by section, then an
  "All Endorsements (Current 10/12/26)" text list with state propositions
  and one county measure.
- The page links to the CADEM "2026 General Election Full Endorsement List"
  but not as the county party's own list. Only picks printed on this page
  were extracted.

## Extracted: 50 positions

- Statewide: 8 offices + BOE District 1 (9).
- Federal: CD 3, 4, 6. Legislature: SD 4, SD 6, AD 1, AD 5.
- Propositions: all 14 ("Support" → Yes, "Oppose" → No).
- Local measure: South Placer Fire District Measure B, Yes.
- Local candidates: 20.

## Review flags

- **Cards vs. "All Endorsements" list.** Four card endorsements are missing
  from the 10/12/26 text list: Rick Bartee (Roseville council D4), Jeff
  Roberts (RJUHSD Area 4), Tricia Caspers (Placer UHSD Area 3) and Derek
  Pell (Roseville City SD Area 4). Bartee, Roberts and Caspers are included,
  since their cards are under the "2026 General Election Endorsements"
  heading. **Derek Pell is omitted**: his card is for the same contest
  (Roseville City SD Trustee Area 4) as Sara Bye, and only Bye is on the text
  list.
- **Measure B title.** The Placer County Elections site returned 403 to curl
  and WebFetch, so the official title could not be fetched. The subject is
  just "South Placer Fire District — Measure B". Add the registrar's short
  title.
- Ballot names: "Dianna Margaret James" (SOS list; the page says "Diana
  James"), "Shirley N. Weber", "Malia M. Cohen". Local names are as printed.
- Titles shared with the Sacramento bundles: "Sierra Joint Community College
  District Board, Area 1" and "Roseville Joint Union High School District
  Board, Area 2" (`us/ca/placer-county`). City jurisdictions: `us/ca/roseville`,
  `us/ca/auburn`, `us/ca/rocklin`.
- Top-two: all state and federal picks are on the SOS certified general list.
