# NOTES — California Medical Association, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.cmadocs.org/voter-guide ("Physician Voter Guide: 2026
  General Election"; HTML, scraped with curl). "As California prepares for
  the November 3 General Election, our Physician Voter Guide provides
  information on CMA-endorsed candidates and ballot measures".

## Extracted: 27 positions

  endorsement."): Governor Becerra, AG Bonta, Lt. Governor Ma, Treasurer
  Kounalakis.
- 4 propositions: "Proposition 3 - VOTE YES"; "VOTE NO" on 39, 40, 44.
- 19 CALPAC-supported candidates ("CALPAC ... supports candidates for state
  and federal office"): US House CD 6 Pan, CD 26 Irwin, CD 48 von Wilpert;
  State Senate 10, 12, 26, 40; Assembly 3, 12, 27, 31, 32, 34, 35, 42, 65,
  66, 67, 68.

## Scope note

CALPAC lists only "state and federal legislative races where an incumbent
is NOT seeking re-election ... For open seat races not listed, CALPAC does
not have an endorsed candidate." Incumbent races are not listed, so
silence there means nothing.

## Top-two check

All candidates are on the SOS Certified List for Nov 3 2026 (8/27/2026) in
the listed district.

## Omitted / review flags

- **Ami Bera (CD 3) and Raul Ruiz (CD 25)** appear under "Physicians

  open-seat list doesn't cover them. Omitted as ambiguous.
- "Andrae Gonzalez" (AD 35) → ballot spelling Andrae Gonzales. ", M.D."
