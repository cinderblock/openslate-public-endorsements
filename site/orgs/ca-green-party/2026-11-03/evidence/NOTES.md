# NOTES: Green Party of California, Nov 3 2026 general

Added 2026-10-30, read 2026-10-30 2:27 PM PDT (21:27Z).

## Extracted
All 14 statewide props, from the party's own voter guide: 11 Yes or No picks, and Props 1, 2 and 5 as `abstain`
("voted to take no position"). The page says positions came from a vote by delegates from active county parties and
need a 2/3 threshold.

## Left out
- **Candidates.** "Greens in Nov. 2026 Elections" says inclusion "does not imply endorsement", which county parties
  decide, so nobody on that list is this slate's pick. Its asterisks mark county endorsements. Four are starred, and
  those county parties have no bundle yet: Mark Rendon (AD 14, Alameda), Daniel Hor (Dublin City Council, Alameda),
  Mathew Lopez (Healdsburg City Council, Sonoma), Alexandra "Xy" VanAntwerp (Carpinteria City Council, Santa Barbara).
- **Left Unity Slate.** The joint slate with the Peace and Freedom Party ran for statewide offices in June. None of
  its seven candidates is on the Secretary of State's November listing, and neither is Butch Ware (Governor).
