## Review flags

- **May slate is primary-era.** "CA HOUSE & SENATE" was posted May 2 and
  says "the primary is often the election". The finalists it names are
  carried forward to November, since the org hasn't withdrawn them.
- **Antioch district for Monica Wilson.** The May slate says "Antioch City
  Council D2"; the Sep post says "Monica Wilson — District 4". The later
  post and the existing subject (District 4, choice "Monica E. Wilson")
  were used.
- **Fontana Mayor ballot name.** The ballot name wasn't verified. "Joz
  Sida" (the name the Run for Something bundle uses) was chosen over
  "Jocelyn Sida".
- **New titles invented:** "Fontana Mayor" (`us/ca/fontana`) and "Diablo
  Water District Board, Ward 2" (`us/ca/contra-costa-county`). The post
  says "Diablo Water District, Ward 2"; check whether the district calls
  its seats wards or divisions.
- Statements for slate entries quote the slate intro plus the list line.
