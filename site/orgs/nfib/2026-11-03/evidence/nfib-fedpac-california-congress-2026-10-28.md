California Small Businesses Endorse Slate of Candidates for Election to Congress

September 29, 2026

NFIB announces support for eight California candidates running for federal office

SACRAMENTO, Calif. (Sept. 29, 2026) – The National Federation of Independent Business (NFIB), the nation’s leading small business advocacy organization, endorsed the following candidates for election to the U.S. House of Representatives to represent California:

Rep. James Gallagher (CA-01)
Rep. Tom McClintock (CA-05)
Rep. Kevin Kiley (CA-06)
Kevin Lincoln (CA-13)
Rep. Vince Fong (CA-20)
Rep. David Valadao (CA-22)
Rep. Jay Obernolte (CA-23)
Jim Desmond (CA-48)

“On behalf of California small businesses, we are proud to support a slate of candidates who have a record of advancing Main Street interests,” said John Kabateck, NFIB California State Director. “State-level policies have continuously burdened Main Street with high costs and an abundance of red tape. California small business owners need effective representation in Washington to ensure that they can compete in the economy and create jobs locally. We are eager to work with these endorsed leaders to safeguard owners’ ability to operate and grow.”

“California is home to a wide range of small businesses, and it is important that owners have their voices heard in Congress,” said Louis Bertolotti, NFIB Political Director. “The candidates we endorsed have a deep understanding of the issues that matter most to Main Street and have pledged to build on last year’s win of making the 20% Small Business Deduction permanent. We look forward to working with them in Washington.”

The endorsement is issued from NFIB FedPAC, NFIB’s political action committee. NFIB FedPAC is funded by voluntary contributions by NFIB members over and above their membership dues. Decisions made by the NFIB FedPAC are managed by a member-driven grassroots evaluation.
