# NOTES — NRA Political Victory Fund, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

NRA-PVF is the NRA's national PAC. This bundle holds only its California
endorsements for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://www.nrapvf.org/grades/california/ ("Grades & Endorsements",
  California, "Election held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026"). It lists every
  general-election candidate for US House, Governor, Lieutenant Governor,
  Attorney General, State Senate and State Assembly with an NRA-PVF letter
  grade. Endorsed candidates carry an extra badge image
  (`alt="NRA Endorsed"`); the page legend says that badge "Indicates an NRA
  Endorsed Candidate."

## Skipped and why

- Letter grades without the badge (A, AQ, ?, F, etc.) are ratings, not
  endorsements, per the task rules. Several A/AQ-rated candidates (e.g.
  CD 2 Robin Littau AQ, CD 3 Robb Tucker AQ, CD 9 John McBride AQ) are not
  endorsed and are not recorded.
- No Governor or Lieutenant Governor candidate carries the badge (Hilton
  and Romero are graded "?").
- No ballot measures appear on the page.

## Top-two check

- The page itself lists only the two November finalists per contest. Every
  endorsed candidate also appears as a finalist in existing bundles (e.g.
  ca-republican-party, ca-democratic-party) for the same district.

## Review flags
