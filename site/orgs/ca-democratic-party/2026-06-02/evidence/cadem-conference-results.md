# Evidence: CADem 2026 endorsing conference results

- Convention results PDF: https://cadem.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-Convention-Endorsing-Conference-Results-FINAL-Results-PDF.pdf
- Endorsements landing page: https://cadem.org/endorsements/
- Press release index: https://cadem.org/for-immediate-release-february-22-2026/
- Address-based lookup tool: https://cadem.org/our-endorsements/
- Retrieved: 2026-06-01

## Methodology

The CADem endorsement decision rule (from the PDF): a candidate placed on
the consent calendar requires ≥60% of the votes cast in an Endorsing
Caucus. "No Endorsement" with ≥60% becomes a formal "No Endorsement".
Otherwise: "No Consensus".

This slate includes ONLY the consent-calendar endorsements from the
convention-stage PDF that this researcher could verify. The full
ratified endorsement list lives at the address-based lookup tool, which
isn't scrape-friendly without a specific address.

## Confirmed consent-calendar picks captured

11 positions: 6 statewide (SoS, Controller, AG, BoE D1/D2/D3), 3
congressional (CD-1/5/7), 1 state senate (SD-24), 1 state assembly
(AD-58).

## No Consensus at convention (NOT endorsed)

- Governor (multi-candidate field, no candidate ≥60%)
- Lieutenant Governor (Fiona Ma led at 49%, below threshold)
- Treasurer (Eleni Kounalakis led at 52%, below threshold)
- Insurance Commissioner (Ben Allen 41.69%, Jane Kim 40.19%, below threshold)
- Superintendent of Public Instruction (5-way split)
- Board of Equalization District 4 (Tom Umberg 53.60%, below threshold)
- Multiple congressional and one state assembly race

## Known unincluded races

The PDF only shows races where there was a contested vote at the
endorsing conference. CADem ALSO automatically endorses incumbents who
file for re-election uncontested — those don't appear in the PDF and
aren't captured in this slate. Augment by fetching the final ratified
consent calendar PDF if/when accessible, or by querying the address
lookup tool district by district.
