# NOTES — Blueprint SF (formerly TogetherSF), Nov 3 2026 general

## Source

Blueprint SF's own "November 2026 Voter Guide",
https://www.sfblueprint.org/advocacy/november-2026-voter-guide (HTML,
scraped). The page describes Blueprint as "Formerly TogetherSF". The
downloadable cheat-sheet PDF linked from the page agrees on every pick.
sfendorsements.com lists this org as "Blueprint SF" and matches the guide.

## Extracted: 17 positions

- Board of Education: Phil Kim, Autumn Brown Garibay, Tim Tung.
- Supervisors: D2 Stephen Sherrill, D4 Alan Wong, D6 Matt Dorsey, D10 Theo
  Ellington.
- SF measures: Yes on A, D, E and F; No on B, H, I and J.
- SF Prop C and Prop G: recorded as stance `neutral` with no choice. The
  guide explicitly says "We are neutral on C" and "We are neutral on G ...
  We're taking no position on Prop G". That is a stated position, not a
  silence, so it is kept as `neutral` instead of being dropped.

Statements are short quotes from the guide's own reasoning. Stray spaces
before periods ("Yes on A .") are an HTML artifact and were tidied.

## Not covered by Blueprint

Statewide offices, state propositions, D8, Assessor-Recorder, Public
Defender, City College, BART and RTM. The guide makes no recommendation on
any of these.
