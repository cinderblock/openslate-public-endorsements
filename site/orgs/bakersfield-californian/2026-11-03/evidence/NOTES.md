# NOTES — The Bakersfield Californian, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

The Californian's editorial board publishes "Our View" pieces. The 16
general-election recommendation editorials used here (Sept 5 to Oct 17,
2026) are listed in `attribution.sources`. They were found through the site
search (https://www.bakersfield.com/search/?q=our+view&...), because
/opinion/our-view/ returns 404.

The articles are subscriber-only. The HTML carries the full body, but the
premium paragraphs are ROT47-obfuscated (TownNews "encrypted-content"). They
were decoded locally. Each evidence file has the free preview first, then the
decoded text under "[decoded subscriber text]". All quoted statements come
from the free preview, the headline, or that decoded text.

Spelling for statewide, congressional and legislative candidates comes from
the SOS certified list. Bakersfield City Council names are as printed in the
editorial; see the flags.
