# NOTES: DSA San Diego, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources
- https://www.dsasandiego.org/november-2026-voter-guide ("DSA San Diego's November 2026 Midterm Voter Guide"), the chapter's own guide. Positions quote its write-ups.
- https://www.dsasandiego.org/endorsements, the chapter's formal endorsements: Kraft, Zuniga, Yes on 3, and Yes on 40. Props 3 and 40 cite this page.
- KPBS lists only Kraft, Zuniga, Prop 3, and Prop 40 for DSA. The chapter's guide is far more complete.

## Stance mapping (reviewer: please confirm)
The guide's "Definitions" section separates **Endorsed** ("require a chapter's whole membership to vote to endorse a candidate and include a commitment ... to devote time and resources") from **Recommended** ("Sometimes we recommend a candidate if the alternatives are much worse. It's best to read the write-up to understand how enthusiastic our recommendation is.").
- Candidates the guide says it endorses (Alexander Kraft, Kenneth Zuniga) are recorded as `endorse`.
- Candidates it "recommends" are recorded as `lean_for`, with the recommending sentence quoted.
- Measures use `endorse` with Yes/No, since the endorsed/recommended distinction is defined for candidates.
- "State Superintendent of Public Instruction: NOT Sonja Shaw (see CA DSA Guide)" is recorded as `oppose` Sonja Shaw.
- The statewide quick-guide picks (Insurance Commissioner and props) are copied from the CA DSA guide, per the page: "We've copied the CA DSA voter guide recommendations here for convenience."
