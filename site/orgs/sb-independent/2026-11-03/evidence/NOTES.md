## Flags for review

- **Summary list vs. body.** The summary list at the top still says "Goleta City Council District 1: TBA / District 2: TBA" and "Santa Barbara County Board of Supervisors 5th District: TBA". The article body has endorsement headings for all three: "### District 1: Luz Reyes-Martín", "### District 2: James Kyriaco", and "### 5th District: Ricardo Valencia", the last with "we are endorsing Ricardo Valencia". The body is treated as authoritative. The summary list looks stale.
- **Goleta.** The write-up praises both incumbents but has no explicit "we endorse" sentence. The inclusion rests on the endorsement-style headings. A reviewer could downgrade these to `lean_for`.
- **Measure H title.** The source says "Measure H (Education and Economic Development Bond for Santa Maria and Alan Hancock College)". The title is `Allan Hancock Joint Community College District — Measure H (Education and Economic Development Bond)`. The official county ballot label was not fetched. On 2026-10-27 the title became `Allan Hancock Joint Community College District — Measure H (Affordable Higher Education Bond)`, after Santa Barbara County's title ("Allan Hancock Joint Community College District Affordable Higher Education"), in every bundle that names it.
- **Jurisdictions.** All local races reuse `us/ca/santa-barbara`, following the June bundle, which used it for both county and city contests. Lompoc, Buellton and Goleta use `us/ca/<city>`.
- **Verification.** Becerra, Bonta, Kounalakis, Allen, Hart (AD 37) and Carbajal (CD 24) are all on the SoS Certified List of Candidates for Nov 3, 2026. The local races are from a November-specific editorial.
- **June picks not carried forward.** The June endorsements for Capps (Supervisor D2), Slattery (Auditor-Controller), Greene (Clerk-Recorder-Assessor) and Steyer (Governor) are not in the November editorial, so none are carried forward. Presumably those races were decided in June, or the candidate did not advance (Steyer is not on the certified Nov ballot). The local cases were not checked further.
