## Review flags — slate mailer

- **This is a slate-mailer PAC** ("Paid for by Affordable Housing Alliance
  Political Action Committee", FPPC# 822268). Its legal notice says:
  "Appearance in this mailer does not necessarily imply endorsement of
  others appearing in this mailer ... Appearance is paid for and
  authorized by each candidate and ballot measure which is designated with
  an *." The page itself calls the list "November 3, 2026 Election
  Endorsements", so the entries are recorded as `endorse`. The asterisk is
  kept in each statement so a reviewer can see which entries are paid
  appearances: Board of Education (all 3), Supervisors (all 4), SF Props
  A, B, C, D, I, J, and state Prop 1.
- CD 11 Connie Chan carries footnote ¹: "Paid for by SF For All
  (www.sfforall.com) and not authorized by any candidate or candidate's
  committee." Kept in the statement.
- "Bunny Mcfadden" → Bunny McFadden; "Dion-Jay (DJ) Brookter" → DJ
  Brookter (the name used in the other SF bundles).
- The page's short measure labels (e.g. "Proposition DNO*" "Restricts
  Voter Initiatives") were matched to the official titles by letter.
- Top-two: state and federal candidates checked against the SOS Certified
  List (8/27/2026). SF races are November-only.
