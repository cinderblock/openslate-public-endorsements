# NOTES — Marin County Republican Central Committee, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://maringop.org/2026/09/15/marin-gop-endorses/ ("Marin GOP Endorses
  …", updated October 13, 2026): "Below are the statewide and local
  candidates endorsed by the Marin GOP, along with its recommendations on
  state propositions and local measures." The actual list is two PNG flyer
  images (Artboard-1, Artboard-2), transcribed by hand in
  `maringop-flyers-transcription.md`. Hence `mode: transcribed`.
- https://maringop.org/elections/endorsements ("Marin GOP Recommendations for
  the 11/3/2026 General Election", updated October 13, 2026), HTML: explains
  the process and has the text "Measure P – OPPOSE!" and "Measure CC –
  OPPOSE!". It says "No position was taken on measures to extend local taxes
  to pay for service."
- Ballot names checked against the Marin County Elections "Local candidates
  ON ballot 11/03/26" page and the SOS certified list.

  Shaw; CD 2 Littau.
- Local (5): Marin Healthcare District Division 1 Clayton Hess ("Dr. Clay
  Hess" on the flyer); Novato council D4 Chris Carpiniello; Richardson Bay
  Sanitary District John Turnacliff; Marin County Board of Education Trustee
  Area 3 Craig Stuart Adams; Novato USD Trustee Area 4 Francis Drouillard.
- State propositions (14): Yes on 39, 41, 42, 43; No on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37,
  38, 40, 44, 45 (No encoded as `endorse` + `choice: "No"`).
- Local measures (2): No on P, No on CC.

## Skipped / not present

- No Assembly District 12 pick (both finalists are Democrats); no picks for
  Board of Equalization or Insurance Commissioner on the flyers.
- The page refers voters to a "Leave No Vote Behind" page with Reform
  California recommendations and a judicial guide; those are third-party
  recommendations, not Marin GOP endorsements, and were not used.
