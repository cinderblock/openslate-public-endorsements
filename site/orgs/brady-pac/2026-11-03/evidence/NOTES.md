# NOTES — Brady Campaign / Brady PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

Brady is a national organization. This bundle holds only its California
picks for the November 3, 2026 general election. The endorsements are
published by "The Brady Campaign" at elections.bradyunited.org ("2026
Elections"). The page meta description reads: "Learn about our endorsed
candidates who are committed to helping free America from gun
violence." The slug `brady-pac` follows the coordinator's assignment.

## Sources

- https://elections.bradyunited.org/candidates?state=california and
  https://elections.bradyunited.org/candidates/p2?state=california: the
  site's own California filter, 2 pages, 24 candidates.
- One candidate page per candidate
  (https://elections.bradyunited.org/candidates/<slug>). Each says "The
  Brady Campaign is proud to endorse ...". Those sentences are the
  statements.
- https://www.bradyunited.org/brady-pac/endorsements is a generic page
  with no candidate list.
