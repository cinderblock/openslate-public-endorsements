## Irvine City Council, District 1

- **Non-Partisan** | **Melinda Liu**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Melinda Liu to keep Irvine on the right track for progress. Melinda Liu has served on the Irvine City Council since 2024 and is a licensed attorney, licensed real estate broker, and certified mediator. She runs her own estate-planning business. On the council, she has improved public safety and walkability, strengthened community engagement, amplified resident voices at City Hall, and advocated for responsible governance. Liu’s top priorities include public safety and infrastructure, economic opportunity and fiscal responsibility, strong neighborhoods and family-friendly policies, and inclusive representation and a transparent government. She is endorsed by LiUNA! Southern California District Council of Laborers, National Union of Healthcare Workers, Orange County Employees Association, Reps. Dave Min and Ted Lieu, Asm. Sharon Quirk-Silva, Orange County Supervisors Katrina Foley and Vicente Sarmiento, and Irvine City Councilmembers William Go and Kathleen Treseder. ## Irvine City Council, District 5

- **Non-Partisan** | **Betty Martinez Franco**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Betty Martinez Franco to keep Irvine on the right track for progress. Betty Martinez Franco was elected to Irvine City Council in 2025 as the first Latina and first Mexican immigrant. She runs her own boutique public relations firm, and serves on several boards, like the Orange County Fire Authority, Great Park, and Irvine Barclay Theater. ## Irvine City Council, District 6

- **Non-Partisan** | **Kathleen Treseder**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Kathleen Treseder to keep Irvine on the right track for progress. Dr. Kathleen Treseder has served as an at-large member of the Irvine City Council since 2022, when she was elected with 23% of the vote in a six-candidate field. In 2024, Irvine voters approved moving to by-district elections, and Treseder is running for the District 6 seat. In her time on the council, she has led environmental and sustainability efforts, including prohibiting gas-powered lawn equipment and driving the city’s Climate Action and Adaptation Plan, advocated for open and transparent government, safeguarded taxpayer dollars at Great Park, and led the effort to reform the city’s lobbyist regulations. Dr. Treseder is a biology professor at the University of California Irvine, where she has led a research program and taught for thirty years. She co-founded OC Clean Power. Treseder’s priorities include climate and clean environment, equality and safety, affordability, expanding Irvine’s infrastructure, and transparency and accountability.
