## Bakersfield City Council, Ward 1

- **Non-Partisan** | **Eric Arias**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Eric Arias to keep Bakersfield on the right track for progress. Arias has served on Bakersfield City Council since 2020, when he was elected with 74% of the vote, and is president and CEO of United Way of Central Eastern California. As councilmember, he has fought for fairer district maps, Southeast Bakersfield investments, park improvements, traffic calming, affordable housing, and balanced budgets that protect core city services. Arias’s top priorities include reducing crime and strengthening public safety, improving quality of life, and fighting for Ward 1’s fair share of investment. He volunteers for Leaders in LIfe and We the People. Arias is endorsed by the Kern County Democratic Central Committee; the Kern, Inyo and Mono Counties Central Labor Council; SEIU Local 521; Planned Parenthood; Asm. Esmeralda Soria; Kern County Supervisor Leticia Perez; Bakersfield City Council Member Manpreet Kaur; and California State Regional Director Noe Garcia. The RaceGeneral election: Two candidates are running in the nonpartisan November 3 general election: Eric Arias (incumbent) and Mercy Peña.
