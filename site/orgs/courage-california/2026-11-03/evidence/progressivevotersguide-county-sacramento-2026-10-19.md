## Sacramento County Board of Supervisors, District 1

- **Non-Partisan** | **Dr. Flojaune Cofer**

  > Elect Dr. Flojaune Cofer for Supervisor to put Sacramento County on the right track for progress. Dr. Flojaune Cofer is an epidemiologist and a senior policy director at Public Health Advocates. She is the four-term chair of Sacramento’s Measure U committee, advising the city on how to spend revenue from a voter-supported sales tax increase, including on solutions to homelessness and programs for youth. Cofer also serves on Sacramento’s Active Transportation Commission, the Mayor’s Commission on Climate Change, and Sacramento County Sheriff’s Outreach Community Advisory Board. She previously worked at the California Department of Public Health. Cofer is endorsed by many groups, including California Working Families Party, Wellstone Progressive Democrats of Sacramento, Planned Parenthood Advocates of Mar Monte, ACCE Action, Sunrise Movement Sacramento, SEIU Local 1021, and National Union of Healthcare Workers. Dr. Cofer previously ran for Sacramento mayor in 2024, losing the general election by just one point. The Race June 2 primary election results: Dr. Flojaune Cofer 46%, Eric Guerra 29%, Deborah Ortiz 15%, and Tim Riley 10%. Dr. Flojaune Cofer and Eric Guerra will contend in the November 3 general election. The Position Each of the 58 counties in California is governed by a five-person board of supervisors. A Board of Supervisors has legislative and executive power to manage county services and resources, including courts, jails, public health, and public lands. They also have quasi-judicial powers, which gives them the right to hold hearings, conduct investigations, and make decisions in a manner similar to judicial courts. Laws passed by Boards of Supervisors are generally called ordinances. Because counties include both incorporated cities, which are administered by their own city councils, and unincorporated areas, which are directly administered by the county, ordinances may or may not apply in different areas of the county.
