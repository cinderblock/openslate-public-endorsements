## San Bernardino County Superintendent of Schools

- **Non-Partisan** | **Alex Vara**

  > Elect Alex Vara for Superintendent to keep San Bernardino County on the right track for progress. Alex Vara has 34 years of experience in education as a former middle school principal, teacher, and aide in Rialto Unified School District. He served on the Redlands Unified School District board, California Interscholastic Federation board, and Colton-Redlands-Yucaipa Regional Occupational Program board. Vara trained future teachers at California State University-San Bernardino. His top priorities are safe classrooms for all, resources to support every child, and career and college pathways starting in middle school. Vara has the endorsement of the Redlands Teachers Association, Redlands Education Support Professionals Association, and California Working Families Party. The RaceJune 2 primary election results: Cali Binks 39%, Alex Vara 31%, Ray Anthony Culberson 15%, and Ken Larson 15%. Cali Binks and Alex Vara will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race. The PositionThe San Bernardino County superintendent of schools oversees the San Bernardino County Office of Education, which provides instruction and services for nearly 400,000 K–12 public school students. ## Rancho Cucamonga City Council, District 2

- **Non-Partisan** | **DeJonaé Shaw**

  > Elect DeJonaé Shaw for City Council to put Rancho Cucamonga on the right track for progress. Progressive endorsements: Shaw is endorsed by National Union of Healthcare Workers, SEIU 121 RN, UAW, UFCW 1167, Community Action Fund of Planned Parenthood Orange and San Bernardino Counties, San Bernardino Democratic Party, Asm. Robert Garcia, Palm Springs Mayor Naomi Soto, Moreno Valley Mayor Ulisses Cabrera, and Riverside County Supervisor Jose Medina. Top issues: Lowering the cost of living, housing working families can afford, economic growth that puts people and community first, expanding small business support, repairing potholes and sidewalks, better health infrastructure and faster emergency response, and youth programs that open doors and build brighter futures Community leadership experience: DeJonaé Shaw is a licensed vocational nurse, is vice chair of the Legislation and Education Committee at her health-care union, and is president of the Inland Empire Labor Council. She is a longtime supporter of youth advocacy and education. Shaw founded the Greater Empire Pageants for young people in the area, and has worked with local organizations like the Young Women’s Empowerment Summit and Just Us 4 Youth. She serves as a leader in her union as the vice chair of Legislation and Education and worked with unions across the country to pass the National Workplace Violence Prevention Health Care and Social Service Workers Act. Shaw is also a board member for the Optimist Youth and Family Home Services and an active member of the Democratic Party Central Committee, the Democratic Club of the Inland Valley, the San Bernardino County Young Democrats, and the San Bernardino County Sheriff’s Next Gen Committee, aimed at increasing community solutions to policing. She volunteered to serve on the Inland Empire Labor Council’s Civil Rights Committee to conduct outreach to ensure that San Bernardino County immigrant communities participated in the 2020 Census. The Race General election: Four candidates are running in the nonpartisan November 3 general election: Kristine Scott, DeJonaé Shaw, David Vangorden, and Connie Velazquez. The Position Incorporated cities in California are generally governed by a five-person city council. A city council is responsible for establishing policy, passing local laws (called ordinances), voting on budget appropriations, and developing an overall vision for the city.
