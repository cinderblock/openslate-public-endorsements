A Board of Supervisors has legislative and executive power to manage county services and resources, including courts, jails, public health, and public lands. ## Stanton City Council, District 1

- **Non-Partisan** | **Donald Torres**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Donald Torres to keep Stanton on the right track for progress. Donald Torres has served on the Stanton City Council since 2022, and is a youth mentor at Western High School’s Independent Learning Center. On the board, he has advocated for imposing mayoral term limits, making livestreamed meetings permanent, and increasing the sheriff’s deputy staffing. Torres created a city immigration resource web page, fought for the release of a Stanton resident from an immigrant detention center, and coordinated chemical spill evacuation with residents. His top priorities include building a safer, stronger Stanton; safe and healthy neighborhoods; addressing homelessness, jobs, and economic opportunity; and clean streets and strong infrastructure. Torres is endorsed by the Democratic Party of Orange County, UNITE HERE Local 11, National Union of Healthcare Workers, Planned Parenthood of Orange San Bernardino Counties, OC Action, and California Working Families Party. He also has the support of Orange County Supervisor Vicente Sarmiento, Asm. Sharon Quirk-Silva, Buena Park Mayor Connor Traut, Stanton Council Member Gary Taylor, Stanton Planning Commissioner Marlon Velasco, and Stanton Public Safety Committee Member Doug Makino. The RaceGeneral election: There are two candidates running in the November 3 general election: Thomas Frazier and Donald Torres (incumbent). This is a nonpartisan race.
