## San Bernardino County Superintendent of Schools

- **Non-Partisan** | **Alex Vara**

  > Elect Alex Vara for Superintendent to keep San Bernardino County on the right track for progress. Alex Vara has 34 years of experience in education as a former middle school principal, teacher, and aide in Rialto Unified School District. He served on the Redlands Unified School District board, California Interscholastic Federation board, and Colton-Redlands-Yucaipa Regional Occupational Program board. Vara trained future teachers at California State University-San Bernardino. His top priorities are safe classrooms for all, resources to support every child, and career and college pathways starting in middle school. Vara has the endorsement of the Redlands Teachers Association, Redlands Education Support Professionals Association, and California Working Families Party. The RaceJune 2 primary election results: Cali Binks 39%, Alex Vara 31%, Ray Anthony Culberson 15%, and Ken Larson 15%. Cali Binks and Alex Vara will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race. The PositionThe San Bernardino County superintendent of schools oversees the San Bernardino County Office of Education, which provides instruction and services for nearly 400,000 K–12 public school students. ## San Bernardino City Council, Ward 2

- **Non-Partisan** | **Christian Thomas Shaughnessy**

  > Elect Christian Thomas Shaughnessy for City Council to keep San Bernardino on the right track for progress. Christian Thomas Shaughnessy is a housing specialist, a homelessness expert, and an educator. He runs a small tutoring and mentoring business, and is chair of the San Bernardino County Youth Coordinated Entry System, staff liaison for the County’s Youth Advisory Board on Homelessness and Substance Abuse, co-chair of the County Youth Homeless Task Force, alternate member of the Central Valley Homeless Provider Network, city commissioner on the General Plan Advisory Committee, and a member of the San Bernardino County Human Rights Coalition and ChICCCAA (Chicano Indigenous Community for Culturally Conscious Advocacy and Action). His top issues are tackling the root causes of homelessness, public safety, addressing blight, fixing our streets, smart economic development, and good-paying jobs. Shaughnessy has the endorsement of Inland Empire Labor Council AFL-CIO, San Bernardino County Democratic Party, IE United, Planned Parenthood CAF of Orange and San Bernardino Counties, San Bernardino School Board Member Mary Ellen Abilez Grande, Riverside City Councilmember Clarissa Cervantes, Moreno Valley School Board President Sammie Luna, and Jurupa Area Recreation and Park District Trustee Edgar Castelon. Shaughnessy previously ran for San Bernardino Community College District Area 4 in 2022 and San Bernardino City Council Ward 3 in 2024. The Race June 2 primary election results: Christian Thomas Shaughnessy 40%, Benito Barrios (33%), and Sandra Ibarra (Incumbent) 28%. Christian Thomas Shaughnessy and Benito Barrios will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race. The Position San Bernardino is governed by a seven-person city council. A city council is responsible for establishing policy, passing local laws (called ordinances), voting on budget appropriations, and developing an overall vision for the city.
