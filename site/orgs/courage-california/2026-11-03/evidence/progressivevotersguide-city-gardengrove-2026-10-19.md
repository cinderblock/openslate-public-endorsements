## Garden Grove City Council, District 4

- **Non-Partisan** | **Hieu Nguyen**

  > Elect Hieu Nguyen for City Council to put Garden Grove on the right track for progress. Hieu Nguyen is a licensed clinical social worker and long-term public servant, most recently serving as director of the County Office of Population Health and Equity. He built the Equity in OC Initiative to increase access to resources across Orange County, and previously co-founded an organization focused on the needs of LGBTQIA+ Vietnamese community. Nguyen’s top issues are strengthening city services, expanding outdoor and public areas, improving mental health services, and addressing the high cost of housing. He has the endorsement of Working Families Party, OC Action, the Democratic Party of Orange County, Chispa, Women for American Values and Ethics, Orange County Labor Federation, and Planned Parenthood. The RaceGeneral election: Hieu Nguyen, Amanda Mohr, Patrick Phat Bui, and incumbent Joe DoVinh will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race.
