# Vote Yes for Transit Funding Measures H and RTM

By SF Bicycle Coalition Board on September 2, 2026

The Board of Directors of the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition endorses in favor of both the Regional Transit Measure (RTM) and local funding measure, Prop H.

Many Bay Area transit agencies including BART, Muni, and CalTrain are facing major, catastrophic service cuts that will imminently go into effect in January 2027 without intervention. For example, if BART doesn’t receive new funding, the agency will have to close 15 stations, shut down two lines, and cut train service by 70%.

The Regional Transit Measure is a measure for the 5 Bay Area counties served by BART that will increase transit funding with a small sales tax increase. Funding from Measure RTM will go to supporting BART, Muni, CalTrain and other Bay Area transit agencies. Proposition H is a San Francisco-only variable parcel tax to cover the remainder of the Muni funding deficit. Funding generated from these two measures will go towards transit operations and improvements to safety, cleanliness, and seamless integration of transit services.

We worked with a coalition of aligned organizations to get these measures on the ballot, and now it’s time to vote for both propositions to ensure that bay area public transit agencies can survive.

We know that a transit friendly city is better for biking. Transit riders and people who bike have shared goals, to find climate friendly ways to get around and to ensure that everyone is able to get to where they need to go safely. Without a well functioning public transit system, the number of private vehicles on roads will increase, creating more hazards for people biking and walking. Let’s vote yes on both transit measures to make sure Bay Area transit agencies can continue providing a valuable service. Vote Yes on Prop H and the Regional Transit Measure (RTM), and get involved in the campaign to volunteer here!
