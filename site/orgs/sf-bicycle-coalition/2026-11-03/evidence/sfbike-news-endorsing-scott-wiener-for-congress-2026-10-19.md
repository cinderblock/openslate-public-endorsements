# Endorsing Scott Wiener for Congress

By SF Bicycle Coalition Board on May 29, 2026

As a 501(c)(4), the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition* makes political endorsements, helping to ensure San Francisco is a better place to live, work, and bike. Our endorsements process is led by our 15-person board of directors that is directly elected by our membership. Decisions regarding endorsements are distinct from all work by our educational and charitable 501(c)(3) arm, the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition Education Fund.

Scott Wiener has once again proven himself to be a strong bicycle advocate. He has shown unwavering support for projects that improve the safety of and access to our streets in California. In his answers to our questionnaire, he highlighted his win passing SB 960 which requires CalTrans to adhere to Complete Streets principles. He is a strong supporter of maintaining Sunset Dunes, remaining firm in his stance to endorse Prop K in 2024. In addition, Weiner co-authored SB 63, which enabled the five-county transit measure to have a chance on the 2026 ballot. In these ways, Scott Wiener has shown strong alignment with our organization’s mission to advocate for biking and rolling that is safe, convenient, and welcoming for everyone in San Francisco.

Below are his words on the federal government’s role in improving active transportation:

“The federal government sets the floor for transportation investment nationally — and right now that floor is too car-centric. I will fight to embed active transportation as a core requirement in surface transportation reauthorization.”

Given his consistency and strength in advocating for safe cycling, we believe that Scott Wiener is ready to bring effective, cogent, and compelling bicycle advocacy to a national stage.
