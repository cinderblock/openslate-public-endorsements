# NOTES — Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.hjta.org/2026votingguide/ ("2026 Voting Guide for California
  Statewide General Election", HTML, scraped with curl; WebFetch was used
  only to locate the page). The page "provides recommendations on statewide
  ballot propositions, local tax measures and HJTA-PAC candidate
  endorsements for the November 3, 2026 General Election." A printable PDF
  version is linked from the page but was not needed; the HTML carries the
  full content.
- The site sits behind Cloudflare; a plain curl with a browser user agent
  got a 200.

## Extracted: 87 positions

- 14 state propositions. "HJTA'S QUICK VOTING GUIDE: YES on 39, 41, 42, 43,
  45; NO on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37, 38, 40, 44". Each has its own "YES/NO on
  Prop. N" heading and a closing "Vote YES/NO on N." line, which is quoted.
- 2 local measures with existing titles: Los Angeles Measure FD (No) and the
  five-county Bay Area regional transit sales tax (No; mapped to the
  existing "Bay Area — Regional Measure RTM (Regional Transit Measure)").
- 6 statewide offices (HJTA-PAC): Governor Hilton, Controller Morgan,
  Secretary of State Wagner, Attorney General Gates, Superintendent Shaw,
  Treasurer Hawks. No endorsement for Lt. Governor or Insurance
  Commissioner.
- 3 Board of Equalization: D1 Grove, D2 Pimentel, D4 Bilodeau.
- 16 US House, 12 State Senate, 29 State Assembly.
- 5 local candidates whose race titles already existed: San Diego County
  Assessor/Recorder/Clerk (Jordan Marks), LACCD Seats 2, 4, 6 (Irwin,
  Alaniz, Arey), South Bay Union School District Trustee Area 1 (Erika
  Lowery).

## Top-two check

Every state, legislative and congressional candidate was checked against
the SOS Certified List of Candidates for the Nov 3 2026 General Election
(https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf,
dated 8/27/2026). All 66 appear on the general ballot in the listed
district. The LACCD, SD Assessor and South Bay USD candidates also appear
in other bundles' general-election lists for the same seats.

## Skipped

- **Local measures without existing titles**: Santa Monica Measure ES
  (parcel tax for schools, No) and Palos Verdes Estates Measure PVE (parcel
  tax, No). Not added because no official title was looked up; a follow-up
  can add them using LA County Registrar titles.
- **Local candidates without existing race titles**: Ted Gaines (El Dorado
  County Supervisor, 4th District), Mari Jane Lopez-Taff (Citrus Heights
  City Council D2), Mark Fantozzi (Cupertino City Council), Dan Hay
  (Fountain Valley City Council), Michael Massey (Paso Robles City Council
  D2), Meri Kaftan (Plumas Lake Elementary School Board), Marjorie Hamada
  (Rancho Cucamonga Mayor), Tenessa Audette (Redding City Council), Lance
  Christensen (Wheatland School Board), Sean Phayakapong (Yorba Linda City
  Council). Ballot status not checked.
- **Judicial retention (2nd District Court of Appeal, Justices Armen
  Tamzarian and Audra Mori)**: HJTA calls the justices "to your attention"
  and says "Voters will decide whether Justices Armen Tamzarian and Audra
  Mori are worthy of retention." There is no explicit Yes/No
  recommendation, so these are omitted as ambiguous.

## Review flags

- Bay Area regional transit tax: HJTA does not name the measure letter; it
  says "NO on regional transit sales tax" for "five Bay Area counties (San
  Francisco, Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo, Santa Clara)". Mapped to the
  existing RTM title.
- Candidate names are as HJTA lists them ("Don Wagner", "Michael Gates",
  "Herb Morgan"); the ballot forms are "Donald P. (Don) Wagner", "Michael
  E. Gates", "Herb W Morgan".
