## Scope of this bundle

This bundle holds the union's **statewide and San Francisco** picks
(`context.jurisdiction` `us/ca/sf`). The East Bay, Peninsula, South Bay,
Sacramento, North Bay and Central Valley picks are in the evidence file
but were NOT extracted. They include Oakland, Berkeley, Hayward, Concord,
San Jose and Stockton races, CD-3/4/6/7/14, and a dozen Assembly and
Senate seats. They could seed a separate bundle, or this one could be
widened to `us/ca`.
