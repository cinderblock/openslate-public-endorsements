## Is this UFCW's own list or a republished Labor Federation list?

The wording "labor-endorsed candidates" echoes the California Labor
Federation. It is kept as UFCW's own list because it differs from the
Labor Federation's general-election list (orgs/ca-labor-federation) in
several places: CD 11 Scott Wiener (Labor Fed: Connie Chan), Insurance
Commissioner dual Allen/Kim (Labor Fed: Kim), SPI No Recommendation (Labor
Fed: Barrera), Prop 40 No Recommendation (Labor Fed: Yes), AD 12 Eric
Lucan (Labor Fed: Jackie Elward), and it adds AD 10, AD 34 and AD 70
picks. A reviewer may still want to treat the overlapping entries as
co-signed with the Labor Federation once that mechanism exists.
