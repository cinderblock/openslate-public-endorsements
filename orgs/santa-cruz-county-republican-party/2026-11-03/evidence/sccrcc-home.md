The June primary delivered a clear message: turnout matters.  Across Santa Cruz County and throughout California, voter participation was low. Elections are decided by those who show up. Prop 39 must pass. It is a straightforward step to strengthen election integrity and public confidence. Santa Cruz County Republicans have never won by waiting for someone else. Success still comes from registering voters, talking to neighbors, making phone calls, walking precincts, and ensuring every Republican ballot is returned and counted. Ballots begin arriving soon. The path forward is simple: get informed, get involved, and—most importantly—vote.
