Measure A (NO)

Measure C (NO)
‍ Santa Clara Public Works Bidding Threshold Increase
Lets a quarter-million dollars of public work be awarded with no competitive bidding and no council vote, and swaps the objective lowest-responsible-bidder standard for subjective best-value scoring that is nearly impossible to challenge after the fact — in a city with a documented contracting controversy record.
Measure D (YES)
Los Altos Downtown Parking Plaza Protection Initiative

‍ Mountain View Charter Omnibus Amendment

‍ Sunnyvale Public Works Contracting Blank Check

Measure H (NO)
‍ Sunnyvale City Manager Settlement Authority Expansion
Lets the council raise by ordinance — never returning to voters — the amount an unelected city manager may pay out to settle legal claims, removing the council vote that is often the only moment the public learns a claim existed at all.

‍ Sunnyvale Appointed Incumbent Term Extension
