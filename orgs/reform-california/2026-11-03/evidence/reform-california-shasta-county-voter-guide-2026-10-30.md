Shasta County Elected Department Head Vacancy Election Requirement
Stops county supervisors from handing the Sheriff's badge, the District Attorney's office or the Auditor-Controller's books to an appointee for years without a vote, requiring instead that vacancies go to the voters at the next scheduled election — with appointment allowed only when no election falls within twelve months, and only after open recruitment, interviews held in public, and a four-fifths vote of the Board.
