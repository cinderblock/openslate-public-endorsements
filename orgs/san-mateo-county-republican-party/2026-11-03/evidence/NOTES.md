# NOTES — San Mateo County Republican Party (SMGOP), Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://smgop.org/voter-guide (HTML): "SMGOP Voter Guide — General Election
  - November 3, 2026", a card list of 15 candidates plus "DOWNLOAD OUR VOTER
  GUIDE" links.
- https://smgop.org/s/SMGOP-November-2026-Election-Guide-v2.pdf, the
  two-page "San Mateo County Voter Recommendations" PDF (v2; a v1 PDF is also
  linked, and v2 was taken as current). All positions cite the PDF, so
  `attribution.mode` is `transcribed`.
- Contest names, ballot names and measure letters were checked against the
  San Mateo County ACRE roster of candidates and measure list (see the Daily
  Journal / SMCDems NOTES for URLs).
- Note: smcgop.com redirects to an unrelated St. Mary's County (MD) GOP site;
  the San Mateo party is at smgop.org.

## Extracted: 59 positions

- 12 state/federal/legislative candidates (Governor, Lt. Gov, AG, SoS,
  Treasurer, Controller, Superintendent, CD 15, CD 16, AD 19, AD 21, AD 23).
- 3 local candidates: SMUHSD (Jennifer Jacobson), Foster City Council D1
  (Art Kiesel), San Carlos City Council (Mark D. Iwanowski).
- 1 judicial retention: Court of Appeal, First District, Justice Kathleen M.
  Banke → `endorse` Yes, reusing the existing retention subject.
- 14 propositions: NO on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37, 38, 40, 44; YES on 39, 41, 42,
  43, 45.
- 29 local measures: RTM and 18 more NO (`endorse` + "No"), P YES, and 10
  NEUTRAL (`neutral`, no choice): CC, DD, F, I, Q, S, AA, L, J, U. The guide's
  own "NEURTAL" typo on J is kept in the statement.

## Judgment calls for review

- "San Mateo Union HSD - Jennifer Jacobsen": no trustee area is given. The
  ACRE roster has "JENNIFER JACOBSON" as a candidate in SMUHSD Trustee Area 3
  (vs. Diana Harris), so it is recorded under `San Mateo Union High School
  District Board, Trustee Area 3` with the ballot spelling "Jennifer Jacobson".
- Ballot names: "Phillip Louis Wing" (guide) → "Philip Louis Wing" (SOS);
  "Peter Sundin Soule" → "Peter Sundin Soulé"; "Mark Iwanowski" → "Mark D.
  Iwanowski". Statewide and AD 21/AD 23 names follow the spelling used by
  other bundles in this repo ("Michael Gates", "Don Wagner", "Herb Morgan",
  "Jabra Muhawieh", "David Johnson").
- The prop descriptions use the guide's own labels (e.g. Prop 3 "Extends
  Income Tax Increases Permanently", Prop 43 "Taxpayer Protection"). The
  numbers match the SOS numbering used by the existing prop subjects.
- The card list uses an image file named "Dave Johnson.png". It is almost
  certainly the photo for the AD 23 card ("David G. Johnson"), not San Mateo
  City Council D5 candidate Dave Johnson, whom neither the page text nor the
  PDF endorses. No position was recorded for San Mateo D5.
