U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. Sam Liccardo for California’s Sixteenth Congressional District
Published
October 13, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Sam Liccardo for California’s Sixteenth Congressional District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congress needs leaders, like Congressman Sam Liccardo, who take a thoughtful and collaborative approach to tackling our nation’s biggest challenges,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “We’ve enjoyed working with Congressman Liccardo on important issues, ranging from AI to housing to permitting reform, that promise to expand economic opportunity for Californians and will ultimately shape the future of our economy. Congressman Liccardo stands out as a true problem solver, and the U.S. Chamber is proud to endorse his reelection to Congress.”
