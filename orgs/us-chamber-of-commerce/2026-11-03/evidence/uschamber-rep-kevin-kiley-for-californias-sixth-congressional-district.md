U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. Kevin Kiley for California’s Sixth Congressional District
Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Kevin Kiley for California’s Sixth Congressional District.
Published
October 08, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Kevin Kiley for California’s Sixth Congressional District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman Kevin Kiley understands that free market policies, not top-down government mandates, are critical for economic growth and prosperity,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “Throughout his time in Congress, Representative Kiley has been a commonsense leader on important issues, ranging from pro-growth tax policy to permitting reform to public safety. Congressman Kiley is a champion for Sacramento-area businesses and families, and the U.S. Chamber endorses his reelection to California’s 6th congressional district.”
“I strongly support Congressman Kevin Kiley for reelection to Congress,” said Lisa Peters, co-owner of Randy Peters Catering in Roseville, CA. "Congressman Kiley understands small businesses and is always willing to help, and he’s exactly the kind of leader we need in Congress.”
“I greatly appreciate Congressman Kevin Kiley’s dedication to our community and leadership on critical issues, like water, workforce, transportation, and public safety,” said Tony Lopez, Vice President at PRIDE Industries in Roseville, CA. “He’s been a great advocate for local businesses and is always willing to pick up the phone and get stuff done. We are supportive of everything’s he’s done for our community and look forward to him continuing to do great things in Congress.”
