U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. Lou Correa for California's Forty-Sixth Congressional District
Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Lou Correa for California's Forty-Sixth Congressional District.
Published
October 09, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Lou Correa for California's Forty-Sixth Congressional District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman Correa supports commonsense policies that expand economic opportunities for Orange County small businesses and families," said Nikkie Nguyen, Western Region Manager at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “He’s a problem solver who has worked across the aisle to cut red tape, protect small businesses from retail theft and frivolous lawsuits, and advance bipartisan permitting reform. The U.S. Chamber appreciates Congressman Correa’s leadership in the newly formed Free Enterprise Caucus, and we are pleased to endorse his re-election to California’s 46th congressional district.”
“The Orange Chamber of Commerce and Visitors Bureau appreciates Congressman Lou Correa’s continued support of our local business community not only through his physical attendance at many events, including ribbon cuttings, business awards ceremonies and our annual State of the City, but also through his willingness to listen, engage, and work alongside business leaders. His commitment to championing small businesses, fostering economic growth, and advocating for the needs of our entrepreneurs helps strengthen the economic vitality of our city,” said Pattie Córdova, Chief Executive Officer at the Orange Chamber of Commerce and Visitors Bureau.
