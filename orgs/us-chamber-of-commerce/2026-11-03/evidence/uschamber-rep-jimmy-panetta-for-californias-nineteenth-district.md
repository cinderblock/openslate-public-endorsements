U.S. Chamber Endorses Rep. Jimmy Panetta for California's Nineteenth District
Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Jimmy Panetta for California's Nineteenth District.
Published
September 15, 2026
WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Rep. Jimmy Panetta for California's Nineteenth District. The U.S. Chamber endorses candidates for federal office who will support policies that drive innovation, grow the economy, and create opportunities for American businesses and workers.
“Congressman Jimmy Panetta is a proven champion of Central Coast small businesses and a commonsense problem solver,” said Jennings Imel, Vice President at the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. “Congressman Panetta has been a leader on critical issues, such as combatting retail theft, streamlining the permitting process, and cutting red tape for Central Coast businesses. We appreciate Congressman Panetta’s bipartisan leadership and are proud to support his reelection to California’s 19th congressional district.”
“We’re pleased to see the U.S. Chamber of Commerce recognize Congressman Panetta’s work on behalf of businesses across the Central Coast. We are grateful for Congressman Panetta’s engagement with our local business community and his attention to issues that impact the ability of businesses to grow, create jobs, and contribute to our region’s economy. We look forward to continuing to work with Congressman Panetta to advance a strong business climate for Santa Cruz County,” said Kristen Brown, CEO of the Santa Cruz Area Chamber.
