## Santa Maria City Council, District 3

- **Non-Partisan** | **Gloria Soto**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Gloria Soto to keep Santa Maria on the right track for progress. Gloria Soto has served on the Santa Maria City Council since 2018, when she was the youngest woman elected to the council. She is the executive director of Future Leaders of America, one of California’s largest Latinx youth-serving organizations, and a board member of the Fund for Santa Barbara, the Allan Hancock Foundation, 805 UndocuFund, and One Community Action. Soto previously worked at Planned Parenthood California Central Coast. Her priorities are affordable housing, youth and families, and small-business vitality. Soto is endorsed by CAUSE Action Fund, Santa Barbara County Democratic Party, and Central Coast Labor Council. Soto is a lifelong resident of Santa Maria. The RaceGeneral election: There are two candidates running in the November 3 general election: Gloria Soto (incumbent) and Corby Poulis Sr. This is a nonpartisan race.
