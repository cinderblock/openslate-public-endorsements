## Fullerton City Council, District 3

- **Non-Partisan** | **Dr. Shana Charles**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Shana Charles to keep Fullerton on the right track for progress. Dr. Shana Charles has served on Fullerton City Council since 2022. Council Member Charles is a professor and public health expert focused on insurance access, underinsurance, and disparities in medical access. As a member of the Fullerton City Council, Charles helped Fullerton join the federal lawsuit against ICE, helped establish rental-assistance programs, and improved infrastructure like crosswalks and traffic signs. Council Member Charles’s top issues are building fiscal sustainability, restoring parks and natural areas, and improving road infrastructure. She has the endorsement of Planned Parenthood, the Orange County Democratic Party, Fullerton Transparency, Moms Demand Action, OC Action, Sierra Club, Wave, and California Working Families Party, Rep. Dave Min, Orange County Supervisor Vicente Sarmiento, Fullerton City Council Member Ahmad Zahra, and several other Fullerton elected officials. The RaceGeneral election: Incumbent Councilmember Shana Charles and Daniel Kim will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan election. The PositionFullerton is governed by a five-person city council. A city council is responsible for establishing policy, passing local laws (called ordinances), voting on budget appropriations, and developing an overall vision for the city. Each year, the council selects members to serve as the Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem for one-year terms. Councilmembers serve four-year terms, and are ‎limited to 3 consecutive terms, or 12 years in office total. After three consecutive terms, city councilmembers are required to sit out a term before running for office again. Read More
