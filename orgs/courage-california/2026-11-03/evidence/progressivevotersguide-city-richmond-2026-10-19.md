## Richmond Mayor

- **Non-Partisan** | **Claudia Jimenez**

  > Elect Claudia Jimenez for Mayor to keep Richmond on the right track for progress. Claudia Jimenez has served as a Richmond City Council member since 2020. In this role, she has strengthened the sanctuary city protections, helped negotiate a $550 million Chevron settlement to fund city services, championed the creation of ROCK (Reach Out with Compassion and Kindness), a mental health crisis response team, and increased city investments in key infrastructure. Jimenez has been a community organizer in Richmond and Contra Costa communities, including advocating for the Rent Control ballot initiative that was passed by Richmond voters in 2016, and the Invest in People, Not Prisons campaign to end the sheriff’s cooperation with ICE and reinvest funds toward reentry programs. She has the endorsement of ACCE Action, Democratic Socialists of America-East Bay, United Auto Workers, APEN Action, Richmond Progressive Alliance, Contra Costa Labor Council, United Teachers of Richmond, California Working Families Party, Lift Up Contra Costa Action, Richmond City Councilmembers Doria Robinson and Sue Wilson, and Alameda County Supervisor Nikki Fortunato Bas. The Race June 2 primary election results: Claudia Jimenez 39%, Ahmad Anderson 28%, Eduardo Martinez (Incumbent) 20%, Demnlus Johnson 10%, and Mark Wassberg 4%. Claudia Jimenez and Ahmad Anderson will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race.
