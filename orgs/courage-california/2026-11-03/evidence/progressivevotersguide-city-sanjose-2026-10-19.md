## San Jose City Council, District 5

- **Non-Partisan** | **Peter Ortiz**

  > Re-elect Councilmember Peter Ortiz to keep San Jose on the right track for progress. Peter Ortiz has served on the San Jose City Council since 2023, when he was elected with 55% of the vote. Prior to city council, he was a trustee on the Santa Clara Board of Education and the Mt. Pleasant School Board, and worked in policy and nonprofit roles. Councilmember Ortiz’s top issues include revitalizing East San José with a dedicated $30 million fund, a safer East San José for all, a humane and accountable approach to homelessness, supporting the local economy and empowering local businesses, making the city more affordable for working families, ensuring parks and open spaces that serve families, and safe and maintained streets for every neighborhood. He has the endorsement of California Working Families Party, Planned Parenthood Advocates Mar Monte, South Bay Labor Council, Silicon Valley Young Democrats, LIUNA Local 270, AFSCME Local 101, Rep. Ro Khanna, Sen. Aisha Wahab, Asm. Ash Kalra, Legislative Progressive Caucus Chair Asm. Alex Lee, Board of Equalization Member Sally Lieber, and Santa Clara County Supervisor Betty Duong. The Race June 2 primary election results: Peter Ortiz (incumbent) 45%, Nora Campos 20%, Karen Martinez 18%, and Vy Dang 17%. Peter Ortiz and Nora Campos will contend in the November 3 general election. This is a nonpartisan race. The Position San Jose is governed by a ten-person city council. A city council is responsible for establishing policy, passing local laws (called ordinances), voting on budget appropriations, and developing an overall vision for the city.
