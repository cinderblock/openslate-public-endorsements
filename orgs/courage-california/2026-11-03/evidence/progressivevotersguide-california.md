# Evidence: Courage California digital voter guide (Progressive Voters Guide, California)

- **Source URL**: https://www.progressivevotersguide.com/california
- **Linked from**: https://couragecalifornia.org/2026-midterm-election-resources/ ("Digital & Customizable California Voter Guide"). Page header: "GUIDE POWERED BY Courage California".
- **Retrieved**: 2026-10-15T01:14:00Z (rendered in Chrome)
- The page is a ~324,000-character JS app with long blurbs per race. Below is a
  verbatim extraction of each race card's heading lines, generated in-browser by
  taking, for every card, four consecutive text lines
  `<district, badge, or date> | <office> | <party label> | <recommended candidate>`
  (and `Proposition #N | <recommendation>` for propositions). State legislative
  races are only shown in county-specific views, not on this statewide view;
  those were taken from the PDF guide instead.
- Cards whose first line reads "Endorsed by Courage California" or a date
  (e.g. "2026-09-28") show the badge/date line in place of the district. Those
  cards are CD6 (Pan), CD7 (Vang), CD14 (Wahab), CD22 (Villegas), as confirmed
  by the PDF guide and the midterm resources page.
