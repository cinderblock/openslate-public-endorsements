## Santa Ana School Board, Member of the Board of Education

- **** | **Hector Bustos**

  > Re-elect Trustee Hector Bustos to keep Santa Ana Unified School District on the right track for progress. Hector Bustos has served as a Santa Ana Unified School District Board (SAUSD) Trustee since 2022 and is currently the vice president. He was elected as the youngest person and the first openly out LGBTQIA+ official in the city’s history. During his tenure, Bustos has delivered strong academic achievement and student success, helped secure funding to modernize schools, launched an innovative lab school at Monroe Elementary in partnership with UC Irvine, expanded school-based mental health teams, stood up for immigrant families, led the Workplace Readiness Resolution, and established student board member positions. He is endorsed by Chispa, Santa Ana Educators’ Association, Orange County Labor Federation, California Working Families Party, Orange County Young Democrats, Planned Parenthood, National Union of Healthcare Workers, and WAVE. Bustos is also supported by State Superintendent Tony Thurmond, Orange County Supervisor Vicente Sarmiento, Santa Ana City Council Members Benjamin Vazquez and Jessie Lopez, and SAUSD Trustees Katelyn Brazer-Aceves and Valerie Magdaleno. The RaceGeneral election: There are two candidates running in the November 3 general election: Hector Bustos (incumbent) and Alejandra Rodriguez. This is a nonpartisan race. The DistrictSchool system: Santa Ana Unified School District is located in Orange County, which is California’s third most populous county. Santa Ana Unified School District includes 11 high schools, 10 middle schools, and 34 elementary schools, serving a population of roughly 44,000 students. Governance structure: Santa Ana Unified School District has a five-person board that provides administrative oversight and manages a budget of $710 million annually. The PositionThe five members of the Santa Ana Unified School District Board of Trustees are elected to districted seats, and joined by two student board members. The Board of Trustees works alongside the superintendent to set the district’s direction, establish an organizational and policy structure, and provide community accountability and leadership for public education.
