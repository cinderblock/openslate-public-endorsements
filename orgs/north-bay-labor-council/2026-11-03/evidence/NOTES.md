# NOTES — North Bay Labor Council, AFL-CIO, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.nbclc.org/2026endorsements (HTML, Wix site, scraped). Heading
  "NORTH BAY LABOR COUNCIL ENDORSEMENTS - 2026 November Election". Sections:
  Federal, State, Sonoma County, Sonoma Measures, Marin County, Marin County
  Measures, Mendocino County, Lake County. "*Priority Race" marks some
  entries.
- Sonoma County measure letters and titles: Sonoma County Registrar of
  Voters, "November 3, 2026, General Election: Local Measures That Were
  Filed"
  (https://sonomacounty.gov/administrative-support-and-fiscal-services/registrar-of-voters/elections/november-3-2026-general-election-local-measures-that-have-been-filed).
- Marin County entries were merged in from a parallel pass by another agent
  (N1). That agent checked Marin ballot names and measure letters/titles
  against the Marin County Elections Department pages ("Local candidates ON
  ballot 11/03/26", "List of measures 11/03/26" and each measure page).
  marincounty.gov returned 403 to this agent, so those Marin titles and names
  were taken as N1 verified them. Every Marin pick is on the source page above.
- Federal and state finalists were checked against the SOS certified list.

## Extracted: 68 positions

- Federal: CD 1, 2, 4 (3). State: SD 2; AD 2, 4, 12 (4).
- Sonoma County: 24 candidates + 5 measures (H, AB, I, K, L; all Yes).
- Marin County: 12 candidates + 11 measures (all Yes).
- Mendocino County: 8 candidates. Lake County: 1.

## Skipped

- "Click here to see the full Statewide endorsement list, including state
  Propositions": a link to the California Labor Federation's PDF. Those are
  the state federation's picks and are covered by `ca-labor-federation`.
- San Rafael City Council District 2 – Eli Hill: N1 found him the sole
  candidate for the seat (uncontested), so it is omitted.
- "College of Marin - Wanden Treanor (Incumbent)": no Marin Community College
  District contest is on Marin's "Local candidates ON ballot" list
  (presumably uncontested), so it is omitted.

## Review flags

- **Marin ballot names** (from N1's check): "William Dittmar" → "Will
  Dittmar", "Susan Adams" (Fairfax) → "Susan Denise Adams", "Deborah
  Gallegos" → "Deborah E. Gallegos". The page lists Beckman and Fruin under
  "Corte Madera City Council" and Gallegos under "Corte Madera Town Council";
  all three are in the single Corte Madera Town Council contest.
- **Two Susan Adamses.** The Rohnert Park D5 "Susan Adams (Incumbent)" is a
  different person. Her choice is "Susan Hollingsworth Adams", the spelling
  the Sonoma County Democratic Party uses.
- **SRJC trustees.** Battenfeld is titled "Sonoma County Junior College
  District Board, Area 5" because the Sonoma Democrats' page gives her area.
  Fishman and Chabaan have no area on either page, so they use the seat-less
  "Sonoma County Junior College District Board of Trustees". Retitle them
  once their areas are known.
- **Top-two.** All federal and state picks are on the SOS certified general
  list. Sonoma Supervisor D2: Paun leads into a runoff with Sylvia Lemus.
  Sonoma Supervisor D4: June coverage showed Bagby in a close three-way race,
  and that she advanced was not confirmed independently (both the council and
  the Sonoma Democrats list her for November). Mendocino Supervisor D3 was
  not checked.
- **Windsor** is a town; the page's "Windsor City Council District 1" is
  titled "Windsor Town Council, District 1".
- Sonoma, Mendocino and Lake local names are as printed on the page; they were
  not checked against those counties' candidate lists.
- Context jurisdiction is `us/ca/sonoma-county` (the council's Santa Rosa
  home), though the council also covers Marin, Mendocino and Lake.
