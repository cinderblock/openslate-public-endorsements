"November 3, 2026 Endorsements" (HTML, scraped). Everything I extracted

only as a cross-check.

## Judgment calls

- Subject titles use the official SF Department of Elections titles (see
  sf-gov-qualified-ballot-measures.md) and the SoS titles for state props.
  Alice's own labels differ slightly, e.g. "Prop 1 - Housing Affordability
  Bond" and "Prop 40 - One-Time Wealth Tax on Taxpayers with Net Worth Above
  $1 Billion".
- "Joaquín Torres" is recorded as "Joaquin Torres" to match the spelling
  other bundles use. The original spelling is kept in the statement.
- "City College Board (4-year term)" maps to "San Francisco Community
  College Board", and "(2-year term)" maps to "...Board, Partial Term".
  These follow the titles other Nov bundles use.
- "Sara Barz for BART Board, District 8" sits oddly on the page, below the
  paid-for disclaimer rather than in the Local Candidates list. It is still
  an explicit endorsement, so it is included. Its jurisdiction is
  `us/ca/bart-8`, matching the majority of Nov bundles.
- RTM is titled "Bay Area — Regional Measure RTM (Regional Transit
  Measure)" with jurisdiction `us/ca/bay-area`. That matches the majority of
  existing Nov bundles; a minority use "Bay Area — Measure RTM (...)".
- All SF races here are November-only, ranked-choice or multi-seat
  contests, so the top-two rule doesn't apply. Every statewide, CD and AD
  candidate listed is on the Nov 3 ballot.
