Ballot Measures
Our thoughts on this year’s statewide propositions. Read our full analyses.
YES on 1
Prop 1 — The 2026 Affordable Housing Bond
This fall, California voters will vote on an $11.25 billion bond measure, Proposition 1, to fund housing programs, including Affordable Housing construction. California YIMBY recommends a “yes” vote on Prop 1.
YES on 2
Prop 2 — Budget Stabilization
To reduce housing costs, we need to reduce local fees. That requires state revenue reform.
NO RECOMMENDATION on 37
Prop 37 — Downpayment Loans
Providing down payment subsidies for first-time homebuyers is a good idea on the merits, but is problematic in a severely supply-constrained market like California.
NO on 40
Prop 40 — Billionaire Wealth Tax
While we believe taxes are good – they’re the price of living in a society, and we generally need more of them – poorly-designed taxes are bad, and can lead to both economic and political harm.
NO on 43
Prop 43 — The Anti-Tax Ballot Measure
It should not take a supermajority of voters to raise revenues for local schools, infrastructure, and public safety.
NO RECOMMENDATION on 45
Prop 45 — Changes to Environmental Review
While this ballot measure meaningfully touches on housing, it will have a much more significant impact on non-housing projects. Pro-housing voters should consider the arguments for and against the measure and make their own decision based on their values.
