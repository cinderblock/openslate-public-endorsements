## santa-clarita / City Council

### District 2: Brett Haddoak [Recommended]

Brett Haddoak is the best choice in the Santa Clarita district 2 council race, rooted in the city’s need and set on solving its challenges. He’s a life-long resident who works as a software engineer. He spends time volunteering with Bridge for Homes, the primary homeless services provider in SCV.

We appreciate the depth and substance of his platform.

He supports building housing of different kinds to accommodate the population and wants to integrate public transit.

He supports building a new hospital to meet the demand in what is now LA County’s third largest city.

He supports removing Flock surveillance cameras.

His opponents are both Republican.

This is an easy one. Vote for Haddoak.

### District 4: Valerie Bradford [Recommended]

In this five candidate race, Valerie Bradford is the best choice against incumbent Republican councilmember Laurene Weste. A nonprofit founder and a long-time community activist, she’s focused on compassionate effective responses to homelessness, protecting renters, creating affordable housing and good jobs. She’s the only Democrat in the race and is the only one backed by environmental and labor organizations.

### District 5: Kelvin Driscoll [Recommended]

This race in a diverse, renter-heavy part of Santa Clarita features a contest between Dr. Kelvin Driscoll, who’s been a leader in social service and child welfare work, versus Bob Khalsa, who’s a landlord. Driscoll has the endorsement of the Santa Clarita Democrats, Congressman George Whitesides, the LA County Federation of Labor, Sierra Club, and many more. Vote for Driscoll.
