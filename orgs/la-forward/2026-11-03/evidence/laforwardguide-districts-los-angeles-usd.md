## los-angeles-usd / Ballot Measures

### Measure SC — Charter Amendment: Campaign Finance [Support]

Measure SC is a relatively minor charter amendment that clarifies campaign finance rules for LAUSD board candidates and aligns those rules with rules for LA City Mayor. It pertains to rules that cover campaign contribution limits, filing deadlines, fundraising windows, and public disclosures.

There are no organized campaigns for or against the charter amendment. It’s backed by Diego Andrades, a former member of the city’s Charter Reform Commission.

LA Forward recommends a YES vote.
