Assembly District 51: Colin Hernandez Recommended Incumbent Rick Zbur was the long-time Executive Director of Equality California when he ran for this seat that stretches diagonally from Santa Monica through East Hollywood in 2022.
We had high hopes for Zbur despite our fondness for his Democratic opponent in that race, Louis Abramson. a physicist and SELAH homelessness outreach leader.
Despite a generally positive voting record (91 out of 100 Courage Score), we’ve been deeply disappointed in Zbur’s political maneuvering the last four years.
A rumored candidate for State Attorney General, Zbur has cozied up to law enforcement unions. He’s received contributions from the California Correctional Peace Officers Association (CCPOA PAC), Peace Officers Research Association for California (PORAC PAC), and LA County Probation Officers Union – AFSCME, Local No. 685 PAC.
To quote the 2024 Initiate Justice Action voter guide, “As a member of the Public Safety Committee, Assemblymember Zbur has voiced concerns with numerous criminalization bills, but still voted to support the overwhelming majority of them.”
Another consequence of Zbur’s tack to the center was his vocal and aggressive support for the conservative Democratic challenge to LA Councilmember Nithya Raman from Ethan Weaver in the March 2024 primary, backed by millions in spending from the police union and corporate landlords.
He refused to vote for LA Forward-supported legislation such as “Make Polluters Pay” and Senate Bill 417 sponsored by tenants’ rights group Alliance of Californians for Community Empowerment, which would have capped annual rent rate increases to the Consumer Price Index, at 5%. Zbur expressed concerns about how some of the proposed bills would hurt landlords.
Zbur is being challenged from the left by DSA member Colin Hernandez. His campaign is a long shot but we’re far too disappointed with Zbur on a wide range of policy and political matters to let him get a pass.
Hernandez is endorsed
