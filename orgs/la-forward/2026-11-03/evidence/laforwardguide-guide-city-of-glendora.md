### District 1: Teri Merrick [Recommended]

Glendora City Council District 1 offers a clear contrast between incumbent Michael Allawos and challenger Teri Merrick. Allawos has served on the council since 2017 and emphasizes public safety, fiscal restraint, local control and economic development. Merrick, a longtime Glendora resident and retired professor, brings a different set of priorities centered on affordable housing, protecting parks and foothills, safer streets for pedestrians and cyclists, community-based policing, and greater public involvement in major city decisions. For voters who place particular importance on housing affordability, sustainability, mobility and participatory government, Merrick presents the stronger argument.

LA Forward recommends voting for Merrick.
