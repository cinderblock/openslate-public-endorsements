Crime Victims United Endorses Dom Belza for California State Assembly
 May 10
Written By Mallory Adams
Crime Victims United is proud to endorse Dom Belza for the California State Assembly representing District 3. As a fourth-generation North State resident with deep roots in the community, Belza has demonstrated a strong understanding of the challenges facing California families and the importance of prioritizing public safety and victims’ rights.
Throughout his campaign, Belza has emphasized the need for safer communities, stronger protections for victims, and a criminal justice system that maintains accountability while supporting those impacted by crime. His commitment to standing alongside victims and advocating for policies that reduce crime and strengthen communities closely aligns with the mission of Crime Victims United.
Belza has also earned overwhelming support from members of the law enforcement community, reflecting confidence in his leadership and dedication to public safety issues affecting Californians across the state. His willingness to engage directly with victims’ advocacy organizations and understand the real-life impact crime has on families further demonstrates his commitment to meaningful reform and stronger protections for victims.
Crime Victims United believes Dom Belza will be a strong voice for victims and public safety in Sacramento and is proud to support his campaign for the California State Assembly.
 Mallory Adams
