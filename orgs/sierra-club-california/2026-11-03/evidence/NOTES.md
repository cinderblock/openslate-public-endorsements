## Ballot check (top-two)

Every endorsed legislative candidate was cross-checked against Wikipedia's
2026 California State Senate / State Assembly general-election matchups
(retrieved 2026-10-15 ~01:35Z). All are on the Nov 3 ballot. SD 24 (John
Erickson) and SD 26 (Sara Hernandez) are same-party D v. D generals; note
that EnviroVoters and Courage endorse the other Democrat in SD 26 (Sarah
Rascón), and EnviroVoters endorses Brian Goldsmith in SD 24.
