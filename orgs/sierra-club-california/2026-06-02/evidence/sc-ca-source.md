Sierra Club California's State Political Committee endorsements for the
2026 election cycle. The source page doesn't separate primary from general
explicitly, but California's top-two open primary system means an
endorsement for the primary stage is the relevant one for June 2 2026
(the top two advance to November regardless of party).

Governor endorsement quote (from press release):
> "Tom Steyer has not just talked about climate and environmental
> leadership, he has helped build and lead it" — Loren Blackford, Sierra
> Club Executive Director.

Party affiliations not listed on the source page; omitted from positions.
