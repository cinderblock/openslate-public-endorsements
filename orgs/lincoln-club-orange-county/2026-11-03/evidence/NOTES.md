# NOTES — Lincoln Club of Orange County, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.lincolnclub.org/voterguide ("2026 VOTER GUIDE", linked from
  the home page). It's a Wix page whose only text is a form confirmation.
  The guide is three embedded PNG screenshots dated 2026-10-08. I
  downloaded them from static.wixstatic.com and transcribed them by hand
  (see `lincolnclub-voterguide.md`). Mode is `transcribed`.

## Extracted: 113 positions

- Congress: CD 13, 22, 40, 45, 47, 48, 49 (7). The club's guide reaches
  beyond OC: CD 13 (Kevin Lincoln), CD 22 (David Valadao), CD 48 (Jim
  Desmond) and SD 4 (Alexandra Duarte).
- Statewide: 7 offices. No Insurance Commissioner pick is listed. BOE
  District 4 (Bilodeau).
- Legislature: SD 4, 34, 36, 38 (4). AD 49, 59, 67, 70, 71, 72, 73, 74 (8).
- County and special districts: Supervisor D4 (Tim Shaw), D5 (Diane
  Dixon), Moulton Niguel Water (Trish Kelley), Mesa Water Division 5
  (Shawn Dewane).
- Propositions: all 14 (No on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37, 38, 40, 44; Yes on 39, 41,
  42, 43, 45), plus Irvine Measure D (No).
- Cities: 47 races. Schools: 20 races.

## Top-two and ballot checks

- All state and federal picks are top-two finalists on the SOS certified
  list (CD 13 Gray vs. Lincoln, CD 22 Villegas vs. Valadao, SD 4 Brandon
  vs. Duarte, CD 40 Calvert vs. Young Kim, and so on).
- Local contests were checked against the OC Registrar's qualified-contests
  page:
  https://ocvote.gov/elections/contests-qualified-for-the-ballot-for-the-2026-general-election

## Skipped

  election):

    Dana Point contest qualified.
  - Laguna Niguel CC D2 (Ray Gennaway).
  - Seal Beach CC D3 (Lisa Landau) and D5 (Nathan Steele). Only D1
    qualified.
  - Newport-Mesa USD Area 4 (Lisa Pearson) and Area 5 (Andrea McElroy).
    Only Areas 2 and 7 qualified.
  - "Orange County Water District 5" (Steve Sheldon). OCWD is not on the
    qualified list.
- Ambiguous:
  - Rancho Santa Margarita "CC — Bradley McGir". No district is given, and
    D1, D2 and D4 are all on the ballot. The OCGOP guide lists "BRAD
    MCGIRR" for District 4, so this is probably RSM D4, but it was left
    out because the source doesn't say.
  - Coast CCD "Area 3 — Xerxez Megino Calilung". Area 3 is listed twice
    (also Conrad Mazeika). The OCGOP guide lists Calilung for Trustee Area
    5, so this is probably a typo for Area 5. Left out; Mazeika was kept
    for Area 3 (Short Term).

## Judgment calls / review flags

- **Spelling.** Choices keep the club's spelling except: "Joe Stapelton" →
  `Joe Stapleton` (OCGOP spelling; reads as a typo) and "Phil Chen" →
  `Phillip Chen` (SOS ballot name). Other names differ from the OCGOP
  guide and were kept as printed: `Anne Hertz-Mallari` (OCGOP: "ANNE
  MALLARI"), `Mike Buley` (OCGOP: "MICHAEL BULEY"), `Trish Kelley` (OCGOP:
  "TRISH KELLY").
- **"At-Large" (Laguna Beach)** maps to "Laguna Beach City Council", a
  three-seat at-large race. **"Santa Ana CC District 2"** maps to Ward 2.
  **"Huntington Beach City Attorney"** is a new title.
- **Short-term seats.** Aliso Viejo D3, Mission Viejo D2/D4 and Coast CCD
  Area 3 are titled "(Short Term)", as the registrar lists them.
- **Supervisor jurisdiction.** Same `us/ca/orange` reuse as the other OC
  bundles; see the oc-democratic-party NOTES.
- The image transcription should be spot-checked against the PNGs (URLs in
  the evidence file).
- **Moulton Niguel Water District.** The registrar has two contests: "3
  Directors, Full Term" and "1 Director, Short Term". This guide doesn't
  say which one Trish Kelly/Kelley is running in. She is filed under the
  full-term title "Moulton Niguel Water District Board". If she is the
  short-term candidate, retitle her to "Moulton Niguel Water District Board
  (Short Term)". The DPOC city guides list Jennifer Engle (full term) and
  David Petty (short term).
