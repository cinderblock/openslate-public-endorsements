# NOTES — VoteVets PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

VoteVets is a national organization. This bundle holds only its
California picks for the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://www.votevets.org/candidates ("Endorsed Candidates"). A single
  page with sections for US Senate, US House, National Security
  Professionals, State Offices and Local Offices. The Local Offices section
  is an alphabetical list that also includes state legislative candidates.
  It has 21 California lines.
- https://www.votevets.org/endorsements and
  https://votevets.org/endorsed-candidates have no candidate list.

## Extracted: 10 positions

- US House: CD 36 Lieu, CD 24 Carbajal, CD 19 Panetta, CD 45 Tran, CD 31
  Cisneros.
- State Senate: SD 10 Sakakihara, SD 20 Menjivar, SD 32 Tate.
- Board of Equalization District 4: Tom Umberg.
- San Francisco Board of Supervisors District 4: Alan Wong (listed as
  "California, City Supervisor").

## Skipped

- Josh Newman (Superintendent of Public Instruction). He did not advance;
  the finalists are Richard Barrera and Sonja Shaw.
- Lucas Pinon ("California, State Assembly"). No district is given and I
  could not identify one. He does not appear among the Assembly finalists
  in existing bundles.
- Local lines with no city or district named, so the contest could not be
  identified: Alexander Patrick Amoroso, Kim Martin Bowman Jr., Dan Hall,
  Andrea Marr, Byron Salvatierra and Angela Denise Scott (each "City
  Council"); James Cho ("School Board"); Huy Nguyen ("College Board of
  Trustees"); Derek Rodriguez ("Rent Stabilization Board"). A reviewer
  could match these to specific contests from the candidates' own sites.

## Review flags

- **Stale district numbers.** The page says "Ted Lieu California-33" and
  "Jimmy Panetta California-20". Those are pre-2022 district numbers.
  Under the current map Lieu runs in CD 36 and Panetta in CD 19 (existing
  bundles). Recorded with the current districts.
- **Alan Wong.** "City Supervisor" only makes sense in San Francisco, and
  Alan Wong is a November finalist for SF Supervisor District 4. Treat as
  probable.
- **Names normalized:** "Thomas J Umberg" -> "Tom Umberg"; "Dr. Tiffanie
  Tate" -> "Tiffanie Tate".
- Every recorded choice matches a November finalist in existing bundles.
