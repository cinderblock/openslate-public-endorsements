 Small business PAC endorses or opposes six of the 14 initiatives in November’s election
For Immediate Release
Contact: John Kabateck, California State Director, john.kabateck@nfib.org
or Tony Malandra, Senior Media Manager, anthony.malandra@nfib.org
SACRAMENTO, CA (Oct. 8, 2026) – The political action arm of the state’s leading small business association today announced its recommendations on six of the 14 initiatives on the November 3 General Election ballot.
“This is a very frustrating time for small business owners in California,” said John Kabateck, state director for NFIB in California, speaking for the NFIB CA PAC, which made the recommendations.
“We live in a state with the highest income taxes and gas prices, the most amount of regulations — by far – and with a hostile Legislature that couldn’t get passed even modest relief from shakedown lawsuits. So, you can understand why Main Street entrepreneurs are in no mood to institute new taxes (Proposition 40) or make current ones permanent (Proposition 3). They are up, however, for cutting red tape (Proposition 45), giving the people back they’re say on property taxes (Proposition 43), and holding government accountable for the money it raises from us (Propositions 41, 42).
“The word ‘affordability’ comes tripping off the lips of politicians and many of the public so frequently, it’s rapidly lost any meaning it once had. One sure way — in fact, the first step — to making California more affordable is to take greater care with the ballot measures we vote on and with the candidates we elect to office.”
The small business PAC’s recommendations are:
PROPOSITION 3: OPPOSE—Permanently extends state income tax hikes (originally opposed by NFIB in 2012)
PROPOSITION 40: OPPOSE—Billionaire’s tax, imposing a 5% tax on total assets
PROPOSITION 41: SUPPORT—Requires audit of any new special taxes
PROPOSITION 42: SUPPORT—Prohibits new state personal property taxes and certain retroactive state taxes
PROPOSITION 43: SUPPORT—Requires 2/3 voter approval for any new special taxes
PROPOSITION 45: SUPPORT—Changes state environmental law to cut red tape, expedite building process.
Having small business on your side is no small matter. Check out The Power of the Small Business Voice. Also check out Why Doesn’t NFIB Take a Position on This Issue to learn more about the small-business issues NFIB advocates for.
