Small Business PAC Endorses 30 Candidates for the California Legislature

October 15, 2026

NFIB gives backing to seven running for the State Senate, 23 running for the State Assembly

SACRAMENTO, CA (Oct. 15, 2026) – The political action committee for California’s leading small business association today announced its endorsements for the Legislature, a bipartisan list of 30 office-seekers for the Senate and the Assembly.

“Small business has to take greater care in choosing candidates for Senate and Assembly than big business and big labor do, because when the cut-and-thrust of legislative activity shifts into high gear, we need to depend on lawmakers keeping their word,” said John Kabateck, state director for NFIB in California, speaking for the NFIB CA PAC, which made the endorsements.

“The candidates we have endorsed either have a prior, pro-small-business voting record in the Legislature or have responded to a questionnaire that assures the concerns of Main Street enterprises will be in their minds throughout the entire legislative session.”
