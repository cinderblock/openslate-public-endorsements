# NOTES — California Nurses Association, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.nationalnursesunited.org/california-endorsements
  ("California Endorsements"; HTML, scraped with curl). The URL
  https://www.nationalnursesunited.org/ca-endorsements redirects here. The
  page says "We are proud to support congressional, state, and local
  candidates and initiatives that share nurses' values". Endorsements are
  listed as "Office – Name"; ballot measures as "... - Support/Oppose".
- The page is undated, but it lists the Nov 2026 proposition numbers and
  the general-election finalists, so it is the general-election list.

## Extracted: 79 positions

- 9 propositions: Support → Yes on 1, 3, 4, 40; Oppose → No on 39, 41, 42,
  43, 45. Props 2, 5, 37, 38 and 44 are not listed.
- 7 statewide/BOE: Lt. Gov Ma, AG Bonta, SoS Weber, Controller Cohen,
  Treasurer Kounalakis, Insurance Commissioner Kim, BOE D2 Lieber. No
  Governor or Superintendent pick is listed.
- 10 State Senate, 45 Assembly positions (44 districts; AD 12 is a dual
  endorsement, "Jackie Elward & Eric Lucan - Dual Endorsement", recorded as
  two `endorse` positions).
- 5 local races whose titles already exist: Los Angeles Mayor (Karen Bass),
  Oakland Mayor (Barbara Lee), Richmond Mayor (Claudia Jimenez), Long Beach
  CCD Areas 1 and 3 (Udak-Joe Ntuk, Sunny Zia).
- 3 local measures whose titles already exist: Oakland Measure EE (Yes), SF
  Proposition D (No), Grossmont-Cuyamaca CCD Measure G (Yes; the page says
  "Grossmont-Cuyamaca Community College District Bond Measure - Support"
  with no letter; Measure G is the district's only Nov 2026 measure and
  other bundles describe it as the district's school bond).
- No US House endorsements are on this page.

Certified List of Candidates (8/27/2026,
https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf).

Mayor runoff (other bundles carry both Bass and Raman); Lee, Jimenez,
Ntuk and Zia appear in other bundles for the same Nov races.
