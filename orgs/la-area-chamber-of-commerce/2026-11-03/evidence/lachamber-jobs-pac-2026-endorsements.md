### City of Los Angeles Ballot Measures

### PROP TT - ✖ OPPOSE

Funding for City Services Through Modifications to the Transient Occupancy Tax

Increases the TOT or "hotel tax" by an additional 2% surcharge through December 2028 and a permanent 1% increase thereafter.

### PROP TC - ✅ SUPPORT

Applying Transient Occupancy Tax to Online Travel Companies

Requires online travel companies to pay the TOT or "hotel tax" in the City.

### County of Los Angeles Ballot Measures

### MEASURE ER - ✖ OPPOSE

Half-Cent General Sales Tax ("Essential Services Restoration Act")

Increases the general sales and use tax by 0.5% for five years to support healthcare-related services.

Voters can also access helpful resources through the Secretary of State’s Promote the Vote California website [https://www.sos.ca.gov/elections/voting-resources/voting-california/help-strengthen-our-democracy/video-resources].
