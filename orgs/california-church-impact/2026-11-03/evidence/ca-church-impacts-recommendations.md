We are pleased to present California Church IMPACT’s recommendations for the statewide propositions on California’s November 3, 2026 General Election ballot. These recommendations from our staff are based on our Legislative Principles, developed by our Board of Directors, and which govern our work and our positions on legislation and ballot propositions. We hope you find them helpful.
