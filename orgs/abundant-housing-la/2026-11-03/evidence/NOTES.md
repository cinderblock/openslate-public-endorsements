## Flags for review

1. **Whittier Measure EV omitted.** The image says "MEASURE EV | YES |
   Whittier | Aligns municipal elections with state and federal
   elections." In the Registrar's list, EV is the Mayor and Council term
   limits charter amendment and **EL** is the election-date amendment.
   The letter and description conflict, so neither was recorded.
2. Monterey Park: Thomas Wong is unopposed in Council District 1; mapped
   to the existing title `Monterey Park City Council` used by other
   bundles.
3. Choices use ballot names (e.g. "John M. Erickson", "Caroline M.
   Torosis", "Thomas Aujero Small", "Juan Munoz-Guevara").
