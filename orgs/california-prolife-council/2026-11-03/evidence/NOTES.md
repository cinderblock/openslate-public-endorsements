# NOTES — California ProLife Council, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://californiaprolife.org/2026election/ ("2026 Recommendations";
  HTML, scraped with curl). The home page link
  https://californiaprolife.org/2026-statewide-and-congressional-endorsements/
  redirects here. Sections: "Constitutional Offices", "Statewide Ballot
  Propositions — Official CPLC Ballot Recommendations for all California
  Voters", and "Official CPLC Congressional Recommendations (25
  Districts)". Paid for by the California ProLife Council PAC.

  election guide.
