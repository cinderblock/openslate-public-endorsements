## Extracted (166 positions)

- 8 statewide offices and 4 Board of Equalization seats.
- 51 US House districts: every district except CD 40, which is a Republican-vs-Republican race (Ken Calvert vs Young Kim).
- 17 State Senate districts.
- 72 State Assembly districts.
- 14 propositions: Yes on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37, 38 and 40; No on 39, 41, 42, 43, 44 and 45. The general-election PDF lists the same positions as the October proposition card.

## Not endorsed, so omitted

- "No Consensus": AD 12, SD 24 and SD 26.
- No entry in the PDF: AD 3, 8, 22, 32, 33, 59 and 63; SD 12. Per the SOS certified list, none of these has a Democrat on the November ballot except **AD 33**, where Hipolito Angel Cerros (D) faces Alexandra Macedo (R) and the PDF lists no CADem pick. AD 8, 22 and 32 have only one candidate.
- Local races and local measures. CADem only covers federal, state and statewide-measure contests; county central committees endorse locally. The by-address "CADEM Voter Guide" lookup was not scraped.

## Verification

Every candidate was checked against the Secretary of State's *Certified List of Candidates for the November 3, 2026, General Election* (dated 8/27/2026, https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf). Each endorsed candidate is on the ballot in the district the PDF names. The June-primary rule about dropping endorsees who didn't advance never comes into play, because CADem made this list after the primary.

## Judgment calls

- **Names** follow the PDF, with two honorifics dropped: "Dr. Richard Pan" (CD 6) became "Richard Pan" and "Dr. Ida Obeso-Martinez" (AD 36) became "Ida Obeso-Martinez". Accents are kept as printed ("Linda Sánchez", "Nanette Barragán").
- **"SD 28 - Lola / Smallwood-Cuevas"** wraps across two lines in the PDF. The ballot name is Lola Smallwood-Cuevas.
- **Proposition statements**: on page 1 of the proposition PDF, the YES/NO column and the description text extract as separate blocks. Both blocks run in proposition order, and the bilingual card on page 2 (short descriptions next to each number) confirms the pairing. Please spot-check the pairing against the PDF image.
- **Candidate statements**: the general-election PDF is a bare list with no justification text, so candidate positions have no `statement`.
