# NOTES — Equality California, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.eqca.org/elections/ ("Our Endorsements" → "Current
  Endorsements"; HTML, scraped with curl). The page says "Equality
  California endorses and supports 100% pro-equality candidates — up and
  down the ballot". It lists the Nov 2026 propositions by number and links
  separately to "our 2026 primary election endorsements" under "Past
  Endorsements", so the current list is the general-election list.

## Extracted: 185 positions

- 8 propositions: YES on 1, 2, 3, 38; NO on 39, 40, 43, 44. Props 4, 5, 37,
  41, 42 and 45 are not listed.
- 12 statewide/BOE: Becerra, Ma, Weber, Bonta, Kounalakis, Barrera, Cohen,
  Insurance Commissioner Ben Allen, BOE Esparza, Lieber, Gipson, Umberg.
- 49 US House, 18 State Senate, 66 State Assembly.
- 2 local measures: San Diego County Measures A and B (YES), mapped to the
  existing county titles.
- 30 local candidates whose race titles already exist in other bundles
  (Berkeley D7, Burbank, Concord D3, Culver City council and USD board, El
  Cajon D1, El Cerrito, Fullerton D5, Grossmont UHSD Area 4, Helix Water
  Divisions 2 and 4, La Mesa-Spring Valley SD Area 3, Lemon Grove, Oakland
  D2 and D4, Pasadena CCD Area 7, SFUSD Board of Education, SF Community
  College Board, San Mateo County Board of Education Area 2, San Ramon D4,
  Santa Monica, Vista Mayor, West Hollywood).

## Top-two check

All statewide, congressional and legislative candidates were checked
against the SOS Certified List of Candidates for Nov 3 2026 (8/27/2026,
https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf).
All are on the general ballot in the listed district (accent differences
only: "Nanette Barragán" is "Nanette Diaz Barragan" on the ballot;
"Anamarie Ávila Farías" is "Anamarie Avila Farias"). The local candidates
mapped to existing titles also appear as choices in other bundles' Nov
lists for those races.

## Review flags

- **Two SF school board lines.** The page has both "San Francisco Board of
  Education, At-Large: Ryan Hazelton" and "San Francisco Unified School
  District Board of Trustees: Phil Kim". Both are mapped to the existing
  "San Francisco Unified School District Board of Education".
- **Two Culver City school lines.** "Culver City School Board: Triston
  Ezidore" and "Culver City Unified School District, At-Large: Stephanie
  Loredo" are both mapped to "Culver City Unified School District Board".
- **Helix Water District.** Mapped to the `us/ca/san-diego` jurisdiction,
  which most bundles use (one bundle uses `us/ca/helix-water-district` for
  Division 2).
- "Dr." dropped from "Dr. Richard Pan"; trailing comma dropped from
  "Anamarie Ávila Farías,".
