# NOTES: League of Women Voters of San Francisco, November 3, 2026 general

Sources:
- https://lwvsf.org/ballot-recommendations ("Proposition endorsements — For
  the November 3, 2026 election"), fetched 2026-10-15T01:06:43Z.
- https://lwvsf.org/proposition-information, fetched 2026-10-15T01:09:42Z.
  This page was only used to confirm the scope of LWVSF's own positions.

## Extracted: 10 positions (SF Props A–J)

- No on A, D, E and F. Yes on B, C, H, I and J. Each has a short verbatim
  quote from the League's reasoning as `statement`.
- Prop G is recorded as `neutral`, not `abstain`. The League says "NEUTRAL"
  and "After discussion, our board of directors decided to be neutral on
  Proposition G". The page distinguishes neutral ("competing positions")
  from "no position" and "no recommendation".
- Titles match the SF Dept. of Elections qualified-measures list
  (https://www.sf.gov/reports/november-2024/qualified-measures; the page
  covers Nov 3, 2026 despite the URL).

## Skipped

- **State propositions and Regional Measure RTM.** LWVSF does not take its
  own position on these. Its proposition-information page sends readers to
  "the League of Women Voters of the Bay Area's regional transit measure
  endorsement" and "the League of Women Voters of California's state
  proposition endorsements". Those belong to other entities. The LWV-CA
  state positions are captured in `orgs/lwv-california/`. LWV Bay Area
  (RTM) is not captured here.
- **Candidates.** LWV is nonpartisan and does not endorse candidates.
