## Substantive description (quoted)

> "The California Fair Elections Act will provide voters the opportunity
> to remove the current prohibition on public financing of campaigns in
> California while establishing basic requirements that public financing
> systems and candidates using public funding must follow to protect
> taxpayers and maximize the benefit to voters."

The Act will:

- "Repeal the current ban on public funding of campaigns so any city,
  county, district, and the state itself can pass public funding systems
  as long as the systems meet specific requirements."
- "Require that public funding candidates must abide by expenditure
  limits and meet strict criteria to qualify."
- "Prohibit the use of public funds to pay for legal defense or fines."
- "Prohibit candidates that receive public funds from repaying personal
  loans with public funds, or repaying personal loans with private
  donations after the campaign ends."
- "Prohibit public funding from discriminating based on party or against
  challengers in favor of incumbents."
