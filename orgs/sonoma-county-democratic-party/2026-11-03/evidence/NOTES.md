## Review flags

- **Measure jurisdictions.** All district measures use
  `us/ca/sonoma-county`; city measures use city slugs. Measure D (Coast Life
  Support District) spans Sonoma and Mendocino, with Mendocino as the
  registrar's "lead county"; it is filed under `us/ca/sonoma-county` here.
- **Registrar typo.** The registrar lists "Measure O: Old Adboe Union School
  District Bond"; the title uses "Adobe".
- **Name spelling.** "Shaun Du Fosee" (page) → "Shaun Du Fosse" (the North
  Bay Labor Council's spelling, likely correct). Not checked against the
  Sonoma candidate list. Other local names are as printed.
- **Top-two.** All federal/state picks are on the SOS certified list. For
  Supervisor D4 (Bagby), June coverage showed a close three-way race; that
  Bagby advanced was not independently confirmed (both this party and the
  North Bay Labor Council list her for November).
- No other bundle has Sonoma County local titles yet, so these are new
  ("<City> City Council, District N", "<District> Board, Area N",
  "<Jurisdiction> — Measure X (<registrar title>)").
