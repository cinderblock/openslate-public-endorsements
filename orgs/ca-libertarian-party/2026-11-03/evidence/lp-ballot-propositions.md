Libertarian Party of California Positions on the November 2026 Ballot Propositions
Last Updated: July 2026
California voters will decide 14 statewide ballot propositions in the November 2026 General Election. The Libertarian Party of California (LPC) has adopted official positions on 12 of the 14 measures through actions of the state convention and the Executive Committee.
The Party has not taken positions on Propositions 5 and 45.
