## Extracted (3 positions)

The post says "Three candidates endorsed by the Peace and Freedom Party will appear on the November 2026 ballot":

- **AD 14: Mark Rendon** (Green Party; Left Unity Slate; endorsed by both GP and PFP).
- **AD 59: Victor Hernandez** (Green Party; Left Unity Slate).
- **AD 63: Kevin Akin** (PFP's own candidate; qualified for November as a write-in in the primary).

All three are on the SOS certified list for November 3: AD 14 Buffy Wicks / Mark Rendon; AD 59 Phillip Chen / Victor Hernandez; AD 63 Natasha Johnson / Kevin Akin.

- **The October 3 forum notice** describes Prop. 45 as one "which guts the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA)" and lists a "No on Prop. 45" speaker plus several Bay Area local candidates. If PFP later publishes a proposition list, a No on 45 would very likely be on it.

## Judgment calls / flags

- **The endorsement is carried over from the primary.** The source is a post-primary results article, not a fresh November slate. It states that these are PFP-endorsed candidates who will be on the November ballot, which I read as a standing endorsement into the general. Two of the three are Green Party candidates, endorsed as part of the joint Left Unity Slate.
- **AD 63 caveat**: the post says Akin "is not yet on the Left Unity Slate". He is still PFP's own candidate, and the post counts him among the "three candidates endorsed by the Peace and Freedom Party".
- **Name**: PFP spells the AD 14 candidate "Mark Rendòn". The ballot name is "Mark Rendon", which I used in `choice`; the statement quotes PFP's spelling.
