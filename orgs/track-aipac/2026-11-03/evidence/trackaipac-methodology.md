# Track AIPAC: Adapting Data Methodology to Identify the Israel Lobby's Hidden Donor Movements

Source: https://www.trackaipac.com/blog/updated-methodology
Fetched: 2026-10-30 8:05 PM PDT (2026-10-31T03:05:49Z)

Update (April 28, 2026): We have decided to revert from our recently launched V3 methodology back to V2 in an effort to improve data clarity and avoid confusion as we build out a publicly searchable database for V3 data. Click here for our official statement on this change.

The figures we report represent federal career totals. Our reports do not capture state level campaign finance data as each state has their own campaign finance reporting system and we do not have the infrastructure or personnel needed to track those – yet.

The finance record alone is not enough to land a member of Congress (or a candidate) with a red graphic. Our scorecards represent a nexus of lobby spending and the candidate’s policy record.

Red graphics are assigned when we evaluate a candidate’s policy as pro-Israel based on their voting record (if one exists), any published policy positions, any public statements, and any credible reporting verifying that the candidate is seeking AIPAC’s support – often by circulating secret position papers favorable to Israel.

If a candidate is identified as being pro-Israel based on these public records, and campaign finance data is not yet available, we will assign a “WARNING!” label to their graphic.

For current members of Congress, we turn to the Congressional Democrat Palestine Tracker maintained by members of the New York DSA and linked to our website under Resources .

Note: Green graphics on their own are not endorsements. They are simply scorecards representing Israel lobby influence.
