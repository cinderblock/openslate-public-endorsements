# NOTES — Orange County Labor Federation, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.oclabor.org/cope-endorsements-2026/ ("2026 General Election
  COPE Endorsements", HTML, scraped). The page links a 5-page PDF with the
  same list,
  https://www.oclabor.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/2026-General-Election-COPE-Endorsements.pdf.
  I downloaded it and checked it line by line against the HTML. The content
  is identical, so the HTML is cited and mode is `scraped`.
- Neither the page nor the PDF takes a position on any proposition or local
  measure. For the statewide labor positions on props, see the
  ca-labor-federation bundle.

## Judgment calls / review flags

- **Two picks for one seat.** The page lists "North OC Community College,
  Area 6" twice, for Paulette Chaffee and for Jesus Silva. Both are
  included as printed. The PDF shows the same thing. This may be a dual
  endorsement or a typo for another area (Areas 1 and 3 are also on the
  ballot).
- **Spelling fixes** (the printed form is kept in each statement):
  "Shirely Weber" → `Shirley Weber`; "Loren Gammeros" → `Loren Gameros`
  (Costa Mesa councilmember; DPOC spelling); "Ben Vasquez" → `Benjamin
  Vazquez` (Santa Ana Ward 2 incumbent; DPOC spelling).
- **Names kept as printed but worth checking**: "Kelly Anne Brown" (DPOC
  and Sierra Club say "Kelly Brown"); "Andrew Paul Gerken" (DPOC says "Dr.
  Andy Gerken"); "Sara J Pelly" (DPOC says "Sara Pelley").
- **Labels mapped to registrar titles**: "Santa Ana City Council, District
  2/6" became Ward 2/6. "Cypress Elementary School District, Area C" became
  Cypress School District Board, Trustee Area C. "Board of Equalization,
  Region 4" became District 4. "(Mayor)" entries became "<City> Mayor".
  Aliso Viejo D3 and Coast CCD Area 3 became "(Short Term)".
- **Supervisor jurisdiction.** The supervisor races reuse the existing
  `us/ca/orange` subject (shared with sierra-club-angeles and
  ca-environmental-voters). It clashes with the City of Orange slug. Other
  county-level contests use `us/ca/orange-county`.
- All state and federal picks are top-two finalists on the SOS certified
  list.
