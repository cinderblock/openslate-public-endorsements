- **SD 14**: the page's link text says "Go to Darnin's Website" (a CRA typo). The card name is "Darin DuPont", which matches the ballot.
- The PDF quick sheet drops "ti"/"tt"/"ff" ligatures in text extraction, so names come from the HTML. The two sources agree apart from AD 49.
- **Proposition statements** are CRA's own list items ("YES on 39", "NO on 1", ...).
