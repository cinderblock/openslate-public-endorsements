# Evidence: California Republican Assembly 2026 primary endorsements

- Sources:
  - https://cragop.org/2026-primary-election-endorsements/
  - https://cragop.org/2026/03/21/california-republican-assembly-june-2026-primary-election-endorsements/
- Retrieved: 2026-06-01

CRA (California Republican Assembly) is the statewide volunteer Republican
grassroots organization. It is separate from the California Republican
Party (CAGOP), and its endorsements are independent.

## Coverage

- 7 statewide constitutional offices
- 2 State Board of Equalization seats
- 28 US House district endorsements
- 16 State Senate district endorsements
- 37 State Assembly district endorsements
- 3 LA County races

## Detailed local races skipped

CRA additionally endorsed in numerous county/city/school district races
across El Dorado, Fresno, Merced, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San
Diego, and Ventura counties (board of supervisors, city council, school
board, county officer races). Those are NOT in this slate yet — augment
