# NOTES — Republican Jewish Coalition PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

The RJC is national. This bundle holds only its California picks for the
November 3, 2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://www.rjcpac.com/ (RJC PAC Portal, "Back the candidates who stand
  with us"), pages 1–4. 156 candidate cards. Page 1 (default "Featured"
  order) holds the 50 cards with the red "Endorsed" bar
  (`rjc-card-endorsed-bar`); they include 2026 nominees such as John
  Sununu (NH-Sen) and Darline Graham (SC-Sen). Pages 2–4 hold cards
  without the bar.
- https://www.rjcpac.com/candidate/david-valadao/: "CA-22·House·CA·Incumbent",
  "Endorsed".
- https://www.rjchq.org/rjc_pac_releases_new_endorsements_2026 (September 23,
  2026, 20 new endorsements): no California candidates.

## Extracted: 1 position

- US House CD 22: David Valadao (vs. Randy Villegas in November).

## Skipped and why

- Six other California Republicans have portal cards but no "Endorsed"
  bar: Ken Calvert (card still says "CA-41"; he runs in CD 40 in 2026),
  Young Kim (CA-40), Vince Fong (CA-20), Jay Obernolte (CA-23), Jim Desmond
  (CA-48) and Jenny Rae LeRoux (CA-47). The portal is a giving page. A
  card without the "Endorsed" bar is not an explicit endorsement, so they
  are not recorded. Reviewer: if RJC confirms these as endorsements, add
  them (note Calvert and Kim face each other in CD 40).
- Search results for RJC releases naming Young Kim, Scott Baugh (CA-47) and
  Michelle Steel are from the 2020–2024 cycles.

  uploaded in 2022, so a reviewer may want to confirm it with an RJC
  release.
- Ballot name: "Rep. David Valadao" recorded as "David Valadao".
