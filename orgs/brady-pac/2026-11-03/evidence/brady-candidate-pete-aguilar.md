Pete Aguilar
Rep. Pete Aguilar knows how gun violence has devastated communities in California.
In 2015, a terrorist attack, a mile from his district office, left 14 people dead and 22 wounded. This tragedy deepened his commitment to ending the gun violence epidemic, prompting him to become an original member of the Mayors Against Illegal Guns Coalition.
Rep. Aguilar said:
We urge House Republicans to join us to protect our kids and their futures, and to come to the table to find a meaningful path forward to end gun violence once and for all.
As Chair of the House Democratic Caucus, and a member of the House Gun Violence Prevention Task Force, Rep. He continues to champion the law’s legacy and push for further reforms, insisting that keeping communities safe from gun violence should never be a partisan issue.
The Brady Campaign is proud to endorse Rep. Aguilar in his campaign to represent California’s 33rd Congressional District in the U.S. House of Representatives.
