 Senator Catherine Blakespear
 Position: California 38th Senate District
 Senator Catherine Blakespear represents California’s 38th Senate District, serving coastal communities across northern San Diego and southern Orange Counties. She is Chair of the Senate Environmental Quality Committee, the first woman ever to hold the position. She carried it through both houses with a coalition including California Environmental Voters, the Natural Resources Defense Council, and Earthjustice. She also helped drive the Legislature’s response to the Tijuana River Valley pollution crisis, chairing a joint Senate and Assembly hearing and writing SB 1046 to protect lifeguards, park rangers, and other workers exposed to toxic cross-border pollution. Both bills await the Governor’s signature.
 Sierra Club California has endorsed her re-election, and the Sierra Club Angeles Chapter honored her with its 2026 Political Leadership Award.
 Before the Senate, she served six years as Mayor of Encinitas, where she adopted an ambitious Climate Action Plan and moved the city to 100 percent renewable power.
