# NOTES — San Mateo County Democratic Party (SMCDems), Nov 3 2026 general

  November 2026 Endorsements": the party "met on September 3rd Endorsement
  Meeting" and "SMCDems endorsed these candidates and took positions on these
  local ballot measures." The list itself is only an embedded PNG graphic.
- The PNG (https://assets.nationbuilder.com/smcdcc/pages/116/attachments/original/1788916751/unnamed.png?1788916751),
  read and transcribed by hand. Hence `attribution.mode: transcribed`.

  (no endorsements), so it is not cited.

  ACRE roster of candidates (https://smcacre.gov/system/files/2026-09/52_candidateroster0903.pdf,
  printed 9/3/2026) and the measure list on
  https://smcacre.gov/elections/november-3-2026-statewide-general-election.

## Extracted: 72 positions

- 43 candidates: Board of Supervisors D5 (short term) 1; city council 26
  (Daly City 3, East Palo Alto 2, Foster City 2, Half Moon Bay 3, Menlo Park
  3, Pacifica 2, Redwood City 1, San Bruno 2, San Carlos 3, San Mateo 3,
  South San Francisco 2); school/college boards 16.
- 29 local measures (every measure on the San Mateo County ballot):
  - 26 "Support" → `endorse` Yes (includes Regional Measure RTM, reusing the
    existing `Bay Area — Regional Measure RTM (Regional Transit Measure)` subject).
  - 2 "Oppose" → `endorse` No: CC (East Palo Alto term limits), P (Menlo
    Park citizen initiative).
  - 1 "Neutral" → `neutral`: E (Redwood City rent control).
- No state, federal, legislative or proposition picks appear in the graphic
  (the county party relies on the California Democratic Party for those).

## Judgment calls for review

- **Pacifica council, Christine Boles**: the graphic says "Pacifica, District
  1", but the ACRE roster has no Pacifica District 1 contest in 2026; Boles is
  the candidate in "City of Pacifica Member, City Council, District 2". Recorded
  under `Pacifica City Council, District 2`. Drop it if you'd rather not
  correct the org's label.
- `choice` uses ACRE ballot names where the graphic shortens them: "Michael
  Mashhack" (graphic) → "Michael Mashack" (ballot); "Phoebe Venkat" → "Phoebe
  Shin Venkat"; "Steven Booker" → "Steven R. Booker"; "Lisa Nash" → "Lisa Diaz
  Nash"; "Aysha Pamukcu" → "Aysha Dominguez Pamukcu"; "Nnenna Okezie-Sanchez" →
  "Nnenna C. Okezie-Sanchez". The statements keep the graphic's spelling.
- School-row labels mapped to ACRE contests: "Millbrae" → Millbrae Elementary
  School District; "Pacifica" → Pacifica School District (full term); "San
  Carlos" → San Carlos School District; "Sequoia High School, Area A" →
  Sequoia Union High School District Trustee Area A; "San Mateo Community
  College, District 2" → SMCCD Trustee Area 2; "South San Francisco, District
  C" → SSFUSD Trustee Area C; "Jefferson Elementary" → the full-term contest
  (all three endorsees are on the full-term ballot).
- All endorsees were confirmed as qualified candidates on the ACRE roster;
  none were dropped.
