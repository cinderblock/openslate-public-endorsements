Our Revolution is proud to endorse Jane Kim for California Insurance Commissioner — a proven progressive leader who has spent her career fighting for working families and taking on corporate power.

As climate-driven disasters intensify and insurance companies seek to raise rates or withdraw coverage, families are being squeezed from all sides. We need a watchdog who will hold insurance corporations accountable, crack down on price gouging, and ensure that communities — not corporate profits — come first.
Jane Kim has the experience, the courage, and the progressive values to do exactly that.
California deserves an Insurance Commissioner who answers to the people. That’s why Our Revolution is proud to stand with Jane Kim.
