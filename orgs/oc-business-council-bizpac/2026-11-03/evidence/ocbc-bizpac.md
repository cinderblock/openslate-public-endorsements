OCBC BIZPAC is a sponsored political action committee affiliated with Orange County Business Council and governed by a separate Board of Trustees. The BIZPAC Board independently evaluates candidates and determines endorsements and political contributions, with a focus on supporting leaders who understand the needs of Orange County’s business community and the importance of a strong, competitive regional economy.

BIZPAC’s activities and decisions are separate from OCBC’s Board of Directors and are conducted in accordance with applicable campaign finance laws and regulations.

For questions about the PAC or endorsement process, please contact [mailto:awalsh@ocbc.org] Amanda Walsh.

Endorsed Candidates

Federal Endorsements

OCBC BIZPAC evaluates candidates through the lens of Orange County’s business community and the policies that support a strong regional economy. Key priorities include economic competitiveness, infrastructure, housing, workforce development and creating an environment where employers can invest, grow and create jobs.

Young Kim

United States House of Representatives, 40th District

Congresswoman Young Kim has helped secure significant federal investment in Orange County’s transportation, water and workforce infrastructure. Her work has supported improvements to I-5 and SR-55, water reliability projects and career training infrastructure that prepares Orange County students for jobs in high-demand industries.

Derek Tran

United States House of Representatives, 45th District

Congressman Derek Tran has worked to bring federal investment back to Orange County, advancing nearly $11 million for local infrastructure, housing and public safety projects in 2026. His work has included funding for the Orange County Housing Finance Trust as well as infrastructure improvements designed to support local businesses and economic activity in communities including Fullerton and Placentia.

State Endorsements

California’s economic future depends on policies that make it possible to build housing and infrastructure, develop a skilled workforce, attract investment and keep employers and jobs in the state. OCBC BIZPAC considers these priorities when evaluating candidates for statewide and legislative office.

Laurie Davies

California State Assembly, 74th District

Assemblymember Laurie Davies brings a practical approach to issues affecting Orange and San Diego counties, including transportation, infrastructure, housing and workforce development. Her legislative work and leadership roles on the Assembly Transportation and Utilities and Energy committees give her an important voice on issues directly affecting Southern California’s economy.

Kate Sanchez

California State Assembly, 71st District

Assemblymember Kate Sanchez has brought a focus on affordability, public safety and fiscal accountability to Sacramento. Her service as Vice Chair of the Assembly Revenue and Taxation Committee gives her a significant role in discussions about California’s tax climate and the costs facing residents and employers.

David Penaloza

California State Assembly, 68th District

David Penaloza brings experience as both a Santa Ana City Councilmember and businessman. His stated priorities include lowering costs, expanding housing and creating economic opportunity, bringing a local government and business perspective to the challenges facing Orange County communities.

Tony Strickland

California State Assembly, 36th District

Senator Tony Strickland has consistently focused on affordability, government spending and reducing costs for Californians. His work in Sacramento brings attention to the impact that taxes, energy costs and the overall cost of doing business have on families, employers and California’s economic competitiveness.

Avelino Valencia

California State Assembly, 34th District

Assemblymember Avelino Valencia represents communities at the heart of Orange County’s economy and is seeking to represent the 34th Senate District. His experience in local government and the State Assembly provides an understanding of how decisions in Sacramento affect Orange County communities, employers and economic opportunity.

Local Endorsements

Many of the decisions with the greatest impact on Orange County businesses are made at the local level. Land use, housing, infrastructure, permitting, public safety, education and fiscal policy all contribute to whether communities can attract investment and create economic opportunity.

Chip Ahlswede

Orange Unified School District Board of Education, 5th Trustee Area

Chip Ahlswede brings decades of experience in business, housing and public affairs to his candidacy for the Orange Unified School District Board of Education. His professional work with Orange County’s multifamily housing industry and his focus on fiscal accountability provide a business perspective on preparing students and responsibly managing public resources.

Valerie Amezcua

Mayor of Santa Ana

Mayor Valerie Amezcua has made economic development, infrastructure and public safety central priorities in Santa Ana. During her tenure, the city experienced significant development activity and established a Same Day Express Permit Program designed to streamline improvements for residents and small businesses.

Michael Buley

Mayor of Costa Mesa

Michael Buley understands the challenges facing local businesses and the importance of creating an environment that encourages investment and economic growth. His commitment to responsible governance, fiscal sustainability and strengthening Costa Mesa's business climate aligns with BIZPAC's vision for a prosperous Orange County.

Mayor of San Clemente
