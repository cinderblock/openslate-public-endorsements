# NOTES — Stanislaus County Democratic Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://standems.org/endorsed-candidates (HTML, Squarespace, scraped).
  "2026 SCDCC Endorsements", sections Federal & State, Local by city, County
  & Regional. The page doesn't name the election, but the races and measures
  (K, O, P) are all on the Nov 3 2026 ballot.
- Ballot names, contests and measure titles: Stanislaus County Registrar of
  Voters "Unofficial Candidate List", General Election November 3, 2026
  (https://www.stanvote.gov/pdf/candidate-list.pdf, dated 8/31/2026,
  downloaded with Windows curl; it includes the local measure ballot
  questions).

## Extracted: 27 positions

- Federal/state: CD 5, 9, 13; SD 4; AD 9 (5).
- Local candidates: 14 `endorse`.
- **5 `oppose`**: "Do Not Support" lines for Turlock Mayor (Amy Bublak and
  Kelley Coelho, recorded separately), Turlock council D1 (Jeremy Rocha),
  D3 (Ryan Taylor), and Turlock USD Area 4 (Jayden Betbabanta).
- Measures: Ceres P Yes, Modesto City Schools K Yes, Waterford O Yes.
