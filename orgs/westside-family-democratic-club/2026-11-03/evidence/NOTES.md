## Judgment calls / review flags

- **Public Defender is recorded as `abstain`, not `oppose`.** The heading is
  "No Endorsement", but the text adds "Raju does not deserve our
  endorsement, or your vote." A reviewer could read that as an explicit
  oppose of Mano Raju, who is unopposed. I kept the stated label.
- **BART D8.** The heading is just "BART Board, District 8". The pick comes
  from the body text: "Sara Barz, this club’s president, is running
  unopposed, and we couldn’t be more excited to see her in this position."
  It is recorded as `endorse` because it sits in the endorsement list.
- The page has no statewide offices or state propositions, and no
  supervisor picks other than D4.
