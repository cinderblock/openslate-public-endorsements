# NOTES — Silicon Valley Young Democrats, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.svyd.org/2026-general-endorsements (HTML, scraped): "The
  Silicon Valley Young Democrats are proud to endorse a diverse and dedicated
  group of candidates for the November 2026 General Election." Endorsements
  are graphics; the HTML carries a caption for each (quoted as the
  `statement`).
- Ballot check: SOS certified list, Santa Clara County ROV qualified local
  candidates list and List of Local Measures.

## Extracted: 6 positions

- Insurance Commissioner Jane Kim; AD 26 Patrick Ahrens; San Jose council D5
  Peter Ortiz and D9 Gordon Chester; San Jose USD TA 2 Tomara Hall;
  Berryessa Union SD Jaria Jaug.

## Skipped (and why)

- Sylvia Arenas, Board of Supervisors D1: decided in June (Arenas 63.7% vs.
  Munson in a two-candidate race; no November runoff). Not on the Nov ballot.
- Alexander Gvatua, Foothill-De Anza CCD TA 2, and Anthony Montes, East Side
  UHSD TA 2: the ROV marks both contests "On Ballot: No" (uncontested,
  appointed in lieu of election).
- "Santa Clara County Measure D": the caption gives no stance and the
  November Measure D is the City of Los Altos parking-plaza initiative, not a
  county measure. The June 2 ballot had a Santa Clara Valley Open Space
  Authority Measure D (parcel tax), so this graphic is probably left over
  from the primary. Omitted as ambiguous.

## Flags for review

- The page is a gallery of images whose captions name only candidate and
  office. The images themselves weren't read; the captions are taken as the
  endorsement text since the page heading says these are the endorsed
  candidates.
