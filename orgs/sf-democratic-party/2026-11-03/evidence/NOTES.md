("November 3rd, 2026 General Election Voter Guide", by Bobak Esfandiari),

- Regional Measure RTM: Yes.
- 10 SF propositions: Yes on A–F, H and J; No on G and I.

The page lists names and Yes/No only, with no reasons, so no `statement`
quotes are included. Every endorsement is stated as a plain endorsement or
"Yes on"/"No on", so all are `endorse`. Measure stances follow the June
convention: `endorse` plus a `choice` of Yes or No.

## Ballot verification (top-two)

- Statewide and district candidates were checked against the SOS Certified
  List of Candidates for the Nov 3, 2026 General Election (dated 8/27/2026),
  https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf,
  fetched 2026-10-15. Every endorsee is a certified finalist.
- SF local candidates were checked against the SF Department of Elections
  qualified list, https://www.sf.gov/candidates-november-3-2026-general-election,
  fetched 2026-10-15. Every endorsee is qualified.
- SF proposition titles were checked against the official qualified-measures
  page, https://www.sf.gov/reports/november-2024/qualified-measures. Despite
  the 2024 in its URL, the page covers Nov 3, 2026.

No June endorsee was dropped as a non-finalist. This guide is a fresh slate
for the general.

## Judgment calls

- Name normalization for cross-org collation: the guide's "Joaquín Torres"
  is recorded as "Joaquin Torres", the ballot spelling, with the original
  noted in `statement`. "Mano Raju" is kept as written; the ballot name is
  Manohar Raju.
- The "Board of Equalization, Seat 2" row maps to the existing title
  "California State Board of Equalization, District 2".
- "Community College Board, Partial Term" maps to the title
  "San Francisco Community College Board, Partial Term". The Dept. of
  Elections calls this contest "Community College Board Partial Term".
- RTM uses the title "Bay Area — Regional Measure RTM (Regional Transit
  Measure)" with jurisdiction `us/ca/bay-area`, the same as the other SF
  bundles. It is a five-county measure: SF, Alameda, Contra Costa, San Mateo
  and Santa Clara.
- The June bundle's "Pass-through of California Democratic Party
  endorsement" statements are not carried over, because the Nov page does
  not say that.
- Worth a reviewer's look: the SF Dems recommend No on Prop 40 (one-time
  tax) and Yes on SF Props D, E and F. Several other progressive SF groups
  take the opposite side on these. The page is unambiguous, so they are
  recorded as published.
