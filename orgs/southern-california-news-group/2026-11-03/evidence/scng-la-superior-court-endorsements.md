Of all of the contests on the November election ballot, perhaps the most vexing are the contested races for Los Angeles County Superior Court.

We’ve got you covered.

Over the course of the year, our editorial sent surveys to all judicial candidates in contested races. All candidates on the November ballot were then interviewed at least once by members of the editorial board and Opinion team.

Through our endorsement process, we sought to understand the backgrounds, thought processes and temperaments of the candidates as best we could given the nature of the office. Judges, after all, are held to different standards than run-of-the-mill politicians and are actually expected to uphold the rule of law, conduct themselves professionally and be as faithful to the facts as they can.

Here, we present our published endorsements to date in an easy-to-access list.

In some races, members of the Opinion team wrote separate commentaries about the races elaborating on their thoughts about the candidates.

Office No. 64

Endorsement: Maria Lisa Ghobadi – Deputy District Attorney, County of Los Angeles [/2026/05/17/endorsement-maria-ghobadi-for-superior-court-office-no-64/]

In the contested race for Los Angeles County Superior Court Office No. 64, long-time prosecutor Maria Ghobadi stands out as the clear and competent choice among the three candidates. Rated “Well Qualified” by the Los Angeles County Bar Association, Ghobadi brings nearly two decades of experience with the L.A. County District Attorney’s Office alongside a personal background as an immigrant from Iran that fuels her dedication to fairness, representation, and the rule of law. Her broad cross-party and organizational endorsements reflect her judicial temperament, measured approach to case management, and commitment to preventing unnecessary court delays. In contrast, her opponent presents significant concerns, public defender Rhonda Haymon, rated “Qualified,” has faced criticism from judicial peers regarding her temperament and professionalism.

Column by columnist Rafael Perez: Maria Ghobadi is the responsible choice for judicial office No. 64 [/2026/05/14/rafael-perez-maria-ghobadi-is-the-responsible-choice-for-judicial-office-no-64/]

Office No. 65

Endorsement: Anna Slotky Reitano – Deputy County Counsel, Justice and Safety Division, County of Los Angeles [/2026/10/01/endorsement-anna-slotky-reitano-for-la-superior-court-office-no-65/]

The race for Los Angeles County Superior Court Office No. 65 features two strong candidates who made it through the primary: Justin Clayton and Anna Slotky Reitano. Both candidates bring public defender backgrounds, earn “Qualified” ratings from the Los Angeles County Bar Association, and hold progressive endorsements. However, the endorsement goes to Reitano due to her broader, system-wide experience spanning both criminal defense as a former public defender and government/administrative law as a current deputy county counsel representing law enforcement.

Column by editorial board member Larry Wilson during the primary: Four fine candidates for L.A. Superior Court Office No. 65 [/2026/05/20/larry-wilson-four-fine-candidates-for-l-a-superior-court-office-no-65/]

Column by columnist Rafael Perez: Clayton vs. Reitano in race for LA County Superior Court Office No. 65 [/2026/09/07/clayton-vs-reitano-in-race-for-la-county-superior-court-office-no-65/]

Office No. 87

Endorsement: Anthony (A.J.) Bayne – Deputy Public Defender, Los Angeles County [/2026/05/28/endorsement-anthony-bayne-for-superior-court-office-no-87/]

In the race for Los Angeles County Superior Court Office No. 87, voters face a choice between two strong candidates who advanced past the primary: David DeJute and Anthony Bayne. DeJute, rated “Qualified” by the Los Angeles County Bar Association, brings a varied legal career as a former Assistant U.S. Attorney and current Pepperdine law professor directing a disaster relief clinic. However, the endorsement goes to long-time deputy public defender Anthony Bayne, who earned the higher “Well Qualified” rating from the Bar Association. With a quarter-century of trial experience, backing from both law enforcement groups and progressive criminal justice organizations, and a reputation as an independent thinker, Bayne offers the deep courtroom experience, efficiency, and balanced judicial philosophy needed to serve effectively on the bench.

Office No. 131

Endorsement: Donna Tryfman – Deputy Public Defender, County of Los Angeles [/2026/05/19/endorsement-donna-tryfman-for-superior-court-office-no-131/]

In the race for Los Angeles County Superior Court Office No. 131, voters are choosing between two long-serving public defenders: David Ross and Donna Tryfman. Both candidates earned “Qualified” ratings from the Los Angeles County Bar Association and demonstrate clear competence, but the endorsement ultimately goes to Tryfman. With three decades of legal experience—including work in the Major Cases Unit, serving as a judge pro tem, and leading local advocacy efforts—Tryfman offers a thoughtful, highly experienced, and balanced perspective on the separation between advocacy and judicial neutrality. In contrast, while Ross brings over two decades as an alternate public defender and a level-headed demeanor, his backing from far-left activist groups raises concerns.
