## Judgment calls / review flags

- **Ferguson is filed under the partial term.** The consolidated page has two
  headings both reading "SF Community College Board – full term", the second
  over "RUTH FERGUSON". That looks like a typo: Ferguson is the only
  partial-term candidate, and the Sept 15 post says "partial term – Ruth
  Ferguson". sfendorsements.com copies the typo and puts her in the
  full-term column.
- **Judicial retention.** The club lists the retention contests as two group
  lines, not by name. They were expanded to the 2 Supreme Court and 11
  First District Court of Appeal retention titles already in the catalog.
  The page calls all eleven "Associate Justices", but Stewart and Brown are
  on the ballot as Presiding Justices. Both are still covered, since the
  count of eleven matches.
- "YES!!" (unanimous) and "YES." are both recorded as `endorse`.
- There are no picks for AD-19, CD-15 or Supervisor D2/D4/D6/D8. The club is
  in AD-17 and D10, and it published nothing for those races.
