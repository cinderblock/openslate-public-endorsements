# NOTES — Inland Empire United, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://ieunited.org/2026-endorsements/ ("OUR 2026 ENDORSEMENTS", HTML,
  scraped). The page is not dated, but its footer points to "the upcoming
  November 3 Election", and the contested picks checked below are all in
  November contests.
- "IE Votes" (named in some Blue Voter Guide listings) has no site of its
  own: ievotes.org does not resolve. It appears to be IE United's voter
  program, so no separate bundle.
- `context.jurisdiction` is `us/ca/riverside-county`. The group covers
  Riverside and San Bernardino counties; Riverside was picked as primary.
  San Bernardino County picks use San Bernardino jurisdictions.

## Extracted: 33 positions

- Legislature: 6 (SD 32; AD 34, 47, 58, 60, 71).

  councils, school boards, Desert Healthcare District).

  Wards 1/2/4, Redlands, Victorville, school boards).
