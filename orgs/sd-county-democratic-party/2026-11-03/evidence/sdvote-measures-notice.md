NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that an election will be held on November 3, 2026, at which there will be submitted
to the qualified electors of each district the following measures:

MEASURE – COUNTY OF SAN DIEGO
Shall the San Diego County Charter be amended to establish an Independent Ethics Commission, Independent
Budget Analyst, Independent Program Auditor, confirmation of certain senior County leaders, and optional Board
of Supervisors appointment of the Public Defender; increase term limits from two to three terms for the Board of
Supervisors members; require public disclosure of spending and performance data; clarify non-interference
provisions; and update terminology and structure?

MEASURE TO FUND SAN DIEGO COUNTY HEALTH & SAFETY SERVICES
To clean-up sewage pollution in Tijuana River Valley, improve wildfire prevention, emergency response, access to
healthcare, food assistance, childhood development services, and authorized administrative expenses; shall the
measure authorizing a half-cent sales tax raising an estimated $400,000,000 to $450,000,000 annually, requiring
citizen oversight, public spending disclosure, and independent annual audits, and lasting until ended by voters, be
adopted?

MEASURE – FALLBROOK UNION ELEMENTARY SCHOOL DISTRICT

To update local elementary and middle schools without increasing tax rates; replace failing roofs, plumbing and

electrical systems; improve school safety; construct/repair/acquire classrooms, labs, facilities, equipment, and

sites; and qualify for State matching funds; shall Fallbrook Union Elementary School District’s measure be

adopted, authorizing $76,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying a projected $30 per $100,000 assessed value

while bonds are outstanding, generating $5,000,000 annually, with public oversight, audits, no funds for

administrators, all funds staying local?

Bonds – Yes                                       Bonds – No

Grossmont-Cuyamaca Community College District Fire Safety, Job Training, College Affordability, No Tax

Rate Increase

Cuyamaca Community College District’s measure to

• improve/expand career training classrooms

• remove asbestos/mold

• reduce wildfire threats

• maintain college affordability

be adopted, authorizing $624 million of bonds with legal rates, levies below $25/$100,000 assessed valuation

while outstanding ($45 million annually), audits, taxpayer oversight, full disclosure of all spending?

support student achievement in math, science, technology, reading, writing, shall Jamul-Dulzura Union School

District’s measure be adopted authorizing $18,000,000 in bonds at legal rates, levying $30 per $100,000

MEASURE - RAMONA MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT
AMENDMENT OF PROPOSITION ‘NN’ TO MODERNIZE THE ORDINANCE.
Shall the voters of the Ramona Municipal Water District adopt a measure amending Proposition ‘NN’ by (1)
exempting grant funding and donations from the calculation of authorized expenditure limit; (2) increasing the
expenditure limit for new projects from $1,000,000 to $10,000,000 where no debt is incurred; and (3) establishing
annual adjustments to the expenditure limit based on the San Diego Area Consumer Price Index beginning
January 1, 2028?

MEASURE - PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION NO. 1000, ZONE NO. 9B – HARRIS ANNEX ("PRD Zone")
Shall the County of San Diego, beginning Fiscal Year 2027-28 and annually thereafter, levy a special tax of
up to $1,000 per developed / $375 per undeveloped parcel within Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone
No. 9B – Harris Annex, replacing the existing assessment? The tax may be adjusted by up to 5% annually
based on the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a comparable Southern California index and remain
until modified or dissolved by law.

MEASURE - PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION NO. 1000, ZONE NO. 13A – PALA MESA ZONE A ("PRD Zone")
Shall the County of San Diego, beginning Fiscal Year 2027-28 and annually thereafter, levy a special tax of up to
$1,786.00 per developed / $893.00 per undeveloped parcel within Permanent Road Division No. 1000, Zone No.
13A – Pala Mesa Zone A, replacing the existing assessment? The tax may be adjusted by up to 5% annually
based on the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a comparable Southern California index and remain until
modified or dissolved by law.
