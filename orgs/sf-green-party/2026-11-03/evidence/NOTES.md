## Review flags

- **The four `abstain` entries.** "No consensus for or against" is an
  explicit statement that the party takes no position, so it is recorded
  as `abstain`, the same as G's "no endorsement". Drop them if you only
  want affirmative picks.
- **Names.** "Jeremy Greco" and "Michael Nguyen" match other bundles (ballot:
  "Jeremy Julian Greco", "Michael T. Nguyen"). D10 uses the ballot name
  `Pearci "PJ" Bastiany III`.
- No statewide candidates, Assembly or BART picks are on the page.

All candidates are on the SF Department of Elections qualified list
(https://www.sf.gov/candidates-november-3-2026-general-election).
