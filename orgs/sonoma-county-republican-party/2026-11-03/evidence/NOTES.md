# NOTES — Sonoma County Republican Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.sonomacountygop.org/ : the home page heading "The Sonoma
  County Republican Party November 2026 Voter Guide" sits above two images:
  - Candidate guide: https://www.sonomacountygop.org/s/cc_images/teaserbox_908396352.PNG?t=1791321085
  - Proposition and measures guide: https://www.sonomacountygop.org/s/cc_images/teaserbox_908396353.PNG?t=1791321072
- Both images were downloaded and transcribed (mode `transcribed`).
- Spelling was checked against the SOS certified list. The Sonoma ROV measure
  list (8/7/26) gave the measure letters, and existing subject titles were reused.

## Extracted: 35 positions

- Candidates (11): CD 1 Gallagher, CD 2 Littau, SD 2 Gibbs, AD 2 Greer,
  Governor Hilton, Lt. Gov. Romero, Controller Morgan, AG Gates, Treasurer
  Hawks, SoS Wagner, Superintendent Shaw.
- Props (14): No on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 37, 38, 40, 44, 45; Yes on 39, 41, 42, 43.
- Local measures (10), all No: D, E, G, H, AB, I, K, L, M, O.

## Skipped

- Measure J (Windsor Unified School District Bond) and Measure N (Schell-Vista
  Fire Protection District) are marked "N/A". That is not an explicit
  "neutral" or "no position", so they are omitted.
- The guide names no Insurance Commissioner or BOE District 1 pick (both
  general-election finalists in Sonoma are Democrats for Insurance
  Commissioner).

## Flags for review

- AD 2: the guide says "Mike Greer"; the SOS ballot name is "Michael Greer",
  which is used as `choice`.
- Positions cite the image URLs as `source`. The `?t=` cache-buster may change
  if the party re-uploads the images; the home page is also listed in
  `attribution.sources`.
