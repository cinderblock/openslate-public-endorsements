Endorsements
These endorsements reflect the Party’s commitment to progressive values, including protecting working families, expanding affordable housing, investing in public education, safeguarding our environment, and ensuring that local government serves the needs of all Monterey County residents. The recommendations, brought forward by the MCDCC Endorsement Committee after candidate interviews and public hearings on August 19th and 24th, were approved by the Full Committee.
Protecting Renters and Housing Stability
A key priority of the endorsed measures is the defense of tenant protections. The Committee voted to recommend a NO vote on Measure H in Salinas, which seeks to repeal the City’s Residential Rental Registration, Rent Stabilization, Tenant Protection, and Anti-Harassment Ordinances.
