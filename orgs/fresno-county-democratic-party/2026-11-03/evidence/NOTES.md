# NOTES — Fresno County Democratic Party, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://fresnocountydemocrats.org/en_US/democratic-party-endorsements/,
  a post titled "Fresno County Democratic Party Endorsements for November 3,
  2026" (published September 28, 2026). The post body is two images with no
  HTML text. Both were downloaded at full size and transcribed by hand, so
  `attribution.mode` is `transcribed`. See
  `fresnocountydemocrats-endorsements.md`.
  - "FRESNO COUNTY DEMOCRATIC PARTY / NOVEMBER 2026 ENDORSEMENTS": local picks.
  - "CADEM ... NOVEMBER 2026 ENDORSEMENTS": state, federal and proposition
    picks. These are the California Democratic Party's endorsements, but the
    county party publishes them as part of its own November slate, so they
    are included here.
- The old domain fresnodemocrats.org shows "This website is offline for
  maintenance."
- Ballot names and contest names were checked against the Fresno County
  Registrar's "Contests on Ballot with Qualified Candidates" report
  (https://www2.fresnocountyca.gov/2850/post/2026NOV/Contests%20on%20Ballot%20with%20Qualified%20Candidates.pdf)
  and the SOS certified list.

## Extracted: 45 positions

- Statewide: 8 offices + BOE District 1 (9).
- Federal: CD 5, 13, 18, 20, 21, 22 (6).
- Legislature: SD 14, SD 16, AD 27, AD 31 (4).
- Propositions: all 14.
- Local: 12 (Supervisor D1; Fresno council D1, D7; Clovis council D5;
  Parlier mayor; County Superintendent of Schools; Superior Court Office
  No. 7; SCCCD Area 1; Fresno USD Area 3; Central USD Areas 1 and 3; Mendota
  USD).

## Skipped

- Every "NO ENDORSEMENT" line (Fresno council D3; Fresno USD Areas 1, 4, 7;
  Central USD Areas 4, 7). These aren't stated neutral positions.
- **Not found on the county's November contest list**, so omitted pending
  review: "Fresno County Board of Education District 1 KIMBERLY
  TAPSCOTT-MUNSON", "Fresno County Board of Education District 4 MARCY
  MATSUMOTO", "State Center Comm College District, Area 4 MAGDALENA GOMEZ".
  The registrar's report lists only SCCCD Areas 1 and 5 and no County Board
  of Education contests. These may have been decided in June or be
  uncontested. Add them back if they turn out to be on the ballot.

## Review flags

- **Spelling fixes from the ballot list.** The graphic's misspellings were
  corrected in `choice`; the statements keep the graphic's text:
  "MICHELL CANTWELL-COPHER" → Michele Cantwell-Copher; "DESTINY RODRIQUEZ" →
  Destiny Rodriguez; "RILEY TALLFORD" → Riley Talford; "JOSH SELLERS" →
  Joshua Sellers; "ERICA PIEDRA" → Erica Gonzalez Piedra; "KEVIN
  ARIAS-ROMERO" → Kevin Gilbert Arias-Romero; "LIONEL LOPEZ" → Lionel Lopez,
  Jr.; "JOSE SALAZAR" → Jose L. Salazar.
- **Central USD Area 3** is the "Trustee Area No. 3 Short Term" contest on the
  county list; titled "Central Unified School District Board, Area 3 (Short
  Term)".
- **Superior Court.** The graphic says "Seat 7"; the county list says "Judge
  of the Superior Court, Office No. 7" (Hammerschmidt vs. Salazar). Title
  invented: "Fresno County Superior Court Judge, Office No. 7".
- **Top-two.** State and federal picks are all on the SOS certified general
  list. Supervisor D1 (Karbassi vs. Maria Pacheco), Fresno council D1 and D7,
  and the judge race are November runoffs on the county list.
- **New titles/jurisdictions:** `us/ca/fresno-county` for county, judicial
  and school contests; `us/ca/fresno`, `us/ca/clovis`, `us/ca/parlier` for
  city contests.
