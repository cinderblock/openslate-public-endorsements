Club for Growth PAC Endorses Rep. Tom McClintock in CA-05 Re-Election Campaign 
 Will Mitola  |  
 February 19, 2026
Washington, D.C. – Club for Growth PAC endorsed Rep. Tom McClintock (CfGF Lifetime Rating: 95%) in California’s 5th Congressional District. McClintock, a 13-time winner of Club for Growth’s Defender of Economic Freedom Award, is running for re-election in the newly redrawn 5th District.
“Rep. Tom McClintock is a proven conservative champion who has fought tirelessly for limited government policies on Capitol Hill,” said Club for Growth PAC President David McIntosh. “Rep. McClintock is the only candidate in the race who has consistently voted to cut spending and lower taxes. Club for Growth PAC is proud to endorse Rep. McClintock for reelection, and we look forward to his leadership on Capitol Hill for years to come.”
Club for Growth PAC is affiliated with Club for Growth Action, the nation’s largest conservative Super PAC. In 2024, Club for Growth PAC endorsed candidates won 79% of their elections.
