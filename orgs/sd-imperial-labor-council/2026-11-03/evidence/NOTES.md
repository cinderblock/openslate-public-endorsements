# NOTES: San Diego & Imperial Counties Labor Council, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources
- https://www.unionyes.org/2026-endorsements, the council's own page. It is the same URL as June and is now headed "November 3, 2026 General Election / UNION ENDORSEMENTS".
- The page links a 4-page PDF on SharePoint, which was not fetched. Every position cites the HTML.
- Ballot verification: the SD ROV candidate list; the SOS certified list for CD-25 and AD-36; and the Imperial County ROV "Candidate Status" PDF (updated 08/20/2026) for the Imperial local races.

## Omitted because the race is not on the Nov 3 ballot
- **Imperial Irrigation District Div 1 (Alex Cardenas) and Div 5 (Karin Euginio):** no IID contests appear in Imperial's Nov 3 candidate-status document. Press reports of the June 2 primary say both incumbents won outright. These are probably carry-overs from the June page.
- **City of Brawley Council (Eric Reyes):** Imperial ROV marks it "NO ELECTION" (2 candidates for 2 seats).
- **SD ROV `Election = NO` or contest not listed:** MiraCosta Area 6 (Aszterbaum); Grossmont-Cuyamaca CCD Areas 1/2 (Page, Justeson; not on the ROV list); SDCCD District E (Arroyo; not on the list); Southwestern Area 5 (Dumas); Sweetwater UHSD Areas 3/5 (Lopez-Zepeda, Emerald); Vista USD Area 2 (Marrocco); "Sweetwater Authority" District 1 / Division 4 (Castaneda, Martinez), which the ROV lists as "SOUTH BAY WATER" Div 1/4 with `Election = NO`; Valle De Oro Community Planning (Jay Steiger).

## Judgment calls
- **Lemon Grove:** the page lists "City Council (At-Large, Vote for All): Krystle Johnson, Jennifer Mendoza, Joe Smith". On the ROV, Jennifer Mendoza runs in the separate short-term contest, so she is recorded under "Lemon Grove City Council (Short Term)".
- **Chula Vista Elementary:** the page's "Area 1/3/5" is ROV "Seat 1/3/5". "Michael Anthony Perez" is matched to the ROV's `PEREZ, MICHAEL "TONY"`, the only Perez in Seat 5. Reviewer may want to confirm.
- **Imperial County Supervisor D5:** this is a runoff (John Hawk vs. Patricia "Patty" Lizarraga). The page says "Patricia Lizarraga".
- **Imperial Community College "Seat 5 - Lizzy Cardenas":** recorded as Trustee Area 5 using the ballot spelling "Lizzie Cardenas".
- **Prop 38** is not listed on the page, so there is no position.
- Prop and measure `statement`s quote the council's one-line labels verbatim (e.g., "41 - NO - Kills Prop 40. ..."). Measure B's label says "Health and Social Services", but the title uses the ROV short title.
