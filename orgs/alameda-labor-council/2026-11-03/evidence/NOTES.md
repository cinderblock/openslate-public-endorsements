# NOTES — Alameda Labor Council, AFL-CIO, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- The council's "2026 General Election Endorsements" Google Doc:
  https://docs.google.com/document/d/17P9ht52TNggvTUvs5ZEtCeiWC6kNYrVF_uGE8MMCDvU/edit?tab=t.0
  ("Document Last Updated September 17, 2026 2:00 PM"). The council's
  homepage, https://alamedalabor.org/, links it from its slider. The text
  was fetched through the doc's public plain-text export.
- The doc's disclaimer says federal and statewide recommendations come from
  the statewide (California Labor Federation) convention process. Local
  picks are the council's own.
- Contest names and ballot names were checked against the Alameda County
  ROV candidate list and measure list for election 260.

## Extracted: 108 positions

- Statewide offices: 9.
- Congress: CD 10, 12, 14, 17.
- Legislature: SD 10; AD 16, 18, 20, 24.
- State propositions: 13 (Yes on 1–5, 37, 40, 44; No on 39, 41, 42, 43,
  45). There's no listing for Prop 38.
- Local races: 70.
- Local measures: 7 (L, U, V, Y, Z, CC, RTM — all Yes).

## Skipped

- "Assembly District 14: No Endorsement". This isn't an explicit
  neutral/no-position statement on the measure-style scale, so it's
  omitted.
- "Open Endorsement" entries. These are Oakland City Council District 2,
  Mayor of San Leandro, and Union City City Council District 4. No
  candidate is named, so they're omitted.
- Three races that aren't on the ballot. The ROV marks each "Not On Ballot"
  (a single candidate, appointed in lieu of election):
  - "Alameda Unified School Board (Short-term): Ryan LaLonde".
  - "Livermore City Council, District 1: Evan Branning".
  - "EBMUD, Ward 4: Andy Katz".

## Flags for review

- Dual endorsements are encoded as two `endorse` positions in the same
  race:
  - Berkeley D1, "Dual Endorsement | Rank Both Candidates": Daria Wrubel and
    Moni Gangopadhyay.
  - OUSD D4: Sylvia Williams and Kathryn Camp.
  - Pleasanton D3, "Choose 1": Jamie Yee and Reena Gupta.
  - Union City D3: Cheris Crocker-Root and Jeff Wang.
  - New Haven USD Area 5: Francis Rojas and Deepti Garg.

  For the "Choose 1" races, a reader should know the council backs either
  candidate.
- Spellings normalized to the ballot name:
  - "Xoahoa Bowen" [sic] → Xouhoa Bowen.
  - "Sylvia Williams" → Sylvia Pfeiffer Williams.
  - "Joel Young" → Joel B. Young.
  - "Robin Lopez" → Robin D. López.
- "Emeryville School Board: Regina Chagolla" maps to the Emery Unified
  School District board.
- "Eden Township Healthcare District, Zone 3" is "Area 3" on the ROV.
- Oro Loma "District 1/3/5" is "Oro Loma Sanitary Dist #1/#3/#5" on the
  ROV. It's titled "... Board of Directors, Area <n>" to match the other
  bundles.
- The Prop 1 label is the council's own ("The Veterans and Affordable
  Housing Bond Act"). It maps to Prop 1, the housing bond.
