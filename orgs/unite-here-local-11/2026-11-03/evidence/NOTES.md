## Skipped

- **Whittier City School District, Trustee Area 5: Andrew Roble.** He is
  the only candidate on the LA County list. Uncontested school board seats
  are normally appointed in lieu of election, and no existing bundle has
  this contest, so it may not be on the ballot. Omitted.

## Flags for review

- **Assembly District 63: Kevin Akin.** The SOS certified list has Natasha
  Johnson (R, incumbent) vs. Kevin Akin (Peace and Freedom). UH11 endorses
  Akin. That's correct per the source, but unusual.
- **Unverified OC ballot spellings.** No Orange County candidate list was
  available (ocvote.gov has no candidate list page for the general). The
  contests themselves are confirmed on OC's "Contests Qualified for the
  Ballot for the 2026 General Election" page. These names use the guide's
  spelling: Anaheim D3 "Elia Rentaría García" (possibly Rentería), Buena
  Park D2 "Yong Choi", Garden Grove USD TA 4 "Tracy La", and Irvine D1
  "Sukhee Kang".
- **LA County Measure E – NO.** The reason given ("Oppose the creation of
  unelected bureaucracies in local government") is word-for-word the same
  as for City Measures EE and SC. It may be a copy-paste in the guide, but
  the stance is stated, so it is kept.
- **Inglewood** Mayor and Council D1/D2 are a "PRIMARY NOMINATING
  ELECTION" on Nov 3 (LA County list), so no top-two filter applies.
- The Supreme Court retention line reads "Asociate Justice Joshua Groban"
  (typo in source, quoted as-is).
