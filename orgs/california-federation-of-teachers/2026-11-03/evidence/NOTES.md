# NOTES — California Federation of Teachers, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.cft.org/2026-midterm-elections-hub (HTML). "CFT
  Endorsements — Check out our endorsements for the 2026 midterm
  election." links the PDF below. The hub also lists CFT news releases on
  Props 3, 39, 40 and 43, which agree with the PDF.
- https://www.cft.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/cft_endorsements-general-election_9.29.2026.pdf
  (2-page PDF, "General Election 2026 Tuesday, November 3", footer dated
  10/12/2026). Transcribed with pdftotext. `mode` is `transcribed`.

## Extracted: 154 positions

- 14 propositions: YES on 1, 3, 4, 5, 37, 40; NO on 39, 41, 42, 43, 45;
  NEUTRAL (→ `neutral`) on 2, 38, 44.
- 8 statewide offices plus 4 BOE seats (9 + 5 positions because of two dual
  endorsements: Insurance Commissioner "Ben Allen | Jane Kim", BOE District
  3 "Mike Gipson | Sam Sukaton").
- 17 State Senate.
- 61 Assembly positions in 59 districts (duals in AD 12 Elward | Lucan and
  AD 68 Penaloza | Lopez).
- 48 US House positions in 47 districts (dual in CD 7 Vang | Matsui).
- Each dual is two `endorse` positions, and the statement notes "+ Dual
  Endorsement".

## Top-two check

All candidates are on the SOS Certified List for Nov 3 2026 (8/27/2026)
in the listed district. In each dual race both finalists are endorsed.
