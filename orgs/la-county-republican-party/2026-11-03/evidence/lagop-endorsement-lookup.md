## About this tool

The LAGOP Endorsement Lookup helps Los Angeles County voters find endorsed and recommended candidates, plus LAGOP's position on every state proposition, for the November 3, 2026 general election. Red Endorsed badges mark official Republican Party endorsements (LAGOP, or CAGOP for statewide offices). Amber Recommended badges mark races where LAGOP recommends a candidate without a formal endorsement.

-
1. Search your area

Type your home address or ZIP code and press Find My Recommendations.

-
2. See your picks

Your candidates appear first, in ballot order, followed by the state propositions.

-
3. Print or share

Print a one-page voter guide to take with you, or share the link with friends and family.
