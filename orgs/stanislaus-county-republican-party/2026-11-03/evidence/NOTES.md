  contest is STANISLAUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT - AREA 1, and Joel Campos is a
  candidate there, so the existing title `Stanislaus Union School District
  Board, Area 1` is used.
- Name normalisation to the ROV ballot names: "Geoffrey Powers" → "Geoff
  Powers", "Chris Chavez" → "Christopher Chavez", "Jeremy Hedden" → "Jeremy T.
  Hedden".
