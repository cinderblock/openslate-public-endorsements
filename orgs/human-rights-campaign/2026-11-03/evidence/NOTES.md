# NOTES — Human Rights Campaign PAC, Nov 3 2026 California general (California picks only)

HRC PAC is a national organization that endorses candidates in many
states. This bundle holds only its California picks for the November 3,
2026 general election.

## Sources

- https://www.hrc.org/lets-get-free-house-elections ("Let's Get Free: House
  Elections"). Featured candidates (incl. CA-22 Villegas, CA-11 Wiener,
  CA-48 von Wilpert) with "Donate Now" links, then a list headed "Other
  Races in the 2026 Election" / "U.S. House of Representatives" giving
  "Name (D-State-District)". The list notes losers (e.g. "John Larson
  (defeated) (D-Connecticut-1)"), so it is HRC's running endorsement list.
- https://www.hrc.org/lets-get-free-state-elections: "HRC is supporting
  many candidates for Governor, Attorney General and other important
  offices." California entries: Governor (Becerra) and Superintendent of
  Public Instruction (Barrera).
- https://www.hrc.org/press-releases/human-rights-campaign-endorses-a-slate-of-pro-equality-members-and-candidates-up-for-election-in-key-competitive-u-s-house-districts
  (June 24, 2026): "announced its endorsement of more than 40 pro-equality
  candidates". California entries: CA-45 Tran, CA-33 Aguilar, CA-39 Robert
  Garcia, CA-42 Mark Takano.
- Not used: the October 2022 ballot-measures release (prior cycle); hrc.org
  has no 2026 California proposition positions that I could find.

## Extracted: 47 positions

- US House: 45 California districts (every district except CD 1, 5, 14,
  20, 23, 26 and 40).
- Statewide: Governor (Xavier Becerra), Superintendent of Public
  Instruction (Richard Barrera).

## Top-two check

Every choice matches a November finalist already recorded in existing
bundles for that district (CD 7 Matsui vs. Mai Vang and CD 11 Wiener vs.
Connie Chan are D-vs-D; HRC backs Matsui and Wiener).

## Review flags

- **Stance.** The "Other Races" list carries no per-name "endorse" verb;
  the page frames it as HRC's support list and the companion state page
  says "HRC is supporting". Recorded as `endorse`. CA-45 and CA-33 cite the
  press release, which says "endorsement" explicitly.
- **District mix-up in the press release.** The June 24 release says
  "CA-39: Robert Garcia" and "CA-42 Mark Takano". Under the current map
  Takano runs in CD 39 and Garcia in CD 42, which is also how the House
  page lists them ("Mark Takano (D-California-39)", "Robert Garcia
  (D-California-42)"). Positions use the House page.
- **Names normalized to ballot names:** "Sydney Kamlager" ->
  "Sydney Kamlager-Dove"; "Nanette Barragan" -> "Nanette Barragán".
