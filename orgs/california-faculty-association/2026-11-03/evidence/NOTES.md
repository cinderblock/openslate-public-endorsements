# NOTES — California Faculty Association, Nov 3 2026 general

## Sources

- https://www.calfac.org/politics/endorsements/ (HTML, scraped with curl).
  It has a section headed "2026 GENERAL ELECTION VOTER GUIDE" with
  "ENDORSED BALLOT MEASURES" (Support / Oppose) and "ENDORSED CANDIDATES"
  (Statewide, US Congress, California Senate, California Assembly). Each
  candidate card gives a name and an office.

## Extracted: 108 positions

- 6 propositions: Support → Yes on 2, 3, 4, 5. Oppose → No on 39, 43.
  CFA lists no position on the other 8 propositions; none recorded.
- 8 statewide: Governor Becerra, Lt. Governor Ma, AG Bonta, Controller
  Cohen, SoS Weber, SPI Barrera, Insurance Commissioner Jane Kim,
  Treasurer Kounalakis.
- 20 US House, 14 State Senate, 60 Assembly.

## Top-two check

Every candidate was checked against the SOS Certified List of Candidates
for Nov 3 2026 (8/27/2026,
https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf).
All are on the general ballot in the listed district.

  recorded.
