- 16 State Senate.
- 45 US House (43 candidates plus "No Recommendation" in CD 16 and 26).

## Stance choice

CTA calls these "recommendations" throughout. The election page says "CTA
recommendations for candidates and ballot measures are voted on by our
State Council of Education", and the word "endorse" does not appear. Per
the shared brief ("Recommended" → `lean_for`), candidate picks are
`lean_for`. Ballot measures say "Support"/"Oppose" explicitly, so they are
`endorse` Yes/No.

## Top-two check

Every named candidate was checked against the SOS Certified List of
Candidates for the Nov 3 2026 General Election (8/27/2026,
https://elections.cdn.sos.ca.gov/statewide-elections/2026-general/cert-list-candidates.pdf).
All are on the general ballot in the listed district.

## Review flags

- **Typo in source.** The PDF lists "AD 27 Brian Pachecho (D)". The ballot
  name is "Brian Pacheco" (AD 27: Brian Pacheco vs. Mike Murphy). The
  choice uses the ballot spelling and the statement keeps the original.
- **NEA approval.** The PDF footnote says "*Congressional candidate
  recommendations are not final until approved by the NEA".
- **October Council recommendations are members-only.**
  https://www.cta.org/document/october-council-recommendations returns "You
  must be logged in to access this document." It may hold more
  recommendations (e.g. the props not on the initiative list). Not used.
