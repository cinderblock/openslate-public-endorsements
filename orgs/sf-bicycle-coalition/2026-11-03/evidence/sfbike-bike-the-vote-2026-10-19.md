# San Francisco Bicycle Coalition Board of Directors Endorsements

The SF Bicycle Coalition has a 14-person board that is directly elected by our membership. If you would like to contact the board, you can email [email protected].

As a 501(c)(4), the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition makes political endorsements, helping to ensure San Francisco is a better place to live, work, and bike. In election years, we send candidate questionnaires to candidates running for office, helping to guide our voting slate. (The San Francisco Bicycle Coalition Education Fund is a distinct 501(c)(3) entity that operates most of our engagement and programmatic work.)

# June 2, 2026 Primary Election

### Candidates

In order to be effective with our endorsement, we have decided to focus on larger elections with strong candidates who truly embody the mission and values of the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition. Therefore, we are taking no position in most candidate races. This will allow us to better support the ballot measures that advance our organization’s mission while still building political power. Here are our endorsements for the upcoming election.

We asked each candidate running in this election to answer our candidate questionnaire. Read more about their positions, particularly on transportation and biking.
