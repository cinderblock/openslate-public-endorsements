# Vote No on Prop G: Save Sunset Dunes Park!

By SF Bicycle Coalition Board on September 2, 2026

Sunset Dunes is on the ballot again, and the Board of Directors of the San Francisco Bicycle Coalition encourages voters to Vote No on Prop G to save the park.

In 2024, we proudly endorsed Prop K, and SF voters decided to convert the previous Upper Great Highway into an oceanfront park. The measure passed with a majority of the vote, and the park opened as Sunset Dunes in April of 2025. Within just 6 months, Sunset Dunes amassed 1 million visits, averaging about 4900 visits daily, and just over 1 year later, Sunset Dunes is the third most popular park in the city. While the park has its opponents, this data shows that Sunset Dunes has mass appeal. Passing Prop K marked a step forward in our coalition’s mission to achieve a network of safe biking and rolling routes around the city.

Opponents of the park are yet again attempting to take away this popular park by bringing it back to the ballot as Prop G. If Prop G passes, it would remove the only protected, north-south bike route in the Sunset Districts. Only 3% of SF streets have separated bike lanes, and removing this 2 mile bike- and pedestrian-only route is a significant reduction in the city’s protected bike network.

Ultimately, Sunset Dunes park will cease to exist, and $10 million dollars of taxpayer money will have to be spent to remove it. If the park is removed and converted back to a road, the city will have to spend up to another $2 million annually to remove sand and maintain the road for cars.

It is crucial that we protect this beloved park, and vote against reverting what SF voters already decided. Vote No on Prop G.
