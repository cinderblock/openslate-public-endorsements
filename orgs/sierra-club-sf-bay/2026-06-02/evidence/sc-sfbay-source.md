# Evidence: Sierra Club SF Bay Chapter 2026 endorsements

- Source: https://www.sierraclub.org/sfbay/2026-endorsements
- Retrieved: 2026-06-01

The SF Bay Chapter endorses on Bay Area races and on the statewide races
that overlap with the Bay (California's top-two open primary means an
endorsement at the primary stage is what voters act on June 2).

For El Cerrito and Richmond city councils and Alameda County Flood Control
the chapter endorsed two candidates each — modeled as two separate
"endorse" positions on the same race (both candidates meet their
criteria).

Statewide endorsements overlap with [[Sierra Club California]] but with
one notable diff: this chapter ALSO endorses Insurance Commissioner Ben
Allen and the rest of the statewide constitutional consensus picks. The
chapter did NOT take an endorsement on Governor.
